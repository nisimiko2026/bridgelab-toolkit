# Polish Club System

## Overview

**Polish Club** is a **natural/strong club hybrid** bidding system developed in Poland and widely played throughout Europe. The system is characterized by a **wide-ranging 1♣ opening**, limited one-level openings in the majors, and highly descriptive relay sequences.

Unlike Precision, the **1♣ opening is not always strong**. It may contain weak natural club hands, balanced hands, or strong hands.

---

# System Summary

| Opening | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **1♣** | Artificial catch-all (12–14 balanced, natural clubs, or any 18+ hand) |
| **1♦** | Natural, 11–17 HCP, 4+ diamonds |
| **1♥** | Natural, 11–17 HCP, 5+ hearts |
| **1♠** | Natural, 11–17 HCP, 5+ spades |
| **1NT** | 15–17 HCP balanced |
| **2♣** | Natural, 5+ clubs, constructive |
| **2♦** | Multi 2♦ (most common) |
| **2♥ / 2♠** | Weak Two |
| **2NT** | 20–21 balanced |
| **3-level** | Preemptive |

# The 1♣ Opening

The hallmark of the Polish Club system.

Shows one of:

### Option 1

12–14 HCP

Balanced

---

### Option 2

Natural clubs

Usually:

- 5+ clubs
- 11–17 HCP

---

### Option 3

Strong hand

Usually:

- 18+ HCP
- Any distribution

Responder's first task is to determine which type opener holds.

---

# Responses to 1♣

## 1♦

Artificial.

Negative or waiting response.

Typically:

- 0–6 HCP

or

Relay asking opener to clarify.

---

## 1♥ / 1♠

Natural.

Shows:

- Five-card major
- Positive values

---

## 1NT

Natural.

Balanced.

Usually:

- 7–10 HCP

---

## 2♣

Natural.

Constructive club raise.

---

## 2♦+

Natural.

Game forcing when appropriate.

# Memory Aid

Think of **Polish Club** as:

- **One club can mean three different hand types**
- **Natural five-card majors**
- **Limited natural openings outside 1♣**
- **Responder's first job is to discover which type of 1♣ opener partner holds**

The key principle is:

> **1♣ is a flexible catch-all opening; subsequent bidding identifies whether opener has a balanced minimum, a natural club hand, or a strong hand.**
