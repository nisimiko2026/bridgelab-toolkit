# Precision Club System

## Overview

**Precision Club** is a **strong club bidding system** in which the **1♣ opening is artificial and forcing**, showing a strong hand. All other one-level openings are limited in strength, allowing the partnership to exchange information efficiently while making slam bidding more accurate.

The structure below describes the **Standard Precision Club** system. Partnership agreements may vary.

---

# System Summary

| Opening | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **1♣** | **Artificial, 16+ HCP, any distribution** |
| **1♦** | 11–15 HCP, 2+ diamonds (short diamond) |
| **1♥** | 11–15 HCP, 5+ hearts |
| **1♠** | 11–15 HCP, 5+ spades |
| **1NT** | 13–15 HCP, balanced |
| **2♣** | 11–15 HCP, 6+ clubs |
| **2♦** | Multi or Weak Two (partnership agreement) |
| **2♥** | Weak Two |
| **2♠** | Weak Two |
| **2NT** | 20–21 balanced (typical) |
| **3-level** | Preemptive |

# Opening Bids

## 1♣ — Strong Club

Artificial.

Shows:

- 16+ HCP
- Any distribution
- Forcing for one round

May contain:

- Balanced hands
- One-suited hands
- Two-suited hands

---

## Responses to 1♣

### 1♦

Negative response.

Shows:

- 0–7 HCP

---

### 1♥

Positive response.

Shows:

- 8+ HCP
- 5+ hearts

---

### 1♠

Positive response.

Shows:

- 8+ HCP
- 5+ spades

---

### 1NT

Positive balanced response.

Shows:

- 8–10 HCP
- Balanced

---

### 2♣ / 2♦

Positive natural response.

Shows:

- Good six-card suit

# Partnership Agreements

Agree on:

- Exact Strong Club range (16+ or 17+).
- Meaning of the negative 1♦ response.
- Positive response structure.
- Short-diamond style.
- 1NT range.
- Meaning of 2♦ opening.
- Roman Key Card version.
- Fourth Suit Forcing.
- Splinters.
- Bergen Raises.
- Drury.
- Support Doubles.

---

# Advantages

- Strong hands identified immediately.
- Limited openings simplify later bidding.
- Excellent slam exploration.
- Accurate hand description.
- Difficult for opponents to compete over the strong club.

---

# Disadvantages

- Artificial opening requires partnership agreement.
- Strong club may be vulnerable to interference.
- More memory-intensive than natural systems.
- Different Precision versions vary significantly.

# Memory Aid

Think of Precision as:

- **Strong Club**
- **Limited Natural Openings**
- **Fast Hand Definition**

The key principle is simple:

> **Strong hands start with 1♣. Everything else is limited.**
