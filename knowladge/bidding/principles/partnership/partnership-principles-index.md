# Partnership Principles

## Overview

Bridge is fundamentally a partnership game. Technical knowledge alone is not
enough; success depends on two players communicating accurately, trusting each
other's agreements, and applying sound judgement throughout the auction.

The **Partnership Principles** section explains the concepts that every
partnership should understand, regardless of the bidding system or conventions
used.

These principles apply equally to

- natural systems,
- artificial systems,
- duplicate bridge,
- rubber bridge,
- tournament partnerships.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should understand

- how successful partnerships communicate,
- how agreements should be developed,
- how responsibilities are shared,
- how judgement complements system,
- how consistency improves results.

# Design Principles

## Partnership Before Convention

Good partnerships outperform large convention collections.

---

## One Agreement — One Meaning

Every partnership agreement should have one clear interpretation.

---

## Documentation

Undiscussed agreements should never be assumed.

Document everything that is not standard.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Partnership agreements should evolve through experience, discussion and review.
