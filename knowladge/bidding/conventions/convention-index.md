# Conventions

Bridge conventions are partnership agreements that assign special meanings to bids beyond their natural interpretation. This index groups the conventions by purpose to make them easy to locate and study.

---

# Notrump Conventions

### Stayman
Ask opener for a four-card major after a Notrump opening.

### Puppet Stayman
An advanced Stayman variation used to locate five- and four-card major fits.

### Jacoby Transfer
Transfer opener to a five-card major after a Notrump opening.

### Texas Transfer
Transfer to a major at the game level after a Notrump opening.

### Smolen
Shows five cards in one major and four in the other after Stayman.

### Gerber
Ace-asking convention using 4♣, primarily after Notrump auctions.

---

# Slam Conventions

### Blackwood
Classic convention for asking partner how many Aces they hold.

### Roman Key Card Blackwood (RKCB)
A refinement of Blackwood that includes the trump King as a Key Card.

### Cue Bids
Show first- or second-round controls while exploring slam.

---

# Major-Suit Raises

### Jacoby 2NT
Game-forcing raise of partner's major with four-card support.

### Bergen Raises
Conventional raises that describe both support and strength.

### Splinter
Jump bid showing trump support with a singleton or void.

### Drury
Helps distinguish minimum and full opening bids after a light third-seat opening.

---

# Minor-Suit Conventions

### Inverted Minors
Single raises of a minor are strong and forcing, while jump raises are weak.

---

# Competitive Conventions

### Michaels Cue Bid
Shows a two-suited hand after an opponent opens.

### Unusual Notrump
Shows the two lowest unbid suits after an opponent's opening bid.

### Cappelletti
Defense against a 1NT opening showing various one- and two-suited hands.

### DONT
Defense against a 1NT opening emphasizing single-suited or two-suited hands.

### Lebensohl
Handles competitive auctions after interference over a Notrump opening.

### Negative Double
Shows values, usually including an unbid major, after partner's overcall.

### Support Double
Shows exactly three-card support for partner's suit.

### Responsive Double
Competitive double showing values and support for the unbid suits.

### Takeout Double
Requests partner to bid an unbid suit, showing opening strength.

### Cue Bid
A conventional bid of the opponents' suit to show strength or a special hand type.

---

# Preemptive Conventions

### Weak Two
A weak opening bid showing a six-card suit.

### Multi 2♦
Artificial opening showing one of several possible weak or strong hand types.

---

# Relay and Artificial Systems

### Relay Bids
Artificial bids requesting partner to describe their hand in detail.

---

# Convention Categories at a Glance

| Category | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Notrump | Find major fits and improve Notrump auctions |
| Slam | Investigate slam safely and accurately |
| Major Raises | Describe support and strength after a major opening |
| Minor Raises | Improve minor-suit auctions |
| Competitive | Compete effectively after opponents enter the auction |
| Preemptive | Disrupt opponents while describing weak distributional hands |
| Relay | Exchange detailed hand information through artificial bidding |

# Memory Aid

**Conventions = "Agreements That Improve Communication."**

Every convention should:

- Have a clearly defined meaning.
- Be fully understood by both partners.
- Be recorded on the convention card.
- Be properly disclosed to the opponents.
- Be used consistently within the partnership.
