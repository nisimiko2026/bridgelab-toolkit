# Responses to Weak Two Openings

## Bridge Insight

**Responding to a Weak Two opening is primarily an exercise in evaluating playing strength rather than high-card points. Responder must determine whether the partnership should compete for a part-score, invite game, bid game directly, or investigate further using conventional inquiries.**

---

# Overview

This article describes the general methods for responding to a natural **Weak Two** opening.

It applies after:

```text
2♦
2♥
2♠
```

when these openings show:

- A natural six-card suit.
- Weak opening values.
- Preemptive intent.

The exact response structure depends upon partnership agreements.

---

# Objectives

Responder aims to:

- Stop safely in a part-score.
- Invite game when appropriate.
- Reach game with sufficient combined strength.
- Investigate opener's hand when necessary.
- Compete effectively after interference.

---

# Evaluating the Hand

Responder should consider:

- High-card strength.
- Trump support.
- Controls.
- Distribution.
- Suit quality.
- Vulnerability.
- Form of scoring.

Playing strength is often more important than HCP.

---

# Standard Responses

## Pass

With:

- Weak hand.
- No game interest.
- Limited support.

Passing is often the percentage action.

---

## Raise the Opening Suit

Simple raise

Usually:

- Constructive.
- Limited values.
- Trump support.

Partnership agreements vary.

---

## Jump Raise

Shows:

- Excellent support.
- Game interest.
- Offensive values.

---

## New Suit

Usually natural.

May show:

- Good five-card or longer suit.
- Constructive values.
- Possible game interest.

---

## 2NT

Artificial.

The most important conventional response.

Depending upon partnership agreement, 2NT may initiate:

- **Ogust**
- **Feature Ask**
- Other inquiry methods

See the relevant convention articles for the detailed response structures.

---

## 3NT

Natural.

Typically:

- Long running suit opposite opener.
- Sufficient stoppers.
- Game-going values.

---

## Game in Opener's Suit

Direct game with:

- Excellent trump support.
- Enough playing strength.

---

---

# Expert Thinking

Responder should remember that a Weak Two opening is based on **playing strength**.

Game is frequently correct despite relatively few combined HCP when opener has:

- Excellent suit quality.
- Favorable distribution.
- Good controls.

Conversely, game may fail with higher point counts if opener's suit is poor.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Overvaluing HCP.
- Ignoring suit quality.
- Forgetting the agreed meaning of 2NT.
- Raising without sufficient trump support.
- Underestimating offensive distribution.

---

# Best Practices

- Evaluate playing strength.
- Agree one inquiry method.
- Practice continuation auctions.
- Record agreements on the convention card.
- Discuss responses after interference.

# Expert Tip

**Do not treat every Weak Two opening alike.**

Before making a constructive move, evaluate:

- Suit quality.
- Vulnerability.
- Distribution.
- Playing tricks.

Two Weak Two openings with identical HCP may have dramatically different offensive potential. Expert responders judge the hand's trick-taking ability, not just its point count.

---

# Memory Aid

**Responding to Weak Two = "Evaluate, Then Investigate."**

Remember:

- Weak hands usually pass or raise simply.
- Constructive hands often begin with **2NT**.
- The meaning of **2NT** depends on your partnership agreement (Ogust, Feature Ask, or another method).
- Suit quality and playing strength are often more important than raw HCP.
- Clear partnership agreements are essential for successful continuation auctions.
