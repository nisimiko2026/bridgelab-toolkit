# Transfer Bids

> *Let partner declare while exchanging information efficiently.*

Transfer bidding is one of the most successful innovations in modern Contract
Bridge. Instead of bidding the intended suit directly, one player asks partner
to bid that suit, allowing the stronger hand to become declarer while preserving
valuable bidding space.

Although transfers originated as a notrump convention, modern partnerships now
employ transfer principles throughout constructive, competitive, and artificial
bidding systems.

This section of **BridgeLab** covers both traditional transfer conventions and
advanced transfer-based bidding methods.

---

# Introduction

A **transfer bid** is an artificial bid requesting partner to bid a specified
suit.

Transfers are used to

- conceal the stronger hand,
- preserve bidding space,
- improve declarer placement,
- simplify later bidding,
- support relay structures,
- increase bidding flexibility.

Most transfer bids are forcing for at least one round.

---

# Objectives

Transfer methods seek to

- place the strong hand on lead,
- improve communication,
- exchange information economically,
- distinguish hand types,
- simplify constructive auctions,
- improve competitive bidding,
- support advanced artificial systems.

# Fundamental Principles

Successful transfer bidding follows several principles.

- The artificial bid requests partner to bid another suit.
- The transferor normally becomes dummy.
- The stronger hand usually becomes declarer.
- Bidding space is preserved whenever possible.
- Partnership agreements are essential.

---

# Why Use Transfers?

Transfers provide several advantages over natural bidding.

They

- conceal honor location,
- protect tenaces,
- simplify later auctions,
- create room for invitations,
- improve slam investigations,
- make competitive auctions more flexible.

---

# Super Accepts

Some transfer methods permit opener to show

- extra values,
- excellent trump support,
- maximum strength,

by accepting the transfer in a special manner.

Partnership agreement determines exactly when these bids apply.

---

# Transfer Acceptance

The requested bid is normally automatic.

However,

later bidding may distinguish

- minimum,
- maximum,
- fit,
- slam interest,
- control holdings.
