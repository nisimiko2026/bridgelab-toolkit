# Defense Against Precision

## Purpose

**Precision** is a strong club bidding system in which:

- **1♣** is an artificial strong opening (typically 16+ HCP).
- Most one-level suit openings are **limited**.
- Relay and artificial continuations are common.

Defending against Precision requires more than simply handling the strong 1♣ opening. A complete defense should also exploit the limited nature of the system's natural openings.

---

# Objectives

A good defense should:

- Disrupt relay sequences after **1♣**.
- Compete aggressively against limited openings.
- Penalize inaccurate competitive decisions.
- Make constructive overcalls when appropriate.
- Preserve natural bidding whenever possible.

---

# Two Parts of the Defense

## 1. Defense to the Strong 1♣ Opening

When opponents open:

```text
1♣
```

the partnership typically adopts one of several methods:

| Method | Description |
|---------|-------------|
|Natural|Natural overcalls and doubles|
|CRASH|Artificial defense showing suit characteristics|
|Suction|Transfer-style interference|
|CROC|Artificial two-suited defense|
|Mathe|Mostly natural defense|

Many partnerships simply use the same agreements they play against any **Strong Club** system.

---

## 2. Defense to Limited Natural Openings

After Precision openings such as:

```text
1♦
1♥
1♠
```

the openings are usually limited.

This permits:

- More aggressive overcalls.
- Lighter takeout doubles.
- More balancing actions.

---

# Natural Competitive Methods

Standard methods remain effective:

- Takeout Double
- Negative Double
- Responsive Double
- Support Double
- Michaels Cue Bid
- Unusual 2NT

Many partnerships play these exactly as against Standard American.

---

# Competing Against Limited Openings

Because opener is limited:

Upgrade:

- Distributional hands.
- Two-suited hands.
- Good five-card suits.

Be willing to:

- Balance aggressively.
- Reopen with lighter values.
- Compete to the three level with suitable shape.

---

# After Strong Club Relays

If opponents begin a relay sequence:

```text
1♣
Pass
1♦
```

interference can be particularly effective because:

- Relay auctions consume little space.
- Artificial continuations depend on uninterrupted communication.
- Even simple natural overcalls may disrupt the exchange of information.

---

# Hand Evaluation

Upgrade:

- Distribution.
- Intermediate honors.
- Offensive hands.

Downgrade:

- Flat balanced minimums.
- Poor suit quality.

---

# Advantages

- Exploits Precision's limited openings.
- Disrupts relay auctions.
- Preserves standard competitive methods.
- Allows flexible partnership agreements.

---

# Disadvantages

- Requires knowledge of Precision structure.
- Artificial defenses increase memory requirements.
- Different Precision partnerships use different continuations.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Agree on:

- Defense against the strong 1♣ opening.
- Meaning of doubles.
- Treatment of limited openings.
- Balancing methods.
- Vulnerability adjustments.
- Responses after relay interference.

---

# Comparison

| Situation | Typical Defense |
|-----------|-----------------|
|Strong 1♣ opening|CRASH, Suction, Natural, CROC, Mathe|
|Limited 1♦ / 1♥ / 1♠ openings|Standard competitive methods with greater aggression|
|Relay auctions|Early natural interference whenever appropriate|

---

# Precision vs. Strong Club

Although Precision is a Strong Club system, it differs because:

- One-level suit openings are limited.
- Relay sequences are more frequent.
- Natural openings are often more accurately defined.

These characteristics encourage more aggressive competitive bidding than against some other Strong Club systems.

# Summary

A successful defense against **Precision** has two components:

1. **A defense to the strong artificial 1♣ opening**, such as Natural, CRASH, Suction, CROC, or Mathe.
2. **Aggressive competition against Precision's limited natural openings**, taking advantage of the system's restricted opening ranges.

**Primary idea:** Defending against Precision is not limited to handling the strong **1♣** opening. Equally important is recognizing that the system's natural openings are **limited**, allowing the partnership to compete more aggressively than against standard natural systems.
