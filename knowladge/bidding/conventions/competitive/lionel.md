# Lionel

## Purpose

**Lionel** is a conventional defense against an opponent's **1NT opening**, developed by **Lionel Wright** of New Zealand.

Its objectives are to:

- Compete aggressively with two-suited hands
- Preserve natural major-suit overcalls
- Make constructive use of the double
- Disrupt the opponents' notrump auction

Unlike many modern defenses, Lionel is designed around **constructive values** rather than purely distributional hands.

---

After an opponent opens **1NT**.

# Standard Structure

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
|Double|Spades + another suit|
|2♣|Clubs + hearts|
|2♦|Diamonds + hearts|
|2♥|Natural hearts|
|2♠|Natural spades|
|2NT|Both minors (common agreement)|

Typical requirements:

- **4-4 or better** in the shown suits
- Approximately **11–12+ HCP** (constructive values)

This is a key distinction from many other 1NT defenses, which often operate with weaker hands. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

# Meanings

## Double

Shows:

- Spades
- Another suit (clubs, diamonds, or hearts)

Partner normally assumes spades are one suit and asks for the second suit if necessary.

---

## 2♣

Shows:

- Clubs
- Hearts

Typically:

- 4-4 or longer

---

## 2♦

Shows:

- Diamonds
- Hearts

Typically:

- 4-4 or longer

---

## 2♥

Natural.

Shows:

- A long heart suit

Usually six or more cards.

---

## 2♠

Natural.

Shows:

- A long spade suit

Usually six or more cards.

---

## 2NT

Most commonly shows:

- Both minors

This treatment is a partnership agreement.

# Advancer's Responses

## After Double

Typical continuations are:

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
|Pass|Penalty interest (usually 10+ HCP)|
|2♣|Pass-or-correct; asks doubler to clarify the second suit|
|2♦|Natural diamonds with heart tolerance or pass/correct by agreement|
|2♥ / 2♠|Natural|
|2NT|Constructive raise of spades|

The relay through **2♣** is the most common method. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## After 2♣ or 2♦

Advancer:

- Passes with a fit.
- Chooses the preferred known suit.
- Invites game with extra values.

# Advantages

- Preserves natural 2♥ and 2♠ overcalls
- Excellent for constructive 4-4 two-suiters
- Keeps the penalty double available through partner's pass
- Relatively easy to remember
- Effective against both weak and strong 1NT openings

---

# Disadvantages

- Less effective with weak distributional hands
- Less widely played than DONT or Cappelletti
- Requires relay agreements after the double

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Minimum HCP (commonly 11+)
- Minimum shape (4-4 or better)
- Meaning of 2NT
- Responses to the double
- Treatment versus weak and strong 1NT openings

**Primary idea:** **Lionel** is a constructive defense to an opponent's **1NT opening** that uses the **double** and **minor-suit overcalls** to show specific two-suited hands while leaving **2♥** and **2♠** available as natural overcalls.
