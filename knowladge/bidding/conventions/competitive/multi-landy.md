# Multi-Landy Convention

## Overview

**Multi-Landy** is an advanced defense against an opponent's **1NT opening**. It combines the strengths of **Landy** (showing both majors) with the flexibility of conventions such as **Cappelletti** and **Hamilton**, enabling the partnership to describe almost every useful one-suited and two-suited hand.

Unlike standard Landy, which only uses **2♣** to show both majors, Multi-Landy assigns artificial meanings to several two-level bids, allowing both partners to compete more accurately while placing immediate pressure on the opponents.

There is **no universally accepted version** of Multi-Landy. The structure below is the most widely played modern treatment.

---

# Purpose

Multi-Landy is designed to:

- Compete aggressively over 1NT.
- Show both one-suited and two-suited hands.
- Locate major-suit fits.
- Increase preemptive pressure.
- Disrupt the opponents' notrump auction.

---

# When to Use

Multi-Landy applies after:

```
(1NT)
?
```

It may be played against:

- Strong Notrump
- Weak Notrump

Many partnerships adopt different strength requirements depending on the opponents' notrump range.

---

# Standard Structure

One popular modern version is:

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Double | Strong balanced hand (penalty or equivalent) |
| 2♣ | Both major suits (5-4 or better) |
| 2♦ | One unspecified major |
| 2♥ | Hearts and a minor |
| 2♠ | Spades and a minor |
| 2NT | Both minor suits |

Several regional variations exist.

---

# Double

```
(1NT)
Double
```

Usually shows:

- Approximately 15-18 HCP
- Balanced hand
- Interest in defending

Some partnerships play a purely penalty double.

---

# 2♣ — Both Majors

```
(1NT)
2♣*
```

Shows:

- Hearts
- Spades

Usually:

- 5-4
- 5-5
- Occasionally good 4-4

Advancer selects the preferred major.

---

# 2♦ — One Major

```
(1NT)
2♦*
```

Artificial.

Shows:

- Exactly one major suit.

Advancer normally bids:

```
2♥
```

Responder then:

- Passes with hearts.
- Corrects to 2♠ with spades.

This structure resembles Multi 2♦.

---

# 2♥ — Hearts and a Minor

```
(1NT)
2♥*
```

Shows:

- Hearts
- An unknown minor

Usually:

- 5-5

Sometimes:

- Strong 5-4

---

# 2♠ — Spades and a Minor

```
(1NT)
2♠*
```

Shows:

- Spades
- An unknown minor

Usually:

- 5-5

---

# 2NT — Both Minors

```
(1NT)
2NT*
```

Shows:

- Clubs
- Diamonds

Usually:

- 5-5 or better

---

# Strength Requirements

Typical style:

Minimum:

- 8-10 HCP

Normal:

- 9-14 HCP

Distribution and playing tricks are more important than raw HCP.

---

# Suit Quality

Good suit quality remains important.

Preferred holdings include:

- Two of the top three honors.
- Three of the top five honors.

Poor suits should generally not be introduced aggressively.

---

# Advancer's Responsibilities

Advancer evaluates:

- Fit.
- Strength.
- Distribution.
- Vulnerability.

Possible actions include:

- Pass.
- Correct.
- Invite game.
- Bid game.
- Compete further.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Partners should discuss:

- Meaning of Double.
- Minimum shape.
- Whether 2♦ shows one major or another hand type.
- Responses to two-suited bids.
- Balancing methods.
- Competitive continuations.

# Competitive Auctions

## Overview

After Multi-Landy enters the auction, both partners should continue naturally while remembering that opener has already described distribution.

---

## Opponent Doubles

Example

```
1NT
2♣*
(Double)
?
```

Typical agreements include:

- Pass.
- Redouble.
- Natural preference.
- Escape bids.

---

## Opponent Raises Notrump

Example

```
1NT
2♣*
3NT
?
```

Evaluate:

- Combined strength.
- Fit.
- Playing tricks.
- Vulnerability.

Sometimes defending is best.

Sometimes sacrificing is correct.

---

## Competition After Artificial Responses

Example

```
1NT
2♦*
Pass
2♥
(Double)
?
```

The overcaller continues naturally.

Both partners reevaluate:

- Distribution.
- Playing strength.
- Vulnerability.

---

## Balancing Position

Many partnerships use the same structure after:

```
1NT
Pass
Pass
?
```

This should be agreed beforehand.

---

# Advantages

Multi-Landy:

- Describes many hand types.
- Locates major fits efficiently.
- Excellent competitive tool.
- Strong preemptive effect.
- Flexible structure.

---

# Limitations

The convention:

- Requires extensive partnership agreements.
- Several competing versions exist.
- Artificial bids may help the opponents.
- More memory-intensive than Landy.

---

# Variations

Popular treatments include:

- Standard Multi-Landy
- English Multi-Landy
- Scandinavian Multi-Landy
- Modified Multi-Landy
- Transfer Multi-Landy

Each partnership should define the exact meanings of every bid.

---

# Common Mistakes

## Forgetting the Artificial Meanings

Almost every bid is conventional.

---

## Confusing Multi-Landy with Landy

Standard Landy uses only:

```
2♣
```

Multi-Landy uses several artificial bids.

---

## Showing Weak Suits

Distribution is important, but suit quality still matters.

---

## Ignoring Partnership Agreements

Different published versions assign different meanings to:

- 2♦
- 2♥
- 2♠
- 2NT

Always review your version before playing.

---

# Tips

- Learn ordinary Landy before Multi-Landy.
- Memorize the complete response structure.
- Practice the relay after 2♦.
- Agree exactly which version your partnership uses.
- Remember that distribution is usually more important than HCP.

---

# Modern Usage

Multi-Landy is played mainly by experienced partnerships that want a more complete defense to 1NT than standard Landy provides. Although less common than **Landy**, **Hamilton**, or **Cappelletti**, it offers excellent descriptive power and fits naturally into modern competitive bidding systems.

Because several versions exist, tournament partnerships should carry a convention card describing their exact agreements.

---

# Summary

Multi-Landy is an advanced defense against a 1NT opening that extends the basic Landy concept by incorporating artificial bids to show one-suited and two-suited hands. It enables partnerships to compete effectively, locate major-suit fits, and disrupt the opponents' notrump auctions while providing considerably more descriptive power than standard Landy. Successful use depends on disciplined partnership agreements and thorough familiarity with the chosen version.
