# Sandwich Notrump

## Bridge Insight

**Sandwich Notrump allows a player caught between two opponents ("in the sandwich seat") to compete aggressively with a two-suited hand. Rather than showing a balanced Notrump hand, the 2NT overcall immediately describes distribution, making it difficult for the opponents to exchange information while helping partner identify the best fit.**

---

# Overview

**Sandwich Notrump** is an artificial **2NT overcall** made when the player is in the **sandwich position**—after one opponent has opened, partner has passed, and the other opponent has responded.

The convention usually shows:

- The **two lowest unbid suits**.
- A distributional hand.
- Competitive values.

Unlike a natural 2NT overcall, Sandwich Notrump emphasizes **shape rather than balanced strength**.

---

# Objectives

Sandwich Notrump enables the partnership to:

- Show a two-suited hand immediately.
- Compete after the opponents have found a fit.
- Consume valuable bidding space.
- Help partner choose the correct strain.
- Describe offensive distribution accurately.

---

# When to Use

Typical auctions:

```text
West      North      East      South

1♣        Pass       1♠        2NT
```

South is in the **sandwich seat**.

The two unbid suits are:

- ♦
- ♥

2NT shows **Diamonds and Hearts**.

---

Another example:

```text
West      North      East      South

1♥        Pass       1♠        2NT
```

The two lowest unbid suits are:

- ♣
- ♦

2NT shows **Clubs and Diamonds**.

# Requirements

Typical hand:

### Strength

- Approximately 10–16 HCP.
- Good offensive playing strength.

### Distribution

Normally:

- 5-5 or better.

Some partnerships permit:

- 5-4 shape.
- Lighter action when favorable vulnerable.

# Advantages

Sandwich Notrump:

- Describes two suits immediately.
- Competes effectively after the opponents exchange information.
- Makes constructive competition easier.
- Creates difficult decisions for the opponents.
- Helps partner judge offensive potential.

---

# Limitations

Sandwich Notrump:

- Requires genuine two-suited distribution.
- Consumes bidding space.
- Vulnerable to penalty doubles.
- Requires partnership agreement.

# Expert Thinking

The sandwich seat is often difficult because the opponents have already exchanged useful information.

Rather than competing with a natural overcall, Sandwich Notrump immediately identifies two long suits, allowing partner to evaluate offensive potential instead of guessing the hand's distribution.

Experts rely on shape more than HCP when employing the convention.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Using Sandwich Notrump with balanced hands.
- Showing unsuitable distribution.
- Forgetting the advertised suits.
- Competing with insufficient playing strength.
- Ignoring vulnerability.

---

# Best Practices

- Promise genuine two-suited distribution.
- Prefer 5-5 shape.
- Agree minimum strength.
- Practice continuation auctions.
- Record agreements on the convention card.

# Expert Tip

**Sandwich Notrump is the competitive counterpart to Unusual 2NT.**

Both conventions emphasize immediate disclosure of distribution rather than balanced strength. The difference is **timing**: Unusual 2NT is used directly over an opening bid, while Sandwich Notrump is employed after the opponents have begun to exchange information, allowing the partnership to compete effectively despite the disadvantage of acting in the sandwich position.
