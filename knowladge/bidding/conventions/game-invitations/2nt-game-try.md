# 2NT Game Try

## Definition

The **2NT Game Try** is an **artificial game invitation** used after a major-suit fit has been established.

Instead of immediately describing

- a long suit,
- shortness,
- or a weak suit,

opener bids **2NT** to ask responder to evaluate the partnership's combined strength according to an agreed partnership structure.

Unlike natural game tries, the **2NT bid itself normally conveys very little information about opener's distribution**.

It simply initiates an artificial exchange.

---

# Basic Philosophy

Natural game tries answer

> "Can this suit produce tricks?"

or

> "Does my shortness fit your hand?"

The Artificial 2NT Game Try asks

> **"Before we decide about game, let's exchange more information."**

The emphasis shifts from

describing immediately

to

describing accurately.

---

# Purpose

The convention allows partnerships to

- preserve bidding space,
- exchange additional information,
- distinguish different hand types,
- improve responder's evaluation,
- reduce guessing,
- reach better game contracts.

---

# Why 2NT?

Among all available bids,

2NT has several advantages.

It

- consumes very little bidding space,
- does not identify a side suit,
- leaves room below game,
- naturally introduces relay sequences.

For these reasons,

many advanced partnerships choose

```
2NT
```

as the artificial inquiry.

---

# General Principle

The auction proceeds in two stages.

Stage 1

```
2NT

↓

Artificial inquiry
```

Stage 2

Responder and opener exchange additional information according to partnership agreement.

The exact continuations vary.

---

# Typical Auction

```
South      North

1♠
2♠
2NT*
```

Meaning

- Spades agreed.
- Invitational values.
- Artificial.
- Further description to follow.

Responder does **not** evaluate immediately.

Instead,

the partnership begins its agreed information exchange.

---

# Why Not Bid a Suit?

Suppose opener bids

```
3♦
```

Responder immediately assumes

diamonds are important.

Suppose opener actually wished to describe

- shortness,
- controls,
- balanced extras.

Natural bidding cannot accomplish this.

Artificial 2NT avoids premature conclusions.

---

# Objectives

The convention seeks to

- exchange information efficiently,
- preserve bidding space,
- identify offensive strength,
- classify hand types,
- improve game decisions.

---

# Information That May Follow

Different partnerships use

2NT

to begin descriptions of

- balanced maximum,
- balanced minimum,
- long suit,
- short suit,
- controls,
- singleton,
- void,
- offensive values,
- Losing Trick Count.

There is **no universal continuation structure**.

---

Most partnerships employ

2NT Game Try

with

- good 14 HCP,
- 15–17 HCP,
- strong offensive values,
- distributional extras,
- uncertainty about game.

Playing strength remains more important than point count.

---

# Advantages

The Artificial 2NT Game Try

- preserves bidding space,
- permits multiple hand descriptions,
- reduces ambiguity,
- integrates naturally with relay methods,
- improves responder's evaluation,
- fits modern bidding theory.

---

# Disadvantages

The convention also has limitations.

- No universally accepted continuation structure.
- Requires extensive partnership agreements.
- Higher memory requirements.
- Not suitable for occasional partnerships.
- Requires careful convention-card disclosure.

---

# Relationship to Other Game Tries

The Artificial 2NT Game Try does **not** replace every natural game try.

Instead,

it provides a framework within which

- Long Suit,
- Short Suit,
- Balanced,
- Distributional

hands can all be described after the initial inquiry.

It therefore represents one of the most flexible Artificial Game Try structures.

---

# Historical Perspective

As bidding theory evolved,

expert partnerships increasingly sought methods that

- exchanged more information,
- consumed less bidding space,
- distinguished more hand types.

The Artificial 2NT Game Try became one of the most widely adopted solutions because

- 2NT is available in many invitational auctions,
- it is naturally artificial,
- it leaves ample room for descriptive continuations.

Different expert partnerships have since developed numerous continuation structures, but the central philosophy has remained unchanged.

# Advanced Philosophy

The Artificial **2NT Game Try** is built upon one central principle:

> **Do not make the final decision until both partners have exchanged the information that matters.**

Traditional game tries require opener to immediately choose one descriptive
feature.

The Artificial **2NT** Game Try postpones that decision.

Instead,

the partnership first establishes an **artificial inquiry**, then exchanges
additional information before deciding whether to bid game.

---

# Information Economy

One of the primary goals of the convention is

**information economy**.

Every bid should communicate

as much useful information

as possible,

while revealing

as little unnecessary information

as possible.

This philosophy is central to modern expert bidding.

Among all available invitational bids,

```
2NT
```

offers several advantages.

It

- consumes little bidding space,
- has no natural suit implication,
- permits relay sequences,
- allows multiple descriptive continuations,
- integrates naturally with advanced systems.

For these reasons,

it has become the preferred artificial inquiry

for many expert partnerships.

# Expert Recommendations

The Artificial **2NT Game Try** is one of the most flexible methods available
for inviting game after a major-suit fit has been established.

Its strength lies **not** in the 2NT bid itself, but in the partnership's
ability to exchange meaningful information before committing to game.

Expert partnerships generally follow three principles:

1. Keep the initial 2NT bid simple.
2. Make the continuation structure logical and consistent.
3. Evaluate hands using playing strength rather than HCP alone.

---

# Choosing Whether to Play 2NT Game Tries

The convention is most suitable for partnerships that

- play together regularly,
- enjoy structured bidding,
- are comfortable with artificial methods,
- maintain a detailed convention card.

Partnerships that play only occasionally usually obtain better results from

- Help Suit Game Tries,
- Long Suit Game Tries,
- Maximal Game Tries.

# Summary

The **Artificial 2NT Game Try** is an advanced partnership treatment in which

```
2NT
```

acts as an **artificial inquiry** rather than a natural description.

Its primary purpose is to preserve bidding space while allowing partners to
exchange information about

- strength,
- shape,
- controls,
- offensive potential,
- distribution,

before deciding whether to bid game.

Unlike natural game tries,

the value of the convention lies almost entirely in the agreed continuation
structure.

For experienced partnerships, Artificial 2NT provides one of the most precise
methods available for evaluating close game contracts.

---

# Key Points

- 2NT is **artificial**, not natural.
- The bid initiates an information exchange rather than describing a suit.
- There is **no universally accepted continuation structure**.
- Relay methods are common but not mandatory.
- Playing strength is more important than HCP.
- Trump quality, controls, and distribution remain critical.
- Most partnerships replace the method with **Maximal Game Try** after significant interference.
- Clear partnership agreements are essential.
- Accurate documentation on the convention card is strongly recommended.
