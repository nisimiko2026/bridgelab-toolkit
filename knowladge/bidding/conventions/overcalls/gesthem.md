# Ghestem Convention

## Bridge Insight

**Ghestem extends the ideas of Michaels Cue Bid and Unusual 2NT into a complete system. Using three artificial overcalls, it allows the partnership to show any two-suited hand immediately and unambiguously.**

---

# Overview

**Ghestem** is an advanced two-suited overcall convention that enables the overcaller to show **any two-suited hand** with a single artificial bid.

Unlike **Michaels Cue Bid** and **Unusual 2NT**, which each describe only certain suit combinations, Ghestem assigns three artificial bids to represent all three possible two-suited combinations.

The convention is particularly attractive because every two-suited hand has a unique and immediate description.

---

# Objectives

Ghestem enables the partnership to:

- Show any two-suited hand.
- Describe distribution immediately.
- Compete aggressively.
- Obstruct the opponents.
- Locate the best fit quickly.

# Convention Structure

Ghestem uses **three different artificial overcalls**.

## Cue Bid

Shows:

- Highest unbid suit.
- Lowest unbid suit.

Example:

```text
(1♥)

2♥
```

Shows:

```
Spades + Clubs
```

---

## 2NT

Shows:

- Two lowest unbid suits.

Example:

```text
(1♥)

2NT
```

Shows:

```
Clubs + Diamonds
```

---

## 3♣

Shows:

- Two highest unbid suits.

Example:

```text
(1♥)

3♣
```

Shows:

```
Spades + Diamonds
```

The exact mapping depends upon the opponent's opening suit.

---

# Suit Combination Table

| Artificial Bid | Shows |
|----------------|-------|
| Cue Bid | Highest + Lowest unbid suits |
| 2NT | Lowest two unbid suits |
| 3♣ | Highest two unbid suits |

Together these three bids describe every possible two-suited hand.

# Advantages

Ghestem:

# Limitations

Ghestem:

- Requires substantial memory.
- Sacrifices some natural overcalls.
- Less common than Michaels.

# Expert Thinking

Ghestem treats **distribution as the most important feature of a competitive hand**.

Instead of asking:

> "How many points do I have?"

the convention asks:

> "Which two suits will produce the most tricks?"

This philosophy explains why Ghestem is popular among aggressive tournament partnerships.

# Expert Tip

**Don't memorize Ghestem auction by auction—memorize the pattern.**

Every Ghestem bid describes a relationship between the **three unbid suits**:

- **Cue Bid = Highest + Lowest**
- **2NT = Lowest Two**
- **3♣ = Highest Two**

Once you understand that pattern, you can reconstruct the correct meaning after any opening bid without memorizing dozens of individual auctions.

---

# Memory Aid

**Ghestem = "Three Bids, Three Combinations."**

Remember:

- Every 5-5 two-suited hand has a unique artificial overcall.
- The convention is built on the **three unbid suits**, not the opponent's suit.
- Ghestem is more descriptive than Michaels or Unusual 2NT, but it requires stronger partnership discipline and better memory.
- For experienced partnerships, it provides one of the most complete systems for showing two-suited competitive hands.
