# Wolff Signoff

## Purpose

**Wolff Signoff** is a convention used after opener rebids **2NT** in a **game-forcing auction**.

Its objectives are to:

- Allow responder to stop in a safe minor-suit contract when game is not desirable.
- Preserve bidding space for slam investigation.
- Clarify responder's distribution before committing to game.

The convention is most commonly played in **Two Over One Game Force (2/1 GF)** systems.

---

# When It Applies

The classic auction is:

| Auction |
|---------|
|1♥ – 2♣ |
|2NT – ? |

or

| Auction |
|---------|
|1♠ – 2♦ |
|2NT – ? |

The partnership is already **in a game force**.

---

# Basic Structure

After opener rebids **2NT**:

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
|3♣|Artificial relay (Wolff Signoff)|
|3♦|Forced response by opener|
|Responder's next bid|Defines the final contract or continues slam exploration|

The **3♣ bid is artificial** and does **not** promise clubs.

---

# Relay Sequence

Typical sequence:

1♥ – 2♣

2NT – 3♣

3♦ (forced)

Now responder chooses:

- Pass
- 3♥
- 3♠
- 3NT
- 4♣
- 4♦
- Cue bids
- Slam investigation

---

# Signing Off

Example:

1♠

2♣

2NT

3♣

3♦

Pass

Responder wanted to play **3♦**.

Without Wolff Signoff, responder would have been forced to bid 3♦ directly, consuming valuable bidding space.

---

# Invitational or Slam Hands

Although the auction is already game forcing, responder may use the relay to:

- Show a long minor
- Clarify distribution
- Begin cue bidding
- Establish a trump suit
- Explore slam

Responder

♠ 5  
♥ K74  
♦ AQJ1086  
♣ KQ82

Auction

1♠

2♣

2NT

3♣

3♦

Pass

Responder signs off in diamonds.

# Example 2

Auction

1♥

2♦

2NT

3♣

3♦

3♥

Responder shows a heart preference while preserving bidding space.

---

# Advantages

- Keeps the bidding low
- Improves minor-suit contract selection
- Preserves space for slam investigation
- Simple relay structure
- Integrates well with 2/1 Game Force

---

# Disadvantages

- Uses an artificial relay
- Requires partnership agreement
- Limited usefulness outside game-forcing auctions

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Exact auctions where Wolff Signoff applies.
- Whether opener must always bid **3♦**.
- Continuations after the relay.
- Interaction with serious/non-serious 3NT.
- Treatment after interference.

# Summary

| Auction | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
|1♥ – 2♣ – 2NT – 3♣|Artificial Wolff Signoff relay|
|… – 3♦|Forced response|
|Responder's next bid|Signs off or continues naturally|

**Primary idea:** After opener's **2NT rebid** in a game-forcing auction, responder bids **3♣** as an artificial relay. Opener must bid **3♦**, allowing responder to sign off in a lower-level contract or continue describing the hand with maximum bidding space.
