# 2NT Inquiry over Weak Two

## Overview

The **2NT Inquiry** is an artificial conventional response to partner's
**Weak Two opening** (2♦, 2♥, or 2♠). It asks opener to describe the hand more
precisely so responder can judge whether the partnership should stop in part
score, invite game, bid game, or investigate slam.

The convention is the foundation for modern continuations after Weak Two
openings and is usually combined with either **Feature Ask** or **Ogust**
responses.

---

# Objectives

The 2NT Inquiry allows responder to

- determine opener's playing strength,
- evaluate suit quality,
- locate outside features,
- judge game prospects,
- distinguish minimum from maximum weak two openings.

Responder is the **captain of the auction**.

---

# When It Is Used

Typical auction

```
West      North      East      South
                     2♥
Pass      2NT*       Pass      ?
```

```
2NT*
```

Artificial inquiry.

It is **forcing for one round**.

---

# Requirements for the Inquiry

Responder normally holds

- approximately 15+ HCP,
- game interest,
- or a hand requiring additional information.

The inquiry is based on **playing strength**, not merely HCP.

---

# Common Response Methods

Modern partnerships normally choose one of two structures:

- **Feature Ask**
- **Ogust**

The partnership should agree on one method.

---

# Method 1 – Feature Ask

The traditional expert treatment.

After

```
2♠
2NT*
```

Typical responses

| Rebid | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 3♣ | Minimum / no feature |
| 3♦ | Diamond feature |
| 3♥ | Heart feature |
| 3♠ | Good spade suit, no side feature (partnership agreement) |
| 3NT | Club feature (or maximum, depending on agreement) |

A **feature** is usually

- an outside Ace,
- an outside King,
- occasionally a guarded Queen,
- depending on partnership style.

---

# Method 2 – Ogust

Many expert partnerships replace Feature Ask with **Ogust**.

After

```
2♥
2NT*
```

Typical responses

| Rebid | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 3♣ | Minimum, poor suit |
| 3♦ | Minimum, good suit |
| 3♥ | Maximum, poor suit |
| 3♠ | Maximum, good suit |
| 3NT | Maximum, excellent suit (optional agreement) |

Ogust emphasizes

- overall strength,
- trump quality,

rather than outside features.

---

# Evaluating Suit Quality

Suit quality normally depends on

- number of honors,
- intermediate cards,
- expected trick-taking ability.

Examples

Good suit

```
AKQJ10xx
KQJ10xxx
AQJ10xxx
```

Poor suit

```
Q1098xxx
K109xxx
```

---

# Continuations

After opener's response,

responder may

- Pass
- Raise to game
- Bid 3NT
- Explore slam
- Return to opener's suit

depending on the information received.

# Choosing Between Feature Ask and Ogust

## Feature Ask

Advantages

- Locates outside controls.
- Better for slam investigation.
- Provides detailed information.

Disadvantages

- Does not immediately distinguish minimum from maximum.

---

## Ogust

Advantages

- Quickly evaluates game chances.
- Simple structure.
- Widely used.

Disadvantages

- Provides less information about outside controls.

---

# Expert Style

Modern expert partnerships tend to

- use **Ogust** when game decisions are the priority,
- use **Feature Ask** when emphasizing outside controls.

Some advanced partnerships combine elements of both methods.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Partners should define

- whether 2NT is Feature Ask or Ogust,
- what constitutes a feature,
- suit-quality requirements,
- continuation structure,
- meanings after interference.

Precise agreements are essential.

---

# Competitive Auctions

If opponents interfere,

partnership agreements should specify

- whether 2NT remains artificial,
- whether doubles replace the inquiry,
- continuation methods.

Many partnerships retain the inquiry whenever possible.

---

# Common Mistakes

## Confusing Feature Ask and Ogust

The two methods convey different information.

---

## Treating 2NT as Natural

In standard expert methods,

2NT is almost always artificial.

---

## Ignoring Playing Strength

Responder should evaluate

- offensive potential,
- fit,
- controls,

not simply High Card Points.

---

## Forgetting Partnership Agreements

Feature definitions and Ogust responses vary.

---

## Investigating Slam Too Frequently

Weak Two openings are limited hands.

Slam should be exceptional.

# Summary

The **2NT Inquiry over a Weak Two** is one of the most important modern
conventions following preemptive openings. It allows responder to evaluate
whether opener has sufficient playing strength for game by requesting additional
information through either **Feature Ask** or **Ogust** responses.

Expert partnerships agree in advance which structure they use, enabling opener
to describe the hand efficiently while allowing responder to make accurate
decisions about partscore, game, or the occasional slam.
