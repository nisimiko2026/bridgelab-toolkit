# Spiral Scan

## Purpose

**Spiral Scan** is an advanced **grand slam investigation** method used **after a successful Key Card Ask**.

Once the partnership knows that **all key cards are present**, the asker may continue with **Spiral Scan** to determine:

- Which side kings partner holds
- Sometimes the location of specific queens
- Whether a grand slam is likely to succeed

Spiral Scan is **not an independent slam convention**. It is a continuation of:

- Roman Key Card Blackwood (RKCB)
- Kickback
- Redwood
- Minorwood
- Other key-card asking methods

---

# When It Applies

Spiral Scan begins only after:

- A trump suit has been agreed.
- The partnership has completed a Key Card Ask.
- The response confirms that no key cards are missing.

Typical auction:

```text
1♠
3♠
4NT
5♣
5NT
```

The **5NT** bid starts the Spiral Scan.

---

# Basic Principle

Rather than simply asking:

> "How many kings do you have?"

the asker requests information about **specific side kings**.

The responder shows kings in a predetermined order using **step responses**.

This gives the partnership much more accurate information when deciding between a small slam and a grand slam.

---

# Typical Response Structure

One common agreement is:

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
|First step|Lowest-ranking side king|
|Second step|Next side king|
|Third step|Next side king|
|Fourth step|Highest-ranking side king|

The exact order must be agreed by the partnership.

---

# Example

Auction:

```text
1♥
3♥
4NT
5♦
5NT
6♣
```

The **6♣** response indicates possession of the first king in the agreed sequence.

Further bids may continue the scan if additional information is required.

---

# Why Use Spiral Scan?

Traditional King Asks tell the partnership **how many kings** partner holds.

Spiral Scan identifies **which kings** partner holds.

This distinction is critical because:

- Some kings are valuable opposite shortness.
- Others may duplicate wasted values.
- Knowing the exact king locations improves grand slam decisions.

---

# Advantages

- More precise than a simple King Ask.
- Excellent for grand slam investigation.
- Integrates naturally with RKCB and Kickback.
- Identifies specific side kings.

---

# Disadvantages

- Rarely played outside advanced partnerships.
- Memory intensive.
- Requires detailed partnership agreements.
- Usually unnecessary below expert level.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Which bid initiates Spiral Scan.
- Order of king responses.
- Whether queens are also shown.
- Whether scanning stops after the first king.
- Treatment after interference.

---

# Variations

Several published methods exist.

Partnerships differ on:

- The order in which kings are shown.
- Whether responses show one king or multiple kings.
- Whether queens or other honors are included.
- Continuations after all kings have been identified.

Always document the partnership's exact structure.

# Summary

**Spiral Scan** is an advanced continuation of **Key Card Blackwood** used after all key cards have been accounted for. Instead of merely counting kings, it identifies **which specific side kings** partner holds through an agreed sequence of responses, allowing the partnership to judge grand slam prospects with much greater accuracy.
