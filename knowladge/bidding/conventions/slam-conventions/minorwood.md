# Minorwood

## Purpose

**Minorwood** is a **Roman Key Card Blackwood (RKCB)** variation used when the agreed trump suit is a **minor**.

Instead of using **4NT** as the key-card ask, the bid of:

- **4♣** over agreed clubs, or
- **4♦** over agreed diamonds

asks partner for **key cards**.

The primary advantage is that the partnership saves valuable bidding space for investigating queens, kings, and grand slams.

---

# When It Applies

Minorwood is used only after:

- Clubs or diamonds have been agreed as the trump suit.
- The partnership is investigating slam.

Examples:

```text
1♦
3♦
4♦   ← Minorwood
```

or

```text
1♣
2♣
4♣   ← Minorwood
```

The jump to four of the agreed minor is **artificial**, not a sign-off.

---

# Key Cards

Minorwood uses the same five key cards as **Roman Key Card Blackwood**:

- Four aces
- The king of the agreed trump suit

---

# Standard Responses (1430 Style)

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
|4♥|1 or 4 key cards|
|4♠|3 or 0 key cards|
|4NT|2 key cards without the trump queen|
|5♣ / 5♦*|2 key cards with the trump queen|

\* The actual response depends on the agreed trump suit and partnership methods.

Some partnerships instead play the **3014** response structure.

---

# Trump Queen Ask

After learning the number of key cards, the asker may make the **next available non-signoff bid** to ask about the **trump queen**.

Typical responses:

- Cheapest step = No trump queen
- Next step = Trump queen
- Higher steps = Trump queen plus side king(s)

Exact responses depend on partnership agreement.

---

# Example 1

```text
1♦
3♦
4♦   Minorwood
4♥   1 or 4 key cards
```

The partnership continues slam investigation.

---

# Example 2

```text
1♣
2♣
4♣   Minorwood
4NT  Two key cards, no trump queen
```

Responder has shown two key cards but denies the club queen.

---

# Advantages

- Saves bidding space compared with 4NT.
- Especially effective with minor-suit slams.
- Allows more room for queen and king asks.
- Integrates naturally with RKCB methods.

---

# Disadvantages

- Requires partnership agreement.
- Uses four of the agreed minor artificially.
- May interfere with natural sign-off auctions.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Whether Minorwood applies after every agreed minor fit.
- Whether responses follow **1430** or **3014**.
- Trump queen ask structure.
- King ask continuations.
- Whether opener or responder may initiate Minorwood.
- Interaction with Kickback and Redwood.

---

# Variations

## 1430 Minorwood

Uses standard 1430 RKCB responses.

---

## 3014 Minorwood

Uses the traditional 3014 RKCB response structure.

---

## Limited Minorwood

Some partnerships restrict Minorwood to auctions below game or only after an explicit minor-suit raise.
