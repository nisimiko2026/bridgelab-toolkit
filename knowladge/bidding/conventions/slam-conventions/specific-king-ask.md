# Specific King Ask

## Purpose

A **Specific King Ask (SKA)** is an advanced slam-investigation method used **after all key cards have been accounted for**.

Rather than asking:

> "How many side kings do you have?"

the asker requests information about **one specific side king**.

This enables the partnership to judge whether a **grand slam** is practical when the success depends on a particular king.

Specific King Asks are found primarily in advanced relay systems and sophisticated Roman Key Card Blackwood continuations.

---

# When It Applies

A Specific King Ask is used only when:

- A trump suit has been agreed.
- A Key Card Ask has been completed.
- The partnership knows that no key cards are missing.
- The location of a particular side king will determine the final contract.

Typical sequence:

```text
1♠
3♠
4NT
5♣
5NT
```

After the general king inquiry or another agreed continuation, the asker may inquire about a **specific king**.

The exact asking bid is determined by partnership agreement.

---

# What Is Asked?

The asker is interested in one particular side king, for example:

- ♣K
- ♦K
- ♥K

The agreed responses indicate whether responder holds that specific king.

---

# Typical Response Methods

## Step Responses

A common style is:

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
|1st Step|Does not hold the requested king|
|2nd Step|Holds the requested king|
|Higher Steps|Requested king plus additional feature (partnership agreement)|

---

## Suit Responses

Some partnerships use the suit itself to confirm possession.

Example:

```text
6♦
```

asks specifically about the ♦K.

Responses distinguish:

- Holds the ♦K
- Does not hold the ♦K

The exact structure is partnership-dependent.

---

# Example

```text
1♥
3♥
4NT
5♦
5NT
6♣
```

The **6♣** bid is an agreed ask for the **club king**.

Responder:

- 6♦ = No club king
- 6♥ = Club king held

(Example agreement only.)

---

# Why Use a Specific King Ask?

A general King Ask may reveal that partner has **one side king**, but not **which** one.

For example:

```
Dummy
♠ AKQJ5
♥ A74
♦ AQ5
♣ Q3

Partner
?????
```

If the partnership needs the **♦K** for a grand slam, knowing that partner merely has "one king" is insufficient.

A Specific King Ask provides that information.

---

# Advantages

- Identifies the exact side king required for a grand slam.
- More precise than a general King Ask.
- Avoids unnecessary grand slam attempts.
- Integrates well with advanced relay methods.

---

# Disadvantages

- Rarely standardized.
- Requires extensive partnership agreements.
- Usually unnecessary outside expert partnerships.
- Consumes valuable bidding space.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Which bid initiates the ask.
- Which king is being requested.
- Response structure.
- Whether additional honors are shown.
- Continuations after interference.

---

# Variations

Different systems employ different approaches:

- Relay-based Specific King Ask
- Precision methods
- Blue Team Club methods
- Symmetric Relay continuations

Some methods incorporate Specific King Asks within **Spiral Scan**, while others treat them as separate continuations.

# Summary

A **Specific King Ask** is an advanced continuation of a **key-card inquiry** that requests information about **one particular side king** rather than the total number of kings. It is used primarily in expert partnerships when the possession of a specific king determines whether a grand slam is likely to succeed.
