# Roman 2♦

## Bridge Insight

**The Roman 2♦ opening is a strong artificial bid used to describe highly distributional hands that do not fit comfortably into traditional strong opening structures. It allows opener to show exceptional playing strength while preserving bidding space for accurate game and slam investigation.**

---

# Overview

The **Roman 2♦** opening is an artificial strong opening used in various **Roman**, **Roman Club**, and related bidding systems.

Unlike the **Multi 2♦**, which is primarily a weak opening, the Roman 2♦ generally promises a strong hand, most often:

- A strong one-suited hand.
- A powerful three-suited hand.
- A hand unsuitable for a natural opening.

The exact meaning depends upon the partnership's chosen Roman system.

---

# Objectives

Roman 2♦ enables the partnership to:

- Describe strong distributional hands.
- Preserve bidding space.
- Differentiate strong hands from natural openings.
- Improve slam investigation.
- Maintain a consistent artificial opening structure.

---

# When to Use

Typical auction

```text
2♦
```

Artificial.

Usually forcing.

---

# Requirements

Typical hand characteristics:

- Strong playing strength.
- Usually eight or more playing tricks.
- Distribution unsuitable for a normal one-level opening.
- Often highly unbalanced.

The exact requirements depend on the Roman system played.

---

# Opening Structure

Typical Roman meanings include one of the following:

### Strong One-Suited Hand

Usually a long solid or semi-solid suit with game-going values.

---

### Strong Three-Suited Hand

Often a hand with shortness in one suit and strength suitable for game.

---

### Other Strong Artificial Hands

Some Roman systems assign additional meanings to the opening.

Partnership agreement is essential.

# Responding

Responses vary considerably.

Common methods include:

### Waiting Response

Responder makes the cheapest available bid.

Purpose:

Allow opener to clarify the hand.

---

### Positive Suit Response

Responder shows:

- Good suit.
- Useful controls.
- Slam interest.

---

### Notrump Response

Artificial inquiry.

Requests additional information.

---

Detailed continuations should be defined by partnership agreement.

---

# Partnership Agreements

The partnership should define:

- Exact meaning of 2♦.
- Minimum playing strength.
- Distribution requirements.
- Waiting responses.
- Positive responses.
- Continuations after interference.

These agreements should appear on the convention card.

---

# System Variations

Common Roman treatments include:

- Roman Club.
- Blue Club.
- Roman Precision variants.
- Modified Roman systems.

Each uses slightly different meanings.

---

# Advantages

Roman 2♦:

- Describes powerful distributional hands.
- Preserves bidding space.
- Improves slam exploration.
- Distinguishes unusual strong hands.
- Fits naturally into Roman systems.

---

# Limitations

Roman 2♦:

- Not standardized.
- Requires detailed partnership agreements.
- Different Roman systems assign different meanings.
- Rare outside Roman-based systems.

# Expert Thinking

Roman systems emphasize **playing strength and distribution** over raw HCP.

Roman 2♦ allows opener to distinguish hands that deserve immediate force but are too distributional for a natural opening.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing Roman 2♦ with Multi 2♦.
- Forgetting partnership response structure.
- Opening with insufficient playing strength.
- Mixing Roman and non-Roman agreements.

---

# Best Practices

- Define waiting responses.
- Practice continuations.
- Record agreements on the convention card.

# Expert Tip

**Roman 2♦ is not a universal convention.**

Unlike Stayman or Blackwood, there is no single standard meaning. Always treat the opening as part of the complete Roman bidding system rather than as an independent convention.
