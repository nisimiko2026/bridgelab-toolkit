# Symmetric Relay

## Purpose

**Symmetric Relay** is one of the most sophisticated relay bidding methods in bridge.

It uses a series of **artificial relay bids** to describe a hand with exceptional precision, allowing the partnership to identify:

- Exact distribution
- Strength range
- Suit lengths
- Controls
- Slam potential

Unlike natural bidding systems, one player (the **Captain**) asks questions while the other (the **Describer**) answers according to a predefined step structure.

---

# Philosophy

Symmetric Relay is based on two roles:

### Captain

The Captain:

- Controls the auction
- Chooses the final contract
- Uses relay bids to request information

### Describer

The Describer:

- Answers each relay
- Follows predefined step responses
- Never makes judgment bids

---

# Basic Structure

Typical relay sequence:

```text
1♣
1♦   Relay
1♥   Step 1
1♠   Relay
2♣   Step 2
2♦   Relay
2♥   Step 3
```

Each response has a fixed meaning.

The cheapest available bid is usually the next relay.

---

# Step Responses

Information is disclosed gradually.

Typical order:

1. Strength
2. Shape
3. Suit lengths
4. Controls
5. Honor location
6. Slam features

Different relay systems define the exact order differently.

---

# Symmetric Principle

The method is called **Symmetric** because identical response structures are used whenever possible.

The same step sequence applies regardless of:

- Opening bid
- Suit
- Direction

This greatly reduces memory requirements compared with older relay systems.

---

# Shape Description

After establishing strength, responder usually describes:

- Balanced hands
- Single-suited hands
- Two-suited hands
- Three-suited hands
- Exact suit lengths

Eventually the Captain may know all 13 cards.

---

# Slam Investigation

Once shape is known, relays continue to determine:

- Controls
- Key cards
- Queen location
- Kings
- Specific honors

The Captain then chooses the final contract.

---

# Advantages

- Extremely accurate hand description
- Excellent slam bidding
- Efficient use of bidding space
- Standardized response structure
- Applicable to many artificial systems

---

# Disadvantages

- Very memory intensive
- Requires expert partnership agreements
- Vulnerable to interference
- Not suitable for casual partnerships

---

# Typical Applications

Symmetric Relay is commonly associated with:

- Strong Club systems
- Precision derivatives
- Relay Club systems
- MOSCITO
- Viking Club
- Other advanced artificial systems

# Summary

**Symmetric Relay** is a highly structured relay bidding method in which one player becomes the **Captain** and the other the **Describer**. Through a series of artificial relay bids and standardized step responses, the partnership exchanges precise information about strength, distribution, and controls, making it one of the most accurate methods for game and slam bidding.
