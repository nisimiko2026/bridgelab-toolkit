# Stayman Convention

## Bridge Insight

**Stayman is the cornerstone of modern Notrump bidding. Its primary purpose is not to find any fit, but to discover an eight-card major-suit fit that is often superior to playing in Notrump.**

---

# Overview

The **Stayman Convention** is an artificial 2♣ response to partner's 1NT (or sometimes 2NT) opening that asks opener whether they hold a four-card major suit.

Although originally designed simply to locate a major-suit fit, modern Stayman also serves as the foundation for many advanced conventions, including Puppet Stayman, Smolen, Garbage Stayman, Five-Card Stayman, and Two-Way Stayman.

Stayman is one of the most widely played conventions in bridge and is considered essential in virtually every natural bidding system.

---

# Objectives

Stayman enables the partnership to:

- Discover an eight-card major-suit fit.
- Choose between a major-suit contract and Notrump.
- Improve game and slam decisions.
- Begin a structured exploration of responder's shape.
- Create a foundation for advanced Stayman treatments.

---

# When to Use

Typical auction:

```text
1NT — 2♣
```

or

```text
2NT — 3♣
```

Responder normally has:

- At least one four-card major.
- Invitational or stronger values.

(Some partnerships extend Stayman to weak hands using Garbage Stayman.)

---

# Requirements

### Opener

- Balanced Notrump opening.

### Responder

Normally:

- One or both four-card majors.
- Interest in locating a major-suit fit.

---

# Convention Structure

## Basic Responses

| Auction | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| 1NT – 2♣ – 2♦ | No four-card major |
| 1NT – 2♣ – 2♥ | Four hearts (may also hold four spades) |
| 1NT – 2♣ – 2♠ | Four spades only |

Some partnerships use different agreements after strong Notrump openings.

---

# Complete Auction Examples

## Example 1

```text
West      East

1NT       2♣
2♥        4♥
```

Responder discovers an eight-card heart fit and places the contract in game.

---

## Example 2

```text
West      East

1NT       2♣
2♦        3NT
```

No major fit exists, so responder chooses the natural Notrump game.

---

## Example 3

```text
West      East

1NT       2♣
2♠        4♠
```

Responder finds the spade fit and avoids playing in Notrump.

---

# Continuations

The Stayman response is only the beginning of the auction.

### After 2♦

Responder may:

- Bid a four-card major.
- Invite with Notrump.
- Bid game in Notrump.
- Show an unbalanced hand.
- Use Smolen (if played).

---

### After 2♥

Responder may:

- Raise Hearts.
- Bid Notrump.
- Bid another suit.
- Explore slam.

---

### After 2♠

Responder follows the same principles.

The continuation depends on:

- Fit.
- Strength.
- Distribution.
- Partnership agreements.

---

# Partnership Agreements

The partnership should agree:

- Is Stayman always forcing?
- Is Garbage Stayman played?
- Is Five-Card Stayman played?
- Is Smolen included?
- Is Puppet Stayman used after 2NT?
- Does opener bypass hearts with both majors?
- How are continuations handled after interference?

These agreements should be recorded on the convention card.

# Expert Thinking

Expert players use Stayman to exchange **shape information**, not merely to ask about majors.

Even when responder expects to play in Notrump, discovering a 4-4 major fit frequently produces an easier and safer game contract.

Experienced partnerships also think several bids ahead, planning the continuations before making the initial 2♣ response.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Using Stayman without a four-card major (unless playing a specific variation).
- Forgetting continuation agreements.
- Missing a major fit after interference.
- Automatically choosing Notrump.
- Treating Stayman as only a beginner convention.

---

# Best Practices

- Discuss continuations thoroughly.
- Record all partnership agreements.
- Learn Stayman before learning its extensions.
- Plan the entire auction, not just the first bid.
- Re-evaluate after opener's response.

# Player's Checklist

Before bidding Stayman ask:

- Do I have a four-card major?
- Would I prefer a major-suit contract?
- Am I invitational or stronger?
- Do we play Garbage Stayman?
- Do I know all continuations?

# AI Summary

**Purpose**

Locate an eight-card major-suit fit after a Notrump opening.

**Artificial Bid**

```
1NT — 2♣
```

**Primary Responses**

- 2♦ = No major
- 2♥ = Hearts
- 2♠ = Spades

**Main Decision**

After opener replies, responder decides whether to:

- Play in a major.
- Continue in Notrump.
- Invite game.
- Explore slam.

---

# Expert Tip

**Stayman is a conversation, not a question.**

The 2♣ bid starts an information exchange that often continues for several rounds. Strong partnerships think beyond opener's first answer and plan the entire auction from the beginning.

---

# Memory Aid

**Stayman = Find the Major First**

Remember:

- Search for an eight-card major fit before settling in Notrump.
- Learn the continuation structure as carefully as the initial responses.
- Advanced Stayman variations build upon the same fundamental principle.
- Successful Stayman auctions depend on partnership agreements as much as on card strength.
