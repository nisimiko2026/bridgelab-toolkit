# SOS Redouble

## Bridge Insight

**The SOS Redouble transforms a dangerous doubled contract into a search for safety. Instead of accepting a likely penalty, responder asks partner to escape to the best available suit, prioritizing survival over scoring.**

---

# Overview

The **SOS Redouble** is a conventional redouble used after the opponents have doubled a notrump contract for penalties.

Rather than showing strength, the redouble asks partner to **run to a suit**. The partnership attempts to escape from a heavily penalized notrump contract into a safer suit contract.

The convention is widely played after:

```text
1NT (Pass) Pass (Double)
Redouble
```

but may also apply after other doubled notrump contracts, depending on partnership agreement.

---

# Objectives

SOS Redouble enables the partnership to:

- Escape from a doubled Notrump contract.
- Avoid severe penalty scores.
- Find the safest suit contract.
- Improve part-score results.
- Survive highly competitive auctions.

---

# When to Use

Typical auction:

```text
West      North      East      South

1NT       Pass       Pass      Double
Redouble
```

The redouble is **artificial** and requests opener to bid the lowest four-card (or longer) suit.

---

# Requirements

Responder typically has:

- Very weak hand.
- No expectation of making the doubled Notrump contract.
- Distribution suitable for playing in a suit.
- Little or no defensive strength.

SOS Redouble is based on **survival**, not strength.

---

# Convention Structure

### Redouble

Artificial.

Meaning:

> "Please take me out of Notrump."

Opener is expected to bid the lowest suitable suit according to partnership agreement.

---

# Opener's Responsibilities

After an SOS Redouble, opener should:

- Bid the lowest four-card or longer suit.
- Continue describing the hand if responder bids again.
- Prioritize finding a playable fit.

The exact escape method should be agreed by the partnership.

---

# Partnership Agreements

The partnership should define:

- Which suit opener bids first.
- Whether four-card or five-card suits are required.
- Whether responder may correct.
- Methods after further interference.
- Whether SOS applies after 2NT openings.

These agreements should appear on the convention card.

---

# System Variations

Common treatments include:

### Standard SOS

Opener bids the lowest available suit.

---

### Relay SOS

Responder may continue correcting until the best fit is found.

---

### Extended SOS

Applies after higher-level Notrump openings.

---

# Advantages

SOS Redouble:

- Escapes dangerous penalty doubles.
- Locates safer contracts.
- Reduces large negative scores.
- Uses existing bidding space efficiently.

---

# Limitations

SOS Redouble:

- Requires detailed partnership agreements.
- May still result in a poor contract.
- Gives opponents information about distribution.
- Less effective with balanced weak hands.

# Expert Thinking

The SOS Redouble reflects an important bridge principle:

**A poor suit contract often scores better than a heavily doubled Notrump contract.**

Experts are willing to play in a 4-3 fit—or even a 4-2 fit—if it avoids a substantial penalty in Notrump.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Treating the redouble as strength.
- Forgetting opener's escape obligations.
- Escaping with unsuitable distribution.
- Ignoring partnership agreements.
- Passing the doubled contract with an unsuitable hand.

---

# Best Practices

- Use SOS only with genuinely weak hands.
- Agree the escape structure in advance.
- Practice common rescue auctions.
- Discuss continuations after interference.
- Record agreements on the convention card.

# Expert Tip

**SOS stands for "Save Our Ship."**

The convention is used only when remaining in the doubled Notrump contract is likely to be disastrous. The partnership's objective is not to find the perfect fit, but simply to reach a contract with better survival prospects.

---

# Memory Aid

**SOS Redouble = "Please Rescue Me."**

Remember:

- It is **not** a strength-showing redouble.
- It is used after a **penalty double of a Notrump contract**.
- Opener is expected to remove the contract to a suit.
- The exact escape method is determined by partnership agreement.
- The convention prioritizes avoiding heavy penalties over finding the ideal fit.
