# Puppet Stayman Convention

## Bridge Insight

**Puppet Stayman extends the Stayman concept to strong Notrump auctions by locating five-card and four-card major suits while preserving the partnership's ability to reach the best game or slam contract.**

---

# Overview

**Puppet Stayman** is an artificial convention used primarily after a **strong 2NT opening** (and in many partnerships after a strong 1NT opening). It allows responder to ask opener about **five-card majors first**, and if none exist, about **four-card majors**.

Unlike standard Stayman, Puppet Stayman is designed to cope with the wider range and stronger hands found in 2NT auctions, where major-suit fits and slam investigation become increasingly important.

---

# Objectives

Puppet Stayman enables the partnership to:

- Discover 5-3 major-suit fits.
- Discover 4-4 major-suit fits.
- Improve game selection.
- Improve slam exploration.
- Describe balanced hands accurately.
- Preserve bidding space.

---

# When to Use

Typical auction:

```text
2NT — 3♣
```

Many partnerships also play Puppet Stayman after:

```text
1NT — 2♣
```

when using a strong 1NT range.

---

# Requirements

### Opener

- Strong Notrump opening.
- Balanced hand.

### Responder

Normally:

- Game-going values or stronger.
- Interest in finding a major-suit fit.
- Balanced or semi-balanced hand.

Some partnerships permit weaker hands.

---

# Convention Structure

One common response structure is:

| Auction | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| 2NT – 3♣ – 3♦ | No five-card major; may have one or both four-card majors |
| 2NT – 3♣ – 3♥ | Five hearts |
| 2NT – 3♣ – 3♠ | Five spades |
| 2NT – 3♣ – 3NT | Five-card major(s) denied, no four-card major (partnership agreement) |

Many alternative structures exist.

---

# Continuations

The continuation depends entirely on opener's reply.

## After 3♦

Responder continues by showing:

- Four-card hearts.
- Four-card spades.
- Neither major.
- Slam interest.

This sequence searches for 4-4 fits after five-card majors have been denied.

---

## After 3♥

Responder decides whether to:

- Raise Hearts.
- Bid 3NT.
- Begin slam investigation.

---

## After 3♠

Responder follows the same principles.

---

## After 3NT

Responder normally places the contract unless holding exceptional slam values.

---

# Partnership Agreements

The partnership should agree:

- Does Puppet Stayman apply after 1NT?
- Which response structure is used?
- Does opener show five-card majors before four-card majors?
- How are both four-card majors shown?
- What happens after interference?
- How are slam continuations handled?

These agreements should be recorded on the convention card.

---

# System Variations

Common variations include:

- Standard Puppet Stayman
- Muppet Stayman
- Five-Card Puppet
- Modified Puppet Stayman
- Puppet after Strong Club systems

Different systems use different response structures.

---

# Expert Thinking

The greatest strength of Puppet Stayman is **information economy**.

Instead of immediately describing every major suit, opener first answers the most important question:

> "Do I have a five-card major?"

Only after that question is answered does the partnership investigate four-card majors.

This sequencing allows strong Notrump auctions to remain efficient while preserving room for slam exploration.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing Puppet Stayman with ordinary Stayman.
- Forgetting the agreed response structure.
- Ignoring 5-3 fits.
- Bidding Notrump too quickly.
- Using Puppet without partnership agreement.

---

# Best Practices

- Memorize the complete response structure.
- Discuss all continuations.
- Record agreements on the convention card.
- Practice common auctions.
- Learn Puppet Stayman before advanced variants.

# Expert Tip

**Think of Puppet Stayman as a two-stage search.**

Stage 1 identifies five-card majors.

Stage 2 investigates four-card majors only if Stage 1 fails.

Keeping those two stages separate makes the response structure much easier to remember and avoids confusion with standard Stayman.

---

# Memory Aid

**Puppet Stayman = "Five First, Four Second."**

Remember:

- Search first for a 5-3 fit.
- If none exists, search for a 4-4 fit.
- Preserve bidding space for game and slam decisions.
- Learn the continuation structure as thoroughly as the initial responses.
- Puppet Stayman is an extension of Stayman, designed specifically for strong Notrump auctions where accurate major-suit discovery is essential.
