# South African Texas

## Bridge Insight

**South African Texas is an extension of the Texas Transfer concept that provides additional flexibility for responder when holding long major suits after partner opens 1NT. By distinguishing between direct game bids and transfer sequences, the partnership can investigate slam while still reaching the correct game contract efficiently.**

---

# Overview

**South African Texas** is a variation of the **Texas Transfer** convention used after a natural **1NT opening**.

Like standard Texas Transfers, responder uses an artificial transfer to show a long major suit. However, the meanings of the transfer bids are modified so that direct game bids remain available for other purposes, typically slam investigation.

The convention is played mainly by expert partnerships and is not universally standardized.

---

# Objectives

South African Texas enables the partnership to:

- Transfer into a major at the game level.
- Preserve bidding space for slam exploration.
- Distinguish sign-off sequences from stronger hands.
- Improve contract selection.
- Reduce ambiguity in game-going auctions.

---

# When to Use

After a natural:

```text
1NT
```

or, by partnership agreement,

```text
2NT
```

---

# Requirements

Responder typically holds:

- A six-card or longer major.
- Game-going values or better.
- Interest in playing in the major suit.
- Possible slam interest.

---

# Convention Structure

Unlike standard Texas Transfers, South African Texas reverses the meanings of the direct game bids and the transfer bids.

A common treatment is:

```text
1NT – 4♣
```

Transfer to **Hearts**.

```text
1NT – 4♦
```

Transfer to **Spades**.

Direct major-suit game bids:

```text
1NT – 4♥
```

or

```text
1NT – 4♠
```

are reserved for stronger hands or slam-oriented auctions, according to partnership agreement.

**Note:** Several variations exist. Partnerships must agree the exact meanings.

# Responder's Continuations

After opener accepts the transfer, responder may:

- Pass.
- Continue with cue bidding.
- Use Roman Key Card Blackwood.
- Begin slam investigation.

The continuation depends upon partnership agreements.

---

# Partnership Agreements

The partnership should define:

- Exact meanings of 4♣ and 4♦.
- Meaning of direct 4♥ and 4♠.
- Whether the convention applies after 2NT.
- Slam continuation methods.
- Responses after interference.

These agreements should appear on the convention card.

---

# System Variations

Common treatments include:

- Standard South African Texas.

Different partnerships assign different meanings to the direct major-suit game bids.

# Limitations

South African Texas:

- Requires explicit partnership agreements.

# Expert Thinking

South African Texas is designed for partnerships that wish to preserve as much bidding space as possible for constructive auctions.

By separating simple sign-offs from stronger game-going hands, the convention provides greater flexibility when exploring slam while still allowing efficient transfers into the major suits.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Confusing the convention with standard Texas Transfers.
- Forgetting the agreed meanings of direct game bids.
- Mixing partnership treatments.
- Using the convention without explicit agreement.
