# Texas Transfers

## Bridge Insight

**Texas Transfers allow the partnership to reach a major-suit game while keeping the strong Notrump hand as declarer. Unlike Jacoby Transfers, Texas immediately commits the partnership to game.**

---

# Overview

The **Texas Transfer Convention** is an artificial response to a **1NT** or **2NT** opening that transfers opener into a major-suit game.

Responder bids:

- **4♦** to transfer opener to **4♥**
- **4♥** to transfer opener to **4♠**

Unlike **Jacoby Transfers**, Texas Transfers are **game-forcing**. They are used when responder already knows the partnership belongs in game and wishes to place the contract efficiently.

Texas Transfers are particularly valuable because they keep the strong Notrump opener hidden from the defenders, protecting high-card strength from the opening lead.

---

# Objectives

Texas Transfers enable the partnership to:

- Reach a major-suit game immediately.
- Keep opener as declarer.
- Conceal opener's strength.
- Eliminate unnecessary bidding.
- Preserve bidding accuracy in game-going auctions.

---

# When to Use

Typical auctions:

```text
1NT — 4♦
4♥
```

Transfer to Hearts.

---

```text
1NT — 4♥
4♠
```

Transfer to Spades.

The convention is equally common after:

```text
2NT
```

---

# Requirements

### Opener

- Natural Notrump opening.
- Balanced hand.

### Responder

Normally:

- Six-card or longer major.
- Game-going values.

Some partnerships allow strong five-card suits.

# Convention Structure

## Basic Transfers

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| 4♦ | Transfer to Hearts |
| 4♥ | Transfer to Spades |

Opener normally completes the transfer.

---

# Jacoby vs Texas Transfers

| Jacoby | Texas |
|---------|--------|
| Begins below game | Immediately commits to game |
| Any strength | Game-going values |
| Describes strength later | Final contract usually known immediately |
| Flexible | Fast arrival |

## Slam Interest

Responder may continue with:

- Cue Bidding.
- Roman Key Card Blackwood.
- Kickback (if played).
- Control Bidding.

The transfer establishes the trump suit before slam investigation begins.

---

## Quantitative Decisions

Some partnerships distinguish between:

- Immediate Texas (major game).
- Quantitative Notrump raises.

The meanings should be agreed before play.

---

# Why Use Texas Instead of Jacoby?

Texas Transfers are preferable when:

- Game is certain.
- No further description is needed.
- The partnership wants to conceal opener's hand.
- Bidding space is no longer important.

Jacoby Transfers are preferable when responder still needs to describe strength or shape.

---

# Partnership Agreements

The partnership should agree:

- Are Texas Transfers played after both 1NT and 2NT?
- What minimum suit length is required?
- Are strong five-card majors acceptable?
- How are continuations after interference handled?
- Are slam continuations standardized?
- May opener ever refuse the transfer? (Normally no.)

These agreements should appear on the convention card.

---

# System Variations

Common treatments include:

- Standard Texas Transfers.
- Texas after Strong Club auctions.
- Extended Texas.
- Texas combined with South African Texas.

Each variation has its own agreements.

---

# Expert Thinking

Experts recognize that once game is certain, **describing the hand further often provides more information to the defenders than to partner.**

Texas Transfers therefore emphasize:

- Declarer placement.
- Simplicity.
- Information concealment.

The convention wins not because it exchanges information, but because it deliberately avoids unnecessary information exchange.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Using Texas with invitational values.
- Confusing Texas with Jacoby Transfers.
- Forgetting the transfer direction.
- Using Texas with insufficient trump length.
- Continuing unnecessarily after reaching game.

---

# Best Practices

- Use Texas only when game is already assured.
- Keep opener as declarer whenever possible.
- Learn when Jacoby Transfers are superior.
- Discuss slam continuations in advance.
- Record partnership agreements on the convention card.

# Expert Tip

**Texas Transfers answer a different question than Jacoby Transfers.**

Jacoby asks:

> "Let's investigate."

Texas says:

> "We already know where we're going."

Use Texas when the destination is known, and Jacoby when further hand description is valuable.

---

# Memory Aid

**Texas = Transfer Directly to Game**

Remember:

- Bid one step below your intended major.
- Opener completes the transfer.
- Game is already assured.
- Opener remains declarer.
- Texas Transfers trade bidding space for simplicity and information concealment, making them one of the most efficient conventions in modern Notrump auctions.
