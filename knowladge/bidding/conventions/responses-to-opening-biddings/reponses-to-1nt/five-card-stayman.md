# Five-Card Stayman

## Bridge Insight

**Five-Card Stayman extends the traditional Stayman convention to modern notrump systems in which opener may hold a five-card major. It enables the partnership to locate both 4-4 and 5-3 major-suit fits while preserving the advantages of a natural 1NT opening.**

---

# Overview

**Five-Card Stayman** is a variation of the Stayman convention used after a natural **1NT opening** when the partnership allows opener to hold a **five-card major**.

Unlike standard Stayman, which assumes opener denies a five-card major, Five-Card Stayman allows responder to determine whether opener holds:

- A four-card major.
- A five-card major.
- Both majors.
- Neither major.

The exact response structure varies according to partnership agreement.

---

# Objectives

Five-Card Stayman enables the partnership to:

- Locate 4-4 major-suit fits.
- Locate 5-3 major-suit fits.
- Improve game selection.
- Improve slam investigation.
- Avoid inferior Notrump contracts.

---

- Both majors.

Many response structures are in use.

# Five-Card Stayman versus Standard Stayman

| Five-Card Stayman | Standard Stayman |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Opener may have a five-card major | Opener normally denies a five-card major |
| Finds 5-3 fits | Primarily finds 4-4 fits |
| More descriptive | Simpler structure |

# Expert Thinking

Modern notrump systems frequently permit opener to hold a five-card major.

Without Five-Card Stayman, many excellent 5-3 major fits would remain undiscovered.

The convention increases the partnership's ability to choose the best game and slam contracts while preserving the efficiency of a natural 1NT opening.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Playing Five-Card Stayman with standard Stayman agreements.
- Forgetting the agreed response meanings.
- Assuming every 2♥ or 2♠ response shows exactly four cards.
- Ignoring the possibility of a 5-3 fit.
- Mixing partnership methods.

---

# Best Practices

- Practice common continuations.
- Distinguish four-card and five-card majors clearly.
- Record agreements on the convention card.
- Review interference auctions.
