# Crawling Stayman

## Bridge Insight

**Crawling Stayman extends the idea of Garbage Stayman by allowing the partnership to reach the safest possible part-score through a series of controlled corrections. Rather than simply escaping from 1NT, responder gradually "crawls" toward the best-fitting suit while revealing as little unnecessary information as possible.**

---

# Overview

**Crawling Stayman** is a variation of the Stayman convention used after a natural **1NT opening** when responder holds a very weak distributional hand.

Like Garbage Stayman, the objective is **not** to investigate game but to escape from Notrump into the safest suit contract.

Unlike Garbage Stayman, responder may continue bidding after opener's response in order to locate a superior fit.

---

# Objectives

Crawling Stayman enables the partnership to:

- Escape from 1NT.
- Locate the safest part-score.
- Improve contracts with weak distributional hands.
- Find 4-3 or better fits.
- Minimize losses on weak deals.

---

# When to Use

Typical auction:

```text
1NT      2♣
```

Responder intends to escape into a suit contract rather than invite game.

---

# Requirements

Typical responder:

- 0–7 HCP.
- Distributional hand.
- Little or no game interest.
- Short clubs.
- Tolerance for Diamonds and/or the majors.

The convention relies more on shape than on high-card strength.

---

# Convention Structure

Responder begins with:

```text
1NT      2♣
```

Stayman.

Opener replies normally:

```text
2♦
```

No four-card major.

```text
2♥
```

Four Hearts.

```text
2♠
```

Four Spades.

Responder may then continue to the preferred strain.

---

# Responder's Continuations

Depending on opener's reply, responder may:

- Pass.
- Correct to another suit.
- Continue toward the safest contract.

The exact correction structure is determined by partnership agreement.

---

# Hand Evaluation

Typical hands feature:

- Very weak values.
- Distributional shape.
- Unsuitable for Notrump.
- Several possible part-score contracts.

Shape is considerably more important than HCP.

---

# Partnership Agreements

The partnership should define:

- Correction sequences.
- Whether 4-3 fits are acceptable.
- Priority of Diamonds versus majors.
- Continuations after interference.
- Maximum responder strength.

These agreements should appear on the convention card.

---

# System Variations

Common treatments include:

- Standard Crawling Stayman.
- Garbage Stayman.
- Weak Stayman.
- Extended Crawling methods.

Partnership agreements determine the exact continuation structure.

---

# Crawling Stayman versus Garbage Stayman

| Crawling Stayman | Garbage Stayman |
|------------------|-----------------|
| Multiple correction options | Usually one correction |
| Searches for the safest fit | Simple escape mechanism |
| More flexible | Simpler structure |
| Greater partnership agreement required | Easier to remember |

---

# Advantages

Crawling Stayman:

- Improves part-score contracts.
- Locates better fits.
- Avoids weak Notrump contracts.
- Makes excellent use of distribution.

---

# Limitations

Crawling Stayman:

- More complex than Garbage Stayman.
- Requires detailed agreements.
- Rarely needed with balanced weak hands.
- May disclose additional information.

---

# Expert Thinking

Experts recognize that **part-score bridge is often won by small improvements**.

Moving from 1NT to a comfortable 4-3 fit frequently gains several tricks, even without additional high-card strength.

Crawling Stayman provides responder with greater flexibility to locate that superior contract.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Using Crawling Stayman with invitational values.
- Forgetting correction sequences.
- Confusing it with Garbage Stayman.
- Ignoring distribution.
- Overcomplicating simple auctions.

---

# Best Practices

- Use only with genuinely weak hands.
- Prioritize safety over perfect fit.
- Practice correction sequences.
- Keep agreements simple.
- Record agreements on the convention card.

Escape from 1NT by gradually locating the safest suit contract.

**Method**

**Goal**

Reach the safest available part-score rather than investigate game.

---

# Expert Tip

**Crawling Stayman is about safety, not accuracy.**

The convention does not promise the perfect trump fit. Instead, it seeks the contract least likely to fail. Expert partnerships are willing to play in a 4-3 fit if it offers better prospects than struggling in 1NT.

---

# Memory Aid

**Crawling Stayman = "Escape One Step at a Time."**

Remember:

- It begins with a standard Stayman inquiry.
- The objective is to leave 1NT safely.
- Responder may continue correcting after opener's reply.
- Distribution is far more important than HCP.
- Clear partnership agreements are essential because continuation methods vary between systems.
