# Jacoby 2NT

## Bridge Insight

**Jacoby 2NT is a game-forcing raise of partner's major-suit opening. Instead of simply showing support, it begins a structured conversation about shortness, strength, and controls, allowing the partnership to judge game and slam accurately.**

---

# Overview

The **Jacoby 2NT Convention** is an artificial game-forcing response to a natural **1♥** or **1♠** opening.

Responder's **2NT** bid shows:

- At least four-card trump support.
- Game-forcing values.
- Interest in exploring slam.

Unlike a direct raise to game, Jacoby 2NT leaves room for opener to describe hand strength and distribution before the partnership commits to the final contract.

---

# Objectives

Jacoby 2NT enables the partnership to:

- Establish an immediate game force.
- Show strong trump support.
- Exchange information about shortness.
- Evaluate slam potential.
- Reach the best game or slam.

---

# When to Use

Typical auctions:

```text
1♥      2NT
```

```text
1♠      2NT
```

The response is artificial.

---

# Requirements

### Opener

- Natural opening of 1♥ or 1♠.

### Responder

Typically:

- Four or more trumps.
- Opening values or better.
- Game-forcing strength.

Many partnerships require 13+ HCP or equivalent playing strength.

---

# Convention Structure

After a Jacoby 2NT response, opener further describes the hand.

A common modern structure is:

| Opener's Rebid | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| New suit at the 3-level | Singleton or void (shortness) |
| Jump in a new suit | Good side suit (partnership agreement) |
| 3 of the agreed major | Minimum balanced hand |
| 3NT | Balanced hand with extra values |
| 4 of the agreed major | Minimum, no useful descriptive feature |

Partnerships differ significantly, so these meanings must be agreed in advance.

# Continuations

The Jacoby 2NT response establishes a game force but does not determine the final contract.

## Minimum Opening

Opener returns to:

```text
4♥

or

4♠
```

Responder usually passes unless holding exceptional slam values.

---

## Shortness Shown

Responder evaluates:

- Wasted values opposite the short suit.
- Controls.
- Distribution.
- Trump quality.

Cue bidding often follows.

---

## Extra Values

If opener shows additional strength:

- Cue Bidding.
- Roman Key Card Blackwood.
- Kickback.
- Control Bidding.

may all be appropriate.

---

# Shortness Evaluation

Shortness is one of the convention's greatest strengths.

Example:

Responder:

```
♠ AQJ8
♥ KQJ6
♦ K83
♣ A74
```

Opener:

```
♠ K109764
♥ A92
♦ —
♣ KQ863
```

After opener shows a diamond singleton, responder can accurately re-evaluate the hand. Diamond honors lose value, while club controls and trump support become more important.

|-------------|-------------|

| Four-card support | Usually three or more-card support |
| Slam interest possible | Usually no slam interest |
| Artificial | Natural |

|-------------|----------|

# Expert Thinking

Jacoby 2NT is fundamentally an **information-exchange convention**.

Responder has already answered two important questions:

- We have a trump fit.
- We belong in game.

The remaining question is:

> **"Should we stop in game or continue toward slam?"**

Everything after 2NT is directed toward answering that question.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Using Jacoby 2NT with only invitational values.
- Forgetting that the response is game forcing.
- Showing shortness inconsistently.
- Ignoring wasted values opposite a singleton.
- Confusing Jacoby 2NT with Jacoby Transfers.

---

# Best Practices

- Agree the rebid structure before playing.
- Evaluate controls rather than HCP alone.
- Reassess honor values opposite shortness.
- Practice cue-bid continuations.
- Record agreements on the convention card.

# Expert Tip

**Jacoby 2NT does not ask a question—it starts a conversation.**

Unlike conventions with fixed responses, Jacoby 2NT opens a structured exchange in which opener describes the hand and responder continually re-evaluates slam potential. The more accurately both partners assess controls, shortness, and fitting honors, the more successful their slam bidding will be.

---

# Memory Aid

**Jacoby 2NT = "Fit First, Describe Second."**

Remember:

- The trump fit is already established.
- Game is guaranteed.
- The remaining auction is devoted to describing hand features.
- Shortness, controls, and extra values are more important than raw HCP.
- Jacoby 2NT is one of the most effective conventions for exploring slams after a major-suit opening because it provides ample room for accurate hand evaluation before committing to the final contract.
