# Snapdragon Double

## Bridge Insight

**The Snapdragon Double is a competitive convention that shows a two-suited hand after both opponents have bid suits. Rather than simply competing, the doubler identifies support for partner's suit together with the remaining unbid suit, allowing the partnership to compete accurately despite limited bidding space.**

---

# Overview

The **Snapdragon Double** is a conventional double used in competitive auctions after:

- The opponents have bid two different suits.
- Partner has bid one suit.
- Exactly one suit remains unbid.

The double shows:

- Support for partner's suit.
- Length in the only unbid suit.
- Competitive values.

It is **not** a penalty double.

---

# Objectives

Snapdragon Double enables the partnership to:

- Show a two-suited competitive hand.
- Support partner without making an immediate raise.
- Introduce the remaining unbid suit.
- Compete accurately at a low level.
- Improve competitive judgment.

# Requirements

Typical hand:

- Opening values or good competitive values.
- Support for partner's suit (usually at least three cards).
- At least four cards in the unbid suit (many partnerships prefer five).
- Distribution suitable for competition.

---

# Convention Structure

Requirements:

- Partner has overcalled.
- Opponents have introduced a second suit.
- Exactly one suit remains unbid.

Double shows:

- Partner's suit.
- The remaining unbid suit.

# Advantages

Snapdragon Double:

- Efficiently describes two suits.
- Conserves bidding space.
- Improves competitive accuracy.
- Helps locate the best fit.
- Distinguishes simple raises from stronger distributional hands.

---

# Limitations

Snapdragon Double:

- Applies only in specific auctions.
- Requires partnership agreement.
- Can reveal useful information to the opponents.
- Less common than Negative Double.

# Expert Thinking

Snapdragon Double solves a common competitive problem.

After partner overcalls and the opponents introduce a second suit, there is often no convenient natural call to show both support for partner and interest in the remaining unbid suit.

The double communicates both pieces of information in a single call, allowing partner to judge the best strain accurately.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Using Snapdragon when more than one suit remains unbid.
- Treating the double as penalty.
- Forgetting the required support for partner's suit.
- Using balanced hands.
- Playing different partnership agreements.

---

# Best Practices

- Use only when exactly one suit remains unbid.
- Promise genuine support for partner.
- Promise real length in the remaining suit.
- Practice common competitive auctions.
- Record agreements on the convention card.

# Expert Tip

**Snapdragon Double is one of the most efficient descriptive doubles in competitive bidding.**

Instead of forcing partner to guess whether your priority is supporting their suit or introducing a second suit, the double communicates both messages simultaneously. Its effectiveness comes from its precise definition: it applies only when **exactly one suit remains unbid**, making the meaning unambiguous.
