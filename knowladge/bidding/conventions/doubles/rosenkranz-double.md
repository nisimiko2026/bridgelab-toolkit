# Rosenkranz Double

## Purpose

The **Rosenkranz Double** is a conventional treatment used after **partner has made a suit overcall** and the opponents continue bidding.

It serves two purposes:

- Shows **support** for partner's overcalled suit.
- Promises a **top honor** (usually the Ace or King) in that suit.

It also provides valuable **lead-direction** information if the opponents eventually declare the contract.

---

# When It Applies

A typical auction is:

```text
West     North     East     South

1♦        1♠        2♦        Double
```

South's **Double** is **not** for penalties.

Instead, it shows:

- Support for partner's spades.
- The ♠A or ♠K (partnership agreement).

---

# Basic Principle

The double tells partner:

> "I like your suit, and I hold a top honor in it."

This helps the overcaller decide whether to:

- Compete further.
- Defend.
- Lead the suit if defending.

---

# Requirements

Typical agreements:

- At least **three-card support** for partner's overcalled suit.
- The **Ace or King** of that suit.
- Competitive values.

Some partnerships require four-card support instead.

---

# Example

South holds:

```
♠ K84
♥ J72
♦ 85
♣ AQJ74
```

Auction:

```text
West     North     East     South

1♦        1♠        2♦        Double
```

The double shows:

- Spade support.
- The ♠K.

North now knows the overcall has excellent support.

---

# Responses

After a Rosenkranz Double, partner may:

- Compete with confidence.
- Invite game.
- Defend.
- Double later for penalties if appropriate.

The exact continuations are natural.

---

# Lead-Directing Value

If the opponents buy the contract:

```
4♥ by West
```

North knows South has:

- A top spade honor.

A spade lead is therefore more attractive.

---

# Advantages

- Distinguishes constructive support.
- Helps judge competitive auctions.
- Improves opening leads.
- Simple once agreed.

---

# Disadvantages

- Gives information to the opponents.
- Sacrifices the natural meaning of the double.
- Requires explicit partnership agreement.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Whether the double promises the Ace only or the Ace or King.
- Minimum support (three or four cards).
- Minimum strength.
- Whether it applies after raises only or after any continuation.
- Treatment after further interference.

---

# Variations

## Standard Rosenkranz

Shows:

- Support.
- Ace or King.

---

## Extended Rosenkranz

Some partnerships also allow:

- Queen with excellent support.
- Four-card support only.
- Additional strength requirements.
