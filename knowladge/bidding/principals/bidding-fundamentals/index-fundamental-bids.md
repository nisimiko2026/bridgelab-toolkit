# Fundamental Bids

## Overview

The **Fundamental Bids** section introduces the basic natural bids that every
bridge player should master before studying advanced bidding systems or
conventions.

These bids establish the language of bridge bidding and form the basis upon
which all constructive, competitive, and slam auctions are built.

Unlike artificial conventions, fundamental bids rely primarily on natural hand
description and sound bidding principles.

This section is intended as the starting point for learning natural bidding.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should understand

- the purpose of every basic bid,
- how bids communicate strength and distribution,
- the priorities of natural bidding,
- forcing and non-forcing auctions,
- the progression of a natural auction.

# Design Principles

## Natural Communication

Every bid should communicate

- strength,
- distribution,
- suit preference,
- partnership fit.

---

## Describe the Hand

Each bid should add new information rather than repeat information already
known.

---

## Partnership Cooperation

Natural bidding is a conversation.

Each player contributes information that allows the partnership to reach the
best contract.
