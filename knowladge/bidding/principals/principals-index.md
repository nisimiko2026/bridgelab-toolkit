# Bidding Principles

## Overview

The **Bidding Principles** section forms the theoretical foundation of the
BridgeLab bidding knowledge base.

Unlike bidding systems and conventions, which explain **what to bid**, these
articles explain **why a bid is correct**.

These principles apply regardless of the partnership's bidding system, making
them universally relevant to

- Standard American,
- SAYC,
- 2/1 Game Force,
- Acol,
- Precision,
- Polish Club,
- Relay Systems,
- and every other bidding style.

A strong understanding of these principles leads to better judgement,
consistency, and partnership cooperation.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should understand

- how bridge hands should be evaluated,
- how auction context affects decisions,
- how vulnerability changes strategy,
- how partnership communication works,
- when judgement overrides mechanical rules,
- why successful bidding is based on principles rather than memorization.
