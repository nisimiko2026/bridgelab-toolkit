# Bidding Systems

## Overview

The **Bidding Systems** section documents the major natural, artificial, and
hybrid bidding systems used throughout the world.

A bidding system is a complete partnership framework that defines

- opening bids,
- responses,
- rebids,
- competitive methods,
- slam bidding,
- partnership agreements,
- hand evaluation philosophy.

Unlike individual conventions, which solve specific bidding problems, a bidding
system provides a complete language for partnership communication.

BridgeLab documents each system independently while cross-referencing the
underlying conventions to avoid duplication.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should understand

- the philosophy behind each bidding system,
- the strengths and weaknesses of each approach,
- opening structures,
- responder methods,
- system-specific conventions,
- suitable partnership styles,
- historical development.

# System Categories

## Natural Systems

Natural systems emphasize

- natural opening bids,
- straightforward hand descriptions,
- gradual information exchange.

Examples include

- Standard American
- SAYC
- Acol
- Benjamin Acol
- 2/1 Game Force
- Kaplan–Sheinwold
- Roth–Stone
- Culbertson
- EHAA
- SEF
- Wspólny Język

These systems are widely used by club and tournament players.

---

## Club Systems

Club systems employ an artificial club opening as the foundation of the system.

Examples include

- Precision
- Super Precision
- Chinese Precision
- Roman Precision
- Blue Club
- Polish Club
- Roman Club
- Neapolitan Club
- Carrot Club
- Magic Diamond
- Ultimate Club
- Moscito

These systems generally provide

- greater descriptive accuracy,
- relay opportunities,
- stronger game and slam investigations,

at the cost of increased complexity.
