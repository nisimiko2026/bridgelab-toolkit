# Standard American Yellow Card (SAYC)

## Overview

**Standard American Yellow Card (SAYC)** is the standard bidding system developed by the American Contract Bridge League (ACBL) for tournament and club play. It is a **natural**, **five-card major** system that incorporates many widely used modern conventions.

SAYC is designed to be easy to learn while providing a complete partnership system suitable for duplicate bridge.

---

# System Summary

| Opening | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **1♣** | Natural, 3+ clubs (may be 2 with 4-4-3-2 shape) |
| **1♦** | Natural, 3+ diamonds |
| **1♥** | 5+ hearts, 12–21 HCP |
| **1♠** | 5+ spades, 12–21 HCP |
| **1NT** | 15–17 HCP, balanced |
| **2♣** | Artificial, 22+ HCP or game-forcing hand |
| **2♦ / 2♥ / 2♠** | Weak Two |
| **2NT** | 20–21 HCP, balanced |
| **3-level** | Preemptive |
| **4-level** | Preemptive |

# Responses to 1NT

## Stayman

```
1NT
2♣
```

Artificial.

Asks opener for a four-card major.

Responses:

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| 2♦ | No four-card major |
| 2♥ | Four hearts |
| 2♠ | Four spades |

---

## Jacoby Transfers

| Bid | Transfer To |
|------|-------------|
| 2♦ | Hearts |
| 2♥ | Spades |

Responder normally has five or more cards in the target suit.

---

## Texas Transfers

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 4♦ | Transfer to Hearts |
| 4♥ | Transfer to Spades |

Used with game-going hands.

---

## Gerber

```
1NT
4♣
```

Asks for aces.

Typically used only over notrump openings or rebids.

# Strong 2♣ Opening

Artificial.

Shows:

- 22+ HCP balanced, or
- Any hand with near game-forcing strength

Responses:

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| 2♦ | Waiting (default) |
| New suit | Good suit and positive values |

# Weak Two Openings

## 2♦ / 2♥ / 2♠

Shows:

- Six-card suit
- 5–10 HCP
- Good suit quality

Not used with two-suited hands.

---

# 2NT Opening

Shows:

- 20–21 HCP
- Balanced

Uses Stayman, Jacoby Transfers, Texas Transfers, and Gerber.

# Responses to Minor Openings

One-over-one responses:

- Natural
- 6+ HCP
- Not forcing to game

New suits at the one level are forcing for one round.

---

# Advantages

- Natural and easy to learn.
- Five-card majors improve major-suit contract selection.
- Well documented and widely recognized.
- Supported by most bridge software and teaching materials.
- Excellent foundation for advanced partnerships.

---

# Disadvantages

- Less efficient than expert systems such as 2/1 Game Force or Precision.
- Some invitational auctions are less descriptive.
- Partnership upgrades often require additional conventions.

# Memory Aid

Think of **SAYC** as the classic modern American system:

- **5-card majors**
- **15–17 notrump**
- **Strong 2♣**
- **Weak Twos**
- **Natural bidding supported by standard conventions**

It provides a complete, well-balanced system suitable for most duplicate bridge partnerships and serves as the foundation for many modern bidding styles.
