# Convention Cards

## Overview

A **Convention Card** is the official written record of a partnership's bidding
and defensive agreements.

It serves three essential purposes:

- Documents partnership agreements
- Informs opponents of partnership methods
- Ensures fair and ethical competition

A properly completed convention card reduces misunderstandings, improves
partnership consistency, and satisfies tournament disclosure requirements.

This section explains how convention cards are completed, interpreted,
maintained, and used in competitive bridge.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should understand

- why convention cards are required,
- how to complete one correctly,

# Design Principles

## Complete Disclosure

Opponents are entitled to understand your partnership agreements.

---

## Partnership Consistency

A convention card should accurately reflect the methods actually played.

---

## Regular Maintenance

Convention cards should be reviewed and updated whenever partnership agreements
change.

---

## Ethical Responsibility

Convention cards support fair competition through complete and accurate
disclosure.
