# Bidding

> *The language of partnership communication.*

Bidding is the foundation of Contract Bridge. Before a single card is played,
the auction allows partners to exchange information about strength,
distribution, suit fits, controls, and ultimately determine the best contract.

This section of **BridgeLab** covers every aspect of bidding, from fundamental
opening bids to the most sophisticated relay systems used by world-class
partnerships.

---

# Introduction

The purpose of bidding is to

- determine the best denomination,
- determine the correct level,
- identify declarer,
- exchange information efficiently,
- obstruct the opponents when appropriate,
- investigate game and slam,
- compete accurately.

Modern bidding combines

- natural methods,
- artificial conventions,
- partnership agreements,
- expert judgment.

# Core Principles

Every successful bidding system is built upon several principles.

- Describe your hand accurately.
- Exchange information economically.
- Preserve bidding space whenever possible.
- Distinguish forcing from non-forcing auctions.
- Identify the partnership fit.
- Compete aggressively when appropriate.
- Trust partnership agreements.
- Apply judgment rather than rigid formulas.

Modern bridge contains hundreds of bidding conventions. No partnership plays all
of them. BridgeLab distinguishes between

approach and identifies significant variations.
