# Texas transfers convention

##  after opening 1NT or
##  after the opener has a hand of 2NT (20-22 HP)

### 4♥️	At least 6 hearts and game values, requiring partner to rebid 4.
### 4♠️ At least 6 spades and game values, requiring partner to rebid 4.


Note that 4♥️ and 4♠️ promise game values, but deny slam values. With a hand interested in slam, responder should start with a Jacoby Transfer and make a forcing rebid at his next turn.
