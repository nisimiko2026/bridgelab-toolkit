# RKCBconvention

##  answer to 4NT ask for key cards, after decision of trump

### 5♣	0 or 3 key cards.
### 5♦️	1 or 4 key cards.
### 5♥️	2 or 5 key cards without the queen of trumps.
### 5♠️	2 or 5 key cards with the queen of trumps.

## Void-showing responses

### 5NT	An even # of key cards and a void somewhere.
### 6♣	An odd # of key cards and:
     A club void if clubs aren't trumps, or
     A void in any other suit if clubs are trumps
### 6♦️	An odd # of key cards and:
     A diamond void if hearts or spades are trumps, or
     A void in hearts or spades, if diamonds are trumps
### 6♥️	An odd # of key cards and:
     A heart void if spades are trumps, or
     A spade void if hearts are trumps

## Void-showing responses Asking for Kings (5NT)

### 6♣  shows king club
### 6♦️ shows king diamond denies king club
### 6♥️ shows king heart, or if the trump is heart no other kings below
### 6♠️  no other kings below

## Asking for the Trump Queen

### Bidding the trump suit  	    No trump queen
### 5NT	The trump queen without any side-suit kings
### Bidding any non-trump suit  	The trump queen AND the king of that suit
