# Stayman convention

## 2♣ after opening 1 NT

### 2♣ might be garbage stayman, minimum HP

## answers after 2♣

### 2♦️	Denies a 4-card major.
###	2♥️ hearts, may have 4 spades.
###	2♠️  spades, denies 4 hearts.

## Responder's rebids after 2♦️

### 2♥️	5 spades and 4 hearts, invitational. Non-forcing.
### 2♠️	4 spades and 5 hearts, invitational. Non-forcing.
### 2NT	Invitational.
### 3♣️	Game forcing with at least 5 clubs (usually 6). Does not guarantee a 4-card major.
### 3♦️	Game forcing with at least 5 diamonds (usually 6). Does not guarantee a 4-card major.
### 3♥️	5 spades and 4 hearts, game-forcing.
### 3♠️	4 spades and 5 hearts, game-forcing.
### 3NT	Signoff.
### 4♦️	transfer to  hearts (6 hearts)
### 4♥️	transfer to  spades (6 spades))
### 4NT	Quantitative, invites 6NT.

## Responder's rebids after 2♥️

### 2NT	limit bid, not necesarrily 4 ♠️
### Pass with a minimum
### 3♣️	Game-forcing with at least 5 clubs (usually 6). Does not guarantee a 4-card major.
### 3♦️	Game-forcing with at least 5 diamonds (usually 6). Does not guarantee a 4-card major.
### 3♥️	4 hearts, invitational.
### 3NT	sign off fewer than 4 hearts. Opener can pass or bid 4 with 4 spades.
### 4♥️	Signoff.
### 4NT	Quantitative, invites 6NT.

## Responder's rebids after 2♠️

### 2NT	Invitational.
### 3♣️	Game-forcing with at least 5 clubs (usually 6). Does not guarantee a 4-card major.
### 3♦️	Game-forcing with at least 5 diamonds (usually 6). Does not guarantee a 4-card major.
### 3♠️	4 spades, invitational.
### 3NT	Signoff with 4 hearts.
### 4♠️	Signoff.
### 4NT	Quantitative, invites 6NT.
