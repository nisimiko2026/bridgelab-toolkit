# Flannery Convention

## Purpose

The **Flannery Convention** is a specialized opening bid designed to solve a common rebid problem with hands containing **five hearts and four spades** of minimum opening strength.

Instead of opening **1♥** and later struggling to describe the hand accurately, opener begins with an artificial **2♦** opening.

The convention helps the partnership:

- Describe 5♥–4♠ distribution immediately.
- Avoid difficult rebid problems.
- Improve game and slam bidding.
- Help responder evaluate major-suit fits.

---

# When It Applies

The Flannery opening is:

```
2♦
```

Artificial.

It replaces the normal Weak Two in diamonds.

---

# Basic Meaning

A **2♦ Flannery** opening shows:

- **11–15 HCP**
- **5 hearts**
- **4 spades**
- No longer major
- Opening strength

Most partnerships play:

- Exactly **5♥ and 4♠**
- Minimum to medium opening values

---

# Requirements

Typical requirements:

- 11–15 HCP
- Exactly 5 hearts
- Exactly 4 spades
- No six-card heart suit
- No singleton or void that would suggest another treatment

---

# Why Flannery?

Without Flannery:

```
♠ KQ84
♥ AQ963
♦ K4
♣ J73
```

Open:

```
1♥
```

If responder bids:

```
1NT
```

there is no perfect rebid.

Flannery solves this problem immediately.

---

# Standard Responses

## 2♥

Shows:

- Preference for hearts
- Weak hand

---

## 2♠

Shows:

- Preference for spades
- Usually four-card support

---

## 2NT

Artificial.

Asks opener for additional information.

---

## 3♣

Natural.

Game interest.

---

## 3♦

Artificial or natural depending on partnership agreement.

---

## 3♥ / 3♠

Invitational raises.

---

## 4♥ / 4♠

To play.

---

# Opener's Rebids After 2NT Inquiry

Most partnerships use the following structure:

| Rebid | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 3♣ | Minimum hand |
| 3♦ | Maximum hand |
| 3♥ | Minimum with better hearts |
| 3♠ | Maximum with better spades |
| 3NT | Balanced maximum (partnership agreement) |

Many alternative structures exist.

# What Flannery Does NOT Show

A Flannery opening does **not** show:

- Weak Two in diamonds.
- Six hearts.
- Five spades.
- Strong opening values.
- Balanced notrump hand.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Agree on:

- Exact HCP range (11–15 or 11–16).
- Exactly 5–4 or occasionally 5–5.
- Meaning of the 2NT inquiry.
- Whether 3♦ remains available naturally.
- Responses after interference.
- Whether Flannery is used in third and fourth seat.

---

# Advantages

- Solves difficult rebid problems.
- Immediately identifies both major suits.
- Improves responder's evaluation.
- Simplifies later bidding.
- Popular in Standard American partnerships.

---

# Disadvantages

- Gives up the Weak Two in diamonds.
- Requires partnership agreements.
- Several response structures exist.

---

# Common Partnership Style

Most expert partnerships play:

- **2♦ = 11–15 HCP**
- **Exactly 5 hearts and 4 spades**
- **2NT** asks for additional information.
- Weak Two in diamonds is no longer available.

Some partnerships expand the range to 11–16 HCP.

---

# Comparison

| Opening | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Weak 2♦ | Weak six-card diamond suit |
| Flannery 2♦ | 11–15 HCP, 5♥–4♠ |
| Multi 2♦ | Weak major or strong hand (varies) |

# Memory Aid

**Flannery = "Five Hearts, Four Spades."**

Remember:

- **2♦ is artificial**
- **11–15 HCP**
- **Exactly 5♥ and 4♠**
- **Opening values**
- **2NT asks for more information**

The Flannery Convention is designed to eliminate awkward rebids with the common 5♥–4♠ opening hand, giving responder an immediate and accurate picture of opener's major-suit distribution.
