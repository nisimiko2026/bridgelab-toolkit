# Drury Convention

## Purpose

The **Drury Convention** is used by a **passed hand** after partner opens **1♥** or **1♠** in third or fourth seat.

Its purpose is to determine whether opener has a **full opening bid** or has opened light (minimum third/fourth seat opening).

Drury helps the partnership avoid reaching games opposite a light opening while still allowing game exploration opposite a normal opening.

---

# When Drury Applies

Requirements:

- You are a **passed hand**
- Partner opens **1♥** or **1♠**
- Opener is in **third or fourth seat**
- You have at least **3-card support** for opener's major

Example:

```
Pass   Pass
1♠     ?
```

Responder may use Drury.

---

# Basic Agreement

## 2♣ = Drury

Artificial.

Shows:

- Passed hand
- 10-12 (sometimes 10-11) HCP
- At least 3-card support
- Invitational values

Asks opener whether the opening was full or light.

---

# Standard Responses

## 2♦

Artificial.

Shows:

- Minimum or light opening
- Does not accept invitation

Responder normally passes or signs off.

---

## 2♥ / 2♠

Natural.

Shows:

- Full opening bid
- Accepts invitation
- Good opening strength

Responder may invite or bid game.

---

## Jump to Game

Example:


Shows:

- Full opening
- Excellent fit

# Reverse Drury

Many partnerships play **Reverse Drury**.

Responses become:

| Response | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| 2♦ | Artificial, full opening |
| 2♥ / 2♠ | Minimum or light opening |

This is now the most common expert treatment.

Partnership agreement required.

---

# Two-Way Drury

Some partnerships use:

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 2♣ | Three-card support |
| 2♦ | Four-card support |

Allows opener to judge games more accurately.

Partnership agreement required.

---

# Opener's Rebids (Standard Drury)

| Rebid | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 2♦ | Light/minimum opening |
| 2M | Full opening |
| 3M | Extra values |
| New suit | Natural feature |

---

# Requirements

Typical Drury hand:

- Passed hand
- 10-12 HCP
- 3+ trumps
- Invitational strength

---

# Advantages

- Detects light third-seat openings
- Avoids poor games
- Allows accurate invitations
- Improves major-suit bidding

---

# Disadvantages

- Uses the natural 2♣ response
- Partnership agreement required
- Several popular variations exist

---

# Partnership Agreements

Decide:

- Standard or Reverse Drury?
- One-Way or Two-Way Drury?
- Minimum HCP required?
- Three-card or four-card support?
- Is Drury on in fourth seat?
- Is Drury off after interference?

---

# Memory Aid

**Drury = Passed Hand Invitational Raise**

Standard Drury:

- **2♣ = Artificial invitation**
- **2♦ = Light opening**
- **2M = Full opening**

Reverse Drury:

- **2♣ = Artificial invitation**
- **2♦ = Full opening**
- **2M = Light opening**

Always agree with partner which version you play.
