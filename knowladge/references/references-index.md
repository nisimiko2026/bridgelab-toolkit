# References

## Overview

The **References** section contains supporting material used throughout the
BridgeLab Knowledge Base.

Unlike the bidding, declarer play, and defence sections, the material here is
primarily informational rather than instructional. These references provide
quick access to definitions, tables, regulations, scoring methods, terminology,
historical information, and official documentation.

This section serves as the encyclopedia and reference library for BridgeLab.

---

# Objectives

The References section enables readers to

- quickly locate factual information,
- verify terminology,
- understand official regulations,
- compare scoring methods,
- consult bridge tables,
- access historical background,
- review official documentation.

# Design Principles

## Reference Rather Than Instruction

Reference articles provide concise factual information.

Detailed instruction belongs elsewhere in the BridgeLab Knowledge Base.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Each factual topic is documented in one authoritative location.

Other articles link to these references rather than duplicating information.

---

## Quick Navigation

Reference material is organized for rapid lookup rather than sequential study.

---

## Official Sources

Whenever possible, reference articles summarize and explain official laws,
regulations, and organizational standards.

---

# Recommended Reading Order
