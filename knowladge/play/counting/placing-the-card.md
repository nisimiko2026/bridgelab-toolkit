# Placing the Card

## Overview

Placing the card is the process of determining which opponent holds a specific unseen card by combining information from the auction, the play, counting, and logical inference.

## Why Place a Card?

- Improve finesse decisions
- Choose between competing lines
- Plan squeezes
- Execute endplays
- Avoid unnecessary guesses
