# Counting the Hand

## Overview

Counting the hand is the process of reconstructing an unseen hand by combining all available information from the auction and the play.

It integrates:

- High-card points
- Distribution
- Shape
- Revealed honors
- Signals
- Inferences

The goal is to know, or closely approximate, every unseen card.

## Step 1 – Count the High-Card Points

- Known HCP
- Missing HCP
- Point ranges from the auction

Determine suit lengths.

## Step 4 – Use the Opening Lead

What does it promise?

- Length
- Strength
- Sequence
- Singleton

## Step 5 – Watch Every Card

Track:

- Follows
- Ruffs
- Discards
- Signals

## Step 6 – Eliminate Impossible Hands

As more information becomes available:

- Remove impossible distributions
- Remove impossible point counts
- Narrow remaining possibilities

## Step 7 – Place Individual Cards

When only one layout remains:

- Place the Queen
- Place the King
- Decide on the correct line of play

## Worked Example

Complete deal from trick one to the final inference.

## Common Mistakes

- Counting only one suit
- Forgetting the auction
- Ignoring revealed honors
- Failing to update after every trick
