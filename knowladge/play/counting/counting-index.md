# Counting

## Overview

Counting is one of the most important skills in contract bridge.

Every successful player continuously counts available information throughout
the play. Accurate counting transforms hidden information into logical
deductions, reducing guesswork and improving both declarer play and defence.

Bridge counting is not limited to high-card points. Players count

- distribution,
- high cards,
- winners,
- losers,
- entries,
- trump,
- remaining cards,
- signals,
- tempo.

Mastering counting allows players to reconstruct unseen hands and choose the
best technical line of play.

# Counting Priorities

During every hand, expert players typically count

1. Winners or losers.
2. Trump.
3. Distribution.
4. Entries.
5. High-card locations.
6. Remaining unknown cards.
7. Signals and inferences.

---

# Design Principles

## Count Continuously

Counting is an ongoing process from the opening lead until the final trick.

---

## Count Before Guessing

Logical counting should always precede intuition.

---

## Combine Information

The best conclusions come from combining

- auction,
- opening lead,
- card play,
- signals,
- tempo.

---

## Declarer and Defence

Both declarer and defenders rely on identical counting principles, although
their objectives differ.
