# Card Play

## Overview

The **Card Play** section covers every aspect of play after the auction has
ended.

Success in bridge depends not only on accurate bidding but also on superior
card play. Whether declaring or defending, players must combine technical
knowledge, planning, counting, inference, communication, and judgement to
maximize the number of tricks won.

BridgeLab separates card play into two complementary disciplines:

- Declarer Play
- Defence

Together they provide a complete treatment of modern bridge card play.

# Main Sections

## Declarer Play

Declarer play focuses on maximizing tricks after becoming declarer.

Topics include

- planning,
- establishing suits,
- trump techniques,
- communication,
- entries,
- safety plays,
- endplays,
- squeezes,
- deceptive play.

The declarer controls both the declarer's hand and dummy.

## Defence

Defence focuses on preventing declarer from making the contract.

- counting,
- defensive planning,
- communication,
- forcing defence,

# Declarer vs Defence

| Declarer Play | Defence |
|---------------|----------|
| Wins tricks | Prevents tricks |
| Controls two hands | Controls one hand |
| Plans entire play | Cooperates with partner |
| Establishes suits | Attacks communication |
| Preserves entries | Destroys entries |
| Executes squeezes | Prevents squeezes |

# Design Principles

## Planning Before Playing

Every hand should begin with a complete plan before playing from dummy.

---

## Count Continuously

Successful card play depends upon counting

- winners,
- losers,
- entries,
- distribution,
- remaining high cards.

---

## Communication

Communication is essential for both declarer and defenders.

Maintaining or destroying entries often determines the outcome.

---

## Technique Before Guessing

Bridge rewards logical technique more consistently than intuition.

Whenever possible, apply sound technical principles before relying on guesses.
