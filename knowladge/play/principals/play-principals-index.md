# Card Play Principles

## Overview

Bridge is not merely a collection of techniques.

Successful players first understand the fundamental principles that govern card
play and then apply the appropriate techniques to each position.

These principles apply to

- declarer play,
- defensive play,
- suit contracts,
- notrump contracts.

They form the conceptual foundation upon which every advanced technique is
built.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- understand the philosophy of expert card play,
- recognize universal bridge principles,
- select techniques based on principles rather than memorization,
- develop consistent thinking processes,
- avoid common strategic errors.

# Core Principles

## Plan Before Playing

Always formulate a complete plan before playing from dummy.

```
plan-before-playing.md
```

---

## Count Everything

Continuously count

- distribution,
- trumps,
- winners,
- losers,
- entries,
- high-card points.

```
count-everything.md
```

---

## Preserve Communication

Maintain transportation between hands and between defenders whenever possible.

```

```

---

## Timing Is Everything

The correct technique played at the wrong time often fails.

```

```

---

## Entries Are Assets

Entries are valuable resources and should never be wasted unnecessarily.

```

```

---

## Make a Purposeful Play

Every card should have a strategic objective.


---

## Think Ahead

Plan several tricks into the future rather than focusing only on the current
trick.


## Improve Information

Delay important decisions until sufficient information has been gathered.

```
improve-information.md
```

---

## Conceal Information

Avoid giving opponents unnecessary clues whenever possible.

```

```

---

## Maximize Flexibility

Keep multiple lines of play available until a decision becomes necessary.

```

```

## Create Extra Tricks

Identify every possible source of additional tricks before committing to a line
of play.


# Design Principles

## Principles Before Techniques

Understanding *why* a technique works is more valuable than memorizing *how* it
works.

---

## Every Decision Has a Purpose

Expert players rarely make automatic plays.

Each card contributes to the overall strategy.

---

## Information Is Power

The player with the better understanding of the unseen cards usually has the
best chance of success.

---

## Flexibility Creates Opportunities

Avoid committing to a single line of play before all relevant information has
been gathered.
