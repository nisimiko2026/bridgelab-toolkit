# Defence

## Overview

Defence is one of the most challenging aspects of contract bridge.

Unlike declarer, each defender controls only one hand and must cooperate with
partner using only legal communication through card play and previously agreed
methods.

Successful defence requires

- planning,
- counting,
- signalling,
- inference,
- communication,
- timing,
- visualization,
- partnership cooperation.

BridgeLab organizes defensive play into logical subject areas that progress
from opening leads to advanced defensive techniques.

---

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- select effective opening leads,

- execute advanced defensive techniques,
- defend as a coordinated partnership.

# Design Principles

## Think Before Trick One

The opening lead should fit the auction, the contract, and the partnership's
agreements.

---

## Count Continuously

Good defenders continually count

- declarer's shape,
- partner's shape,
- remaining high cards,
- trumps,
- entries.

---

## Partnership Cooperation

Neither defender can succeed alone.

Successful defence depends upon

- trust,
- accurate signalling,
- shared planning,
- logical inference.

---

## Information Management

Defenders should

- reveal only necessary information,
- avoid helping declarer,
- recognize declarer's plan,
- interfere with declarer's communication.

---

## Revise the Plan

Every trick provides new information.

Expert defenders continually update their analysis throughout the play.
