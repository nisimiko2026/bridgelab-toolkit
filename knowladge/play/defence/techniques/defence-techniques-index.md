# Defensive Techniques

## Overview

Defensive techniques are the practical tools used by defenders after the opening
lead has been made and dummy has appeared.

Unlike planning or signalling, defensive techniques are the actual plays that
allow defenders to

- establish tricks,
- destroy declarer's communication,
- promote partner's cards,
- prevent declarer's techniques,
- force declarer into unfavorable positions.

Most successful defenses combine several techniques throughout a single hand.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- recognize common defensive opportunities,
- choose the correct defensive technique,
- coordinate with partner,
- prevent declarer's advanced techniques,
- execute technical defensive plays accurately,
- adapt as the hand develops.

# Design Principles

## Technique Supports the Plan

Defensive techniques should always support the overall defensive strategy.

---

## Communication Is Critical

Many successful defensive techniques depend upon preserving communication with
partner.

---

## Timing Wins Tricks

Correct timing is often more important than selecting the technically strongest
play.

---

## Think Like Declarer

The best countermeasure is often found by identifying declarer's intended
technique and disrupting it before it develops.
