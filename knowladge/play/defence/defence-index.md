Defense is the partnership's effort to defeat the contract through accurate opening leads, effective signaling, careful counting, and coordinated planning. This index organizes the defensive topics from fundamental principles to advanced techniques.

# Memory Aid

**Defense = "Lead • Count • Signal • Cooperate."**

Successful defenders should:

- Develop a plan before making the opening lead.
- Use partnership lead agreements consistently.
- Count declarer's shape and high-card strength throughout the play.
- Signal clearly and according to partnership agreements.
- Work together as a partnership to create defensive tricks rather than relying on individual brilliance.
