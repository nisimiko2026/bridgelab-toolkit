# Defensive Counting

## Overview

Expert defenders continuously reconstruct the unseen hands throughout the play.

Unlike declarer, defenders must work with incomplete information and rely on

- the auction,
- the opening lead,
- dummy,
- partner's signals,
- declarer's line of play,
- card distribution,
- logical inference.

Accurate counting allows defenders to

- anticipate declarer's plan,
- locate missing honors,
- determine suit distributions,
- preserve communication,
- choose winning switches,
- defeat squeezes and endplays.

Counting is not a separate activity—it is an ongoing process that continues
from the opening lead until the last trick.

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- count declarer's distribution,

# Design Principles

## Count Continuously

Every card played changes the available information.

Expert defenders never stop counting.

---

## Every Clue Matters

Combine information from

- the auction,
- partner,
- declarer,
- dummy,
- previous tricks.

---

## Build the Complete Picture

Individual counts are valuable, but the ultimate goal is to reconstruct the
entire unseen deal.

---

## Counting Supports Every Decision

Opening leads, switches, discards, signals, and endgame defense all become more
accurate through disciplined counting.
