# Probability

## Overview

Probability is one of the foundations of expert bridge.

Every technical decision—whether to finesse, play for the drop, establish a
long suit, execute a squeeze, or choose a safety play—is ultimately based on
probability.

Bridge probabilities differ from pure mathematics because new information is
continually obtained from

- the auction,
- the opening lead,
- defenders' signals,
- tempo,
- previous tricks,
- restricted choice.

Expert players continually update probabilities as additional information
becomes available.

# Design Principles

## Percentage Before Guessing

Choose the mathematically superior line unless reliable evidence suggests
otherwise.

---

## Update Continuously

Every bid and every card played provides new information that changes the
probabilities.

---

## Probability Supports Technique

Mathematics guides technical decisions but never replaces sound technique.

---

## Practical Bridge

The correct mathematical play may differ depending on

- scoring method,
- vulnerability,
- contract,
- partnership objectives.
