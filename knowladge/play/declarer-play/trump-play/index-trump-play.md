# Trump Play

Trump play is one of the defining features of suit contracts. Successful
declarers learn when to draw trumps, when to delay, how to use trumps to create
extra tricks, and how to exploit advanced trump techniques such as crossruffs,
dummy reversals, and trump coups.

This section covers the fundamental and advanced methods of managing the trump
suit during declarer play.

## Fundamental Trump Techniques

- **trump-play.md** — Principles of declarer play in trump contracts.
- **trump-control.md** — Managing, preserving, and drawing trumps effectively.
- **ruffing-losers.md** — Using trumps to eliminate losing cards.
- **crossruff.md** — Creating additional tricks by ruffing in both hands.
- **dummy-reversal.md** — Reversing the normal trump roles of declarer and dummy.

## Advanced Trump Techniques

- **uppercut.md** — Promoting trump tricks by forcing overruffs.
