# Elimination Play

## Overview

**Elimination Play** is a family of declarer techniques in which declarer
removes ("eliminates") one or more suits before forcing a defender to lead a
remaining suit that benefits declarer.

Elimination frequently combines with

- throw-ins,
- endplays,
- strip plays,
- trump management,
- squeezes,
- communication,
- timing.

Rather than relying on finesses or favorable card locations, elimination
creates situations where the defenders have no safe exit card.

It is one of the most powerful forms of technical declarer play.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- recognize elimination opportunities,
- identify dangerous exit cards,
- remove safe defensive exits,
- execute throw-ins,
- combine elimination with endplays,
- recognize positions where elimination is superior to a finesse.

# Design Principles

## Eliminate Before Throwing In

The throw-in is only effective after defenders' safe exits have been removed.

---

## Timing Is Critical

The order in which suits are eliminated often determines success.

---

## Count Carefully

Successful elimination depends upon accurate counting of

- distribution,
- entries,
- trump,
- remaining exit cards.

---

## Force, Don't Guess

Elimination frequently replaces an uncertain finesse with a virtually certain
technical play.
