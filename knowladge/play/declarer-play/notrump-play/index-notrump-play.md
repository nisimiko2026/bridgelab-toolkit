# Notrump Play

## Overview

Playing notrump contracts differs fundamentally from playing trump contracts.

Without a trump suit, declarer cannot ruff losers and must instead rely on

- establishing winners,
- preserving entries,
- maintaining communication,
- timing,
- ducking,
- hold-up play,
- accurate counting,
- careful planning.

Because every trick is precious, notrump play rewards precise technique and
careful visualization.

This section explains the principles and techniques unique to successful
notrump declarer play.

# Design Principles

## Count Winners First

Unlike suit contracts, notrump play begins by counting winners rather than
losers.

---

## Entries Are Precious

Many notrump contracts succeed or fail because of entry management.

---

## Timing Is Critical

Develop side suits before defenders establish theirs.

---

## Communication Wins Contracts

Maintaining transportation between declarer and dummy is often the key to
success.

---

## Hold Up Only With a Purpose

Hold-up plays should achieve a specific objective, such as breaking defenders'
communication or exhausting a dangerous suit.
