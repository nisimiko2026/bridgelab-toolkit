# Planning

Successful declarer play begins before the first card is played. Careful
planning allows declarer to evaluate winners, losers, entries, communication,
timing, and the best line of play before committing to a strategy.

This section covers the core planning techniques that guide declarer throughout
the play of the hand.

## Planning Fundamentals

- **planning.md** — Developing an overall declarer plan before trick one.
- **choosing-a-line.md** — Selecting the best line of play based on available information.
- **creating-extra-tricks.md** — Identifying and developing additional winning tricks.
- **entries.md** — Managing communication and preserving entries between declarer and dummy.
- **timing.md** — Executing the chosen plan in the correct order.

## Supporting Topics

- **counting.md** — Counting winners, losers, distribution, and outstanding cards.
- **probability.md** — Using mathematical odds to choose the best line of play.
- **hand-evaluation.md** — Assessing the offensive potential of the combined hands.
- **visualization.md** — Mentally mapping the play before and during the hand.
