# Coups

## Overview

A **coup** is a named technical maneuver that achieves a result which appears
impossible through ordinary card play.

Most coups exploit

- timing,
- communication,
- entries,
- trump control,
- endplay,
- squeeze positions,
- card combinations,
- psychological deception.

Many coups are combinations of several elementary techniques rather than unique
plays. Understanding the underlying ideas is often more important than
memorizing their names.

The articles in this section explain both the mechanics of each coup and the
general principles that make them successful.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- recognize classic coup positions,
- understand the underlying principles,
- identify opportunities during play,
- combine coups with squeezes and endplays,
- avoid common errors,
- apply advanced technical play confidently.

# Design Principles

## Technique Before Memorization

A coup is best understood by mastering its underlying principle rather than
memorizing the name alone.

---

## Combination Techniques

Many coups combine

- timing,
- entries,
- communication,
- squeezes,
- endplays,
- trump management.

---

## Rare but Valuable

Although many coups occur infrequently, recognizing them can transform an
otherwise impossible contract into a successful one.

---

## Practical Recognition

Expert players identify the *position* first and only then recognize the
appropriate coup.
