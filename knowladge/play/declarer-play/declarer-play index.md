# Declarer Play

Declarer play is the art of planning and executing the play of the hand to maximize the chances of making the contract. This index organizes the techniques from fundamental planning to advanced endplays and coups.

---

# Planning

### Planning the Play
Develop a complete plan before playing from dummy at trick one.

### Timing
Determine the correct order in which to establish and cash winners.

### Tempo
Understand how preserving or gaining the lead affects the entire play.

---

# Counting

### Counting Winners
Count immediate and potential winners before choosing a line of play.

### Counting Losers
Identify losers in suit contracts to determine how they can be eliminated.

### Counting Distribution
Infer the defenders' distribution from the auction and play.

### Counting Declarer's Shape
Deduce declarer's hand pattern from the bidding and revealed cards.

---

# Probability

### Probability and Percentages
Apply mathematical probabilities to select the best line of play.

### Percentage Plays
Choose the line that succeeds most often over the long run.

### Restricted Choice
Use probability theory when defenders play equivalent honor cards.

---

# Finesses

### Finesse
Win a trick by playing for the favorable position of a missing honor.

### Double Finesse
Attempt two simultaneous finesses within the same suit.

### Deep Finesse
Take a finesse against an intermediate honor rather than the highest missing honor.

### Intra-finesse
Execute a finesse that depends on the location of intermediate honors.

---

# Entry Management

### Entry Management
Create, preserve, and use entries efficiently between declarer and dummy.

### Avoidance Play
Keep a dangerous defender off lead by controlling who wins tricks.

### Hold-Up Play
Delay winning a trick to disrupt the defenders' communications.

---

# Elimination and Endplays

### Throw-In
Force a defender to give declarer a favorable lead.

### Discovery Play
Reveal the defenders' distributions before committing to a line of play.

### Strip Squeeze
Combine elimination and squeeze techniques to force a favorable ending.

### Vienna Coup
Unblock a high card early to prepare a later squeeze.

### Rectifying the Count
Lose the necessary tricks before attempting a squeeze.

---

# Coups

### Coup en Passant
Score a trump trick by ruffing after an opponent is unable to overruff.

### Trump Coup
Win a trick by shortening your own trumps to neutralize a higher trump.

### Devil's Coup
A deceptive coup that forces the defenders into an unfavorable position.

### Scissors Coup
Destroy the defenders' communications by removing a vital entry.

### Merrimac Coup
Sacrifice a high card to eliminate a defender's important entry.

### Bath Coup
Duck an honor to mislead the opponents and preserve communication.

### Morton's Fork
Force the defenders into a choice where either option benefits declarer.

---

# Related Topics

### End Position Analysis
Analyze simplified endings to identify winning lines of play.

# Memory Aid

**Declarer Play = "Plan • Count • Time • Execute."**

Every declarer should:

- Form a complete plan before playing from dummy.
- Count winners, losers, and distributions continuously.
- Preserve entries and communications.
- Choose the percentage line whenever possible.
- Execute advanced techniques only after the necessary preparation has been completed.
