# Declarer Play

## Overview

Declarer play is the art of maximizing the number of tricks after winning the
contract.

Unlike defenders, declarer has complete control over both the declarer's hand
and dummy. Success depends on planning, counting, technique, timing,
communication, and accurate inference.

BridgeLab organizes declarer play into logical subject areas that progress from
fundamental planning to the most advanced technical play.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- develop a complete plan before playing to trick one,
- count winners, losers and entries,
- establish additional tricks,
- manage trump efficiently,
- maintain communication,
- execute advanced technical plays,
- combine technique with logical inference.

# Design Principles

## Plan Before Playing

Every contract should begin with a complete plan before playing from dummy.

---

## Count Continuously

Count

- winners,
- losers,
- entries,
- trumps,
- distribution,

throughout the hand.

---

## Technique Before Guessing

Apply sound technical methods before relying on intuition.

---

## Preserve Communication

Many contracts succeed or fail because of entry management.

Communication is often more valuable than raw strength.
