# Intra-Finesse Technique

## Overview

An **Intra-Finesse** is an advanced declarer-play technique that combines a **finesse** with a **deep finesse** to obtain more tricks than a normal finesse would produce. Declarer first takes a finesse against one missing honor and, if successful, later takes a second finesse against an intermediate honor in the same suit.

The technique is relatively uncommon but can produce an additional trick that cannot be obtained by ordinary play.

An Intra-Finesse requires careful planning, proper entries, and favorable card positions.

---

# Purpose

The Intra-Finesse allows declarer to:

- Win an extra trick in a suit.
- Maximize tricks from a powerful honor combination.
- Exploit favorable honor locations.
- Improve slam chances.
- Combine multiple finesse techniques.

---

# Basic Principle

The guiding principle is:

> **Use one successful finesse to create the opportunity for another finesse in the same suit.**

Instead of simply taking one finesse, declarer plans a sequence of plays that attacks multiple missing honors.

---

# When to Use

An Intra-Finesse is appropriate when:

- Two or more honors are missing.
- Declarer holds a strong honor combination.
- Multiple entries are available.
- An extra trick is valuable.
- The suit layout supports successive finesses.

---

# Requirements

A successful Intra-Finesse usually requires:

- A favorable honor structure.
- Correct entries.
- Accurate timing.
- Proper communication.
- Suitable placement of the missing honors.

---

# Classic Example

Holding

```
Dummy
♠ A1098

Declarer
♠ Q32
```

Missing:

```
♠ KJ7654
```

Declarer leads a small spade toward dummy.

If East plays low:

Play the **10**.

If the 10 wins, later lead toward the **Queen**.

When the missing honors are favorably placed, declarer may score **three tricks** instead of two.

---

# Example 2 – Diamond Suit

Holding

```
Dummy
♦ AQ98

Declarer
♦ 543
```

Missing:

```
♦ KJ1076
```

Declarer first finesses the 9.

If successful, declarer later finesses the Queen against the King.

The combination of the two finesses may establish an additional winner.

---

# Example 3 – Slam Contract

Contract

```
6NT
```

Declarer needs one additional trick.

Rather than relying on a single finesse, declarer executes an Intra-Finesse to maximize tricks in one suit while preserving communication.

---

# Planning the Play

Before attempting an Intra-Finesse, ask:

- Which honors are missing?
- Can one finesse create another?
- Do I have enough entries?
- Is there a safer line?
- How many tricks do I need from the suit?

---

# Timing

Timing is critical.

Generally:

1. Preserve entries.
2. Take the first finesse.
3. Return to the appropriate hand.
4. Take the second finesse.
5. Cash established winners.

---

# Probability

The success of an Intra-Finesse depends on:

- The location of the missing honors.
- Suit distribution.
- Information from the auction.
- Defenders' signals.

Like all finesse techniques, the odds improve when declarer gathers additional information before committing to the play.

---

# Common Situations

Intra-Finesses most often occur:

- In slam contracts.
- In notrump contracts.
- When every trick is important.
- With strong honor combinations.
- After careful planning and counting.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Taking the honors in the wrong order.
- Losing an essential entry.
- Blocking the suit.
- Attempting the play without sufficient communication.
- Choosing the Intra-Finesse when a simpler winning line exists.

---

# Defensive Countermeasures

Defenders should:

- Conceal honor locations.
- Falsecard when appropriate.
- Preserve communication.
- Avoid revealing distribution.
- Count declarer's likely entries.

---

# Advantages

- May produce an extra trick unavailable by normal play.
- Maximizes strong honor combinations.
- Valuable in close games and slams.
- Rewards careful planning and accurate counting.

---

# Disadvantages

- Rarely available.
- Requires precise timing.
- Often entry-intensive.
- Depends on favorable honor locations.

# Memory Aid

**Intra-Finesse = "One Finesse Creates Another."**

Remember:

- **Plan the entire suit before playing the first card.**
- **Preserve the entries needed for both finesses.**
- **Use the first successful finesse to create a second opportunity.**
- **Compare this line with simpler percentage or safety plays.**
- **Because the position is uncommon, careful visualization is essential.**

The Intra-Finesse is one of bridge's more sophisticated finesse techniques. Although it arises infrequently, recognizing the opportunity can produce an extra trick that is unavailable through ordinary play. Mastering the Intra-Finesse requires a solid understanding of honor combinations, entry management, and declarer planning.
