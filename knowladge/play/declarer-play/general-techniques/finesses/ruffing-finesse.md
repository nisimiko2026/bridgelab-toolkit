# Ruffing Finesse Technique

## Overview

A **Ruffing Finesse** is an advanced declarer-play technique in which declarer attempts to win a trick by **ruffing after an opponent plays a higher honor**. Instead of taking a normal finesse with a lower honor, declarer uses a trump to win the trick if the missing honor appears from the correct opponent.

The Ruffing Finesse combines the principles of a **finesse** and a **ruff**, allowing declarer to gain an extra trick when a standard finesse is not possible or is less effective.

---

# Purpose

The Ruffing Finesse allows declarer to:

- Win a trick against a missing honor.
- Promote an otherwise losing card.
- Create additional tricks in slam contracts.
- Avoid losing control of the hand.
- Improve the odds of making the contract.

---

# Basic Principle

Instead of leading toward an honor:

1. Lead a lower honor from one hand.
2. If the opponent covers with the missing higher honor, ruff the trick.
3. If the opponent does not cover, allow the honor to win naturally.

The trump replaces the normal finesse.

---

# When to Use

A Ruffing Finesse is appropriate when:

- A trump fit has been established.
- Declarer can ruff in the opposite hand.
- A missing honor is expected to be with one opponent.
- Ruffing preserves or gains an extra trick.

---

# Requirements

A successful Ruffing Finesse usually requires:

- A trump contract.
- A suitable trump entry.
- A lower honor opposite shortness.
- The missing honor located favorably.
- Sufficient trump length to afford the ruff.

---

# Basic Example

## Holding

Dummy

```
♠ AQJ
```

Declarer (trumps)

```
♥ AKQ
♠ 4
```

Missing:

```
♠ K
```

Lead the ♠Q from dummy.

- If East covers with the ♠K, ruff with a heart.
- If East plays low, the ♠Q wins.

Either outcome is favorable.

# Planning the Play

Before attempting a Ruffing Finesse, ask:

- Which opponent is likely to hold the missing honor?
- Can I afford to ruff?
- Do I have enough trumps remaining?
- Is there a safer alternative?

---

# Timing

Timing is essential.

Generally:

1. Draw enough opposing trumps to prevent overruffs.
2. Preserve the necessary entry.
3. Execute the Ruffing Finesse.
4. Continue with the established winners.

---

# Probability

A Ruffing Finesse succeeds when:

- The missing honor is held by the opponent who is expected to cover.
- The opponent follows normal defensive practice.

As with any finesse, success often depends on the location of the missing honor.

---

# Common Situations

Ruffing Finesses frequently occur:

- In slam contracts.
- After a suit has been established.
- When declarer has a singleton opposite touching honors.
- When a normal finesse would lose control.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Attempting the finesse too early.
- Forgetting to draw enough opposing trumps.
- Ruffing too high unnecessarily.
- Misjudging the location of the missing honor.
- Losing the entry required to repeat the play.

---

# Defensive Countermeasures

Defenders should:

- Recognize the possibility of a Ruffing Finesse.
- Decide carefully whether to cover an honor.
- Count declarer's trumps.
- Preserve trump control whenever possible.

---

# Advantages

- Creates an extra trick.
- Combines two powerful techniques.
- Frequently succeeds in slam contracts.
- Preserves communication.
- Avoids some losing finesses.

---

# Disadvantages

- Requires a trump contract.
- Depends on the location of the missing honor.
- Timing is critical.
- Costs a trump when the honor is covered.

# Declarer's Checklist

Before attempting a Ruffing Finesse:

- Have enough enemy trumps been removed?
- Is the missing honor likely to be on my right?
- Can I afford the ruff?
- Do I have sufficient entries?
- Will the finesse gain a trick if successful?

---

# Memory Guide

## Four Steps

1. Lead the lower honor.
2. Watch for the cover.
3. Ruff if covered.
4. Win naturally if not covered.

---

# Memory Aid

**Ruffing Finesse = "Cover and I Ruff; Duck and I Win."**

Remember:

- **Requires a trump contract.**
- **Lead the lower honor from the long holding.**
- **If the missing honor appears, ruff it.**
- **If it is not covered, win the trick naturally.**
- **Often appears in slam play.**

The Ruffing Finesse is one of declarer's most elegant techniques. By combining a traditional finesse with the power of a trump, declarer can gain extra tricks that are unavailable through ordinary play, particularly in suit contracts and slam auctions.
