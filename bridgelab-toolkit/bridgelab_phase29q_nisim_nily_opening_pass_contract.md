## B–D. Formal contract, evidence and scope

Each family check records a typed `POSITIVE`, `NEGATIVE` or `UNRESOLVED` state, a concrete reason, provenance, and an opening call only where independently supported. The assessment records actual hand, HCP, lengths, Rule20, dealer, acting seat, vulnerability, opening position, all checks, result and final reason.

The reducer requires one check for every required family exactly once. Missing, duplicate or incomplete collections return OTHER_UNRESOLVED. With a complete collection:

1. A positive opening family produces OPEN_SUPPORTED or PARTNERSHIP_TREATMENT. Other unknown exclusions do not make a known opener Pass.
2. With no positive family, any unresolved check blocks Pass.
3. Only an explicit conjunction of all NEGATIVE checks produces PASS_SUPPORTED.

The algebra is tested using clearly marked synthetic evidence. The real-hand assessor derives its checks internally; it accepts no no-rule-match or production-status override. It never constructs a production RuleDecision or modifies a router.
