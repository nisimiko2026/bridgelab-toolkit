# Acronyms

## Overview

Bridge literature makes extensive use of abbreviations and acronyms for
conventions, bidding systems, card-play techniques, scoring methods, and
tournament terminology.

This reference provides the standard meanings of those abbreviations as used
throughout BridgeLab.

Where an acronym represents a convention or system, the corresponding article
contains the complete explanation.

# Notes

## Multiple Meanings

Some acronyms have different meanings in different bidding systems or bridge
publications. Where ambiguity exists, BridgeLab follows the most widely accepted
modern expert usage.

---

## Partnership Acronyms

Many expert partnerships invent private abbreviations for system notes. Those
partnership-specific terms are not included in this glossary unless they have
become established in bridge literature.

---

## Historical Acronyms

Older bridge books occasionally use abbreviations that are now obsolete. Where
they remain relevant to understanding historical systems or literature, they
may be included in future editions.
