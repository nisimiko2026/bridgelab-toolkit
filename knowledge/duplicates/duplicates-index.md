# Duplicate Bridge

## Overview

**Duplicate Bridge** is the world's most popular form of competitive contract
bridge.

Unlike rubber bridge, duplicate bridge minimizes the effect of the cards dealt
by having multiple partnerships play the same hands. Results are determined by
comparison with other pairs or teams that played identical deals.

Success in duplicate bridge depends not only on technical bridge skills but
also on

- scoring strategy,
- risk management,
- partnership agreements,
- tournament procedures,
- psychological judgement.

This section explains every aspect of duplicate bridge competition.
