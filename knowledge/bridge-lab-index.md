# About BridgeLab

BridgeLab is organized as an encyclopedia rather than a traditional textbook.

Each article focuses on a single concept, convention, technique, or strategic
idea and includes

- explanations,
- objectives,
- principles,
- examples,
- expert recommendations,
- common mistakes,
- related articles,
- historical perspective.

This modular approach allows readers to explore individual topics while building
a complete understanding of the game.

# Conventions

BridgeLab documents both widely played and expert-level conventions.

Where a convention has multiple published versions, the encyclopedia

- identifies the standard treatment,
- explains important variations,
- highlights partnership agreements,
- distinguishes established methods from partnership style.

# Expert Philosophy

BridgeLab emphasizes

- sound bridge principles,
- partnership understanding,
- practical judgment,
- modern expert methods,
- technically accurate descriptions,
- historical context.

Where no universal standard exists, articles clearly distinguish between
generally accepted practice and partnership-specific agreements.
