# Natural Rebids

A rebid is a player's **second bid** in the auction. Rebids refine the
description of the hand by showing additional strength, distribution, support,
or notrump values after the partnership has exchanged initial information.
Accurate rebids are essential for reaching the correct partscore, game, or slam.
