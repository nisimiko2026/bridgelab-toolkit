# Response to 2NT Opening

## Overview

The **2NT opening** describes one of the most accurately defined hands in
bridge.

Unlike suit openings, opener has already described

- strength,
- balanced distribution,
- limited hand,
- no singleton or void.

Because opener's hand is well defined, responder usually becomes **captain** of
the auction.

Responder's responsibilities are to determine

- whether a major-suit fit exists,
- whether game or slam is appropriate,
- whether notrump is the best contract,
- whether transfers or Stayman should be used.

---

# Meaning of a 2NT Opening

Most partnerships define a 2NT opening as

- **20–21 HCP**
- balanced distribution

Typical shapes include

- 4-3-3-3
- 4-4-3-2
- 5-3-3-2

Some partnerships use

- 21–22 HCP
- 19–21 HCP

depending on their 1NT range.

---

# Objectives

Responder should

- locate a major-suit fit,
- evaluate slam potential,
- choose between suit and notrump,
- preserve bidding space,
- become captain of the auction.

---

# General Priorities

Responder normally follows this order

1. Search for a major fit.
2. Evaluate slam.
3. Choose between suit and notrump.
4. Use transfer bids whenever appropriate.
5. Preserve bidding space.

---

# Strength Categories

| Strength | Typical Action |
|----------|----------------|
| 0–3 HCP | Sign off |
| 4–5 HCP | Invitational (rare) |
| 6–10 HCP | Game |
| 11+ HCP | Slam investigation |

Because opener already has about 20–21 HCP, game is frequently assured.

---

# Natural Responses

## Pass

Rare.

Normally only with

- extremely weak hand,
- partnership agreement.

Most partnerships continue bidding.

---

## 3NT

Natural.

Shows

- balanced hand,
- no major fit,
- no slam interest.

---

# Stayman

The primary convention.

```
2NT
3♣
```

Artificial.

Requests

- four-card major(s).

---

## Opener's Replies

```
3♦
```

No four-card major.

---

```
3♥
```

Four hearts.

---

```
3♠
```

Four spades.

---

# Stayman Continuations

Responder may

- sign off,
- bid game,
- investigate slam,
- employ Smolen,
- use Puppet Stayman continuations.

# Jacoby Transfers

Transfers remain standard.

```
2NT
3♦
```

Transfer to hearts.

```

- conceal opener's honors,
- improve contract placement.

Game-going transfers.

```

```

```

# Minor-Suit Transfers

Many expert partnerships play

```
4♣
```

Transfer to diamonds.

```
4♦
```

Transfer to clubs.

Others use different structures.

Agreement required.

---

# Puppet Stayman

Widely used after strong notrump openings.

Purpose

- locate five-card majors,
- locate 4-4 fits,
- improve slam bidding.

Typical sequence

```
2NT
3♣
```

Artificial.

Opener follows the agreed Puppet structure.

---

# Smolen

After Stayman denies a major,

Smolen allows responder to show

- five cards in one major,
- four cards in the other,
- game-forcing values.

---

# Quantitative Invitation

```
2NT
4NT
```

Shows

- approximately 11–12 HCP,
- balanced hand,
- asks opener to bid slam with a maximum.

Not Blackwood.

---

# Gerber

Some partnerships play

```
2NT
4♣
```

as Gerber.

Requests aces.

Others reserve 4♣ for transfers.

Agreement is essential.

---

# Slam Investigation

Because opener is already strong,

many auctions continue toward slam.

Common methods include

- cue bids,
- control bids,
- Roman Key Card Blackwood,
- Quantitative 4NT,
- Gerber,
- Exclusion,
- Grand Slam Force.

---

# Balanced Hands

Balanced responders often

- use Stayman,
- bid 3NT,
- invite slam,
- investigate notrump slams.

---

# Unbalanced Hands

Distributional responders

- transfer,
- establish a trump suit,
- show shortness,
- cue bid controls.

Suit slams are common.

---

# Major-Suit Fits

Finding a 4-4 or 5-3 major fit usually takes precedence over playing 3NT.

Suit contracts frequently produce additional tricks through ruffing.

---

# Minor-Suit Contracts

Minor contracts occur less frequently.

Responder normally considers

- 3NT,
- six of a minor,
- notrump slam

before choosing five of a minor.

---

# Competitive Auctions

After interference,

many partnerships employ

- Lebensohl,
- Rubensohl,
- Transfer Lebensohl,
- cue bids,
- doubles.

Partnership agreement is critical.

---

# Summary

Responding to a **2NT opening** is primarily an exercise in **game and slam
selection**, rather than determining whether game should be reached. Since
opener has already described a strong, balanced hand, responder becomes captain
and directs the auction toward the optimum contract.

Modern response structures—centered on **Stayman**, **Jacoby Transfers**,
**Texas Transfers**, **Puppet Stayman**, **Smolen**, and **Quantitative 4NT**—
allow partnerships to identify major-suit fits, preserve declarership, and
conduct highly accurate slam investigations while making efficient use of the
available bidding space.
