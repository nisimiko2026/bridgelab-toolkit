# Response to Three-Level Preempt

## Overview

Responding to a **three-level preemptive opening** is primarily an exercise in
judgment rather than investigation.

Unlike one-level openings, opener has already described

- a limited hand,
- a long suit,
- strong offensive distribution,
- preemptive intentions.

Responder's objective is not to explore the hand in detail, but to determine
whether the partnership should

- pass,
- compete,
- bid game,
- sacrifice,
- or, on rare occasions, investigate slam.

Because opener has already limited the hand, **responder becomes captain of the
auction.**

---

# Meaning of a Three-Level Preempt

A natural three-level opening normally promises

- 5–10 HCP,
- a good seven-card suit,
- offensive distribution,
- no suitable one-level or Weak Two opening.

The bid is

- natural,
- preemptive,
- limited.

---

# Objectives

Responder should

- evaluate game prospects,
- judge offensive potential,
- decide whether to compete,
- protect the partnership from unnecessary penalties,
- preserve the preemptive value of the opening.

---

# Evaluating the Hand

Responder considers

- High Card Points,
- trump support,
- controls,
- distribution,
- playing tricks,
- vulnerability,
- offensive versus defensive values.

Distribution usually outweighs HCP.

---

# Typical Strength Categories

| Strength | Typical Action |
|----------|----------------|
| 0–11 HCP | Usually Pass |
| 12–15 HCP | Compete or evaluate game |
| 16+ HCP | Serious game investigation |
| Exceptional | Rare slam interest |

---

# Passing

The most common action.

Example

```
3♠
Pass
```

Responder passes with

- little support,
- limited values,
- no realistic game prospects.

---

# Raising Partner's Suit

Example

```
3♥
4♥
```

Shows

- excellent trump support,
- offensive values,
- expectation that game is likely.

Raises are based more on

- fit,
- distribution,
- playing strength

than on raw HCP.

---

# Raising Minor Preempts

Example

```
3♦
5♦
```

Shows

- strong trump support,
- confidence that the partnership can make game.

Before choosing five of a minor, responder should always consider

```
3NT
```

---

# New Suit Response

Example

```
3♠
4♣
```

Usually shows

- a natural suit,
- strong values,
- game interest.

Because bidding space is limited, new suits are forcing in most partnerships.

---

# Notrump Contracts

Responder may bid

```
3NT
```

after a minor-suit preempt when holding

- reliable stoppers,
- strong outside values,
- confidence that opener's suit will provide sufficient tricks.

Example

```
3♣
3NT
```

---

# Game Decisions

Responder should aggressively evaluate game when holding

- excellent trump support,
- controls,
- shortness,
- offensive distribution.

Distribution frequently upgrades responder's hand.

---

# Slam Investigation

Rare.

Slam should be investigated only when responder has

- exceptional controls,
- outstanding trump support,
- significant extra strength.

The lack of bidding space usually limits detailed investigation.

---

# Sacrifice Decisions

Three-level preempts often create profitable sacrifices.

Responder should evaluate

- vulnerability,
- expected defensive tricks,
- offensive potential,
- likely opponent contract.

Judgment is considerably more important than point count.

---

# Competitive Auctions

When opponents enter the auction,

responder evaluates

- fit,
- vulnerability,
- sacrifice potential,
- defensive values.

Common actions include

- raise,
- double,
- cue bid,
- natural suit bid.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Partners should define

- forcing new suits,
- game raises,
- sacrifice philosophy,
- competitive doubles,
- cue bids,
- vulnerability style.

---

# Modern Expert Style

Most expert partnerships employ

- disciplined game raises,
- natural forcing new suits,
- aggressive sacrifice evaluation,
- offensive judgment,
- vulnerability adjustments.

Little artificial bidding is used because the opening has already consumed
significant bidding space.

## Overestimating Opener's Strength

Remember that opener has already limited the hand.

---

## Ignoring Distribution

Fit and shape frequently outweigh HCP.

---


Experts evaluate

- playing tricks,
- trump support,
- controls,
- offensive potential,
- vulnerability,

Responder should

- assume captaincy,
- preserve the preemptive effect,
- evaluate offensive strength,
- bid game aggressively with strong fits,
- avoid unnecessary investigation.

# Summary

Responding to a **three-level preempt** is based primarily on **judgment** rather
than detailed hand investigation. Since opener has already shown a **limited
hand with a long seven-card suit**, responder's role is to decide whether the
partnership should pass, compete, bid game, or sacrifice.

Successful responses depend on accurate evaluation of **trump support**,
**distribution**, **playing strength**, **vulnerability**, and **offensive
potential**. Unlike Weak Two auctions, artificial methods are uncommon because
the high-level opening has already consumed most of the available bidding
space, making practical decision-making the key to success.
