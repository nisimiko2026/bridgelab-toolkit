# Natural Opening Bids

Natural opening bids form the foundation of constructive bidding. They describe
the opening hand, establish the auction, and provide the framework for all
subsequent bidding decisions. This section covers opening requirements, standard
natural openings, preemptive openings, and strong artificial openings commonly
used within natural bidding systems.
