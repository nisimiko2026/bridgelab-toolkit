# Opening Bids

> *The first description of your hand and the foundation of every auction.*

Opening bids establish the direction of the auction and provide partner with the
first information about strength, distribution, and suit preference. Every
successful bidding system begins with sound opening-bid principles.

The **Opening Bids** section of BridgeLab explains natural openings, artificial
openings, preemptive openings, strong openings, and modern partnership styles
used throughout contemporary bridge.

---

# Introduction

An opening bid serves several purposes simultaneously.

It should

- describe the hand,
- establish the partnership's strength,
- identify a possible trump suit,
- consume bidding space efficiently,
- prepare for future rebids,
- interfere with the opponents when appropriate.

Good opening bids balance descriptive accuracy with practical judgment.

---

# Objectives

Opening bids are designed to

- communicate strength,
- describe distribution,
- identify long suits,
- locate major-suit fits,
- begin constructive auctions,
- establish forcing or non-forcing sequences,
- support partnership agreements.

# Fundamental Principles

Successful opening bids

- describe rather than conceal,
- leave room for later bidding,
- fit the partnership system,
- consider vulnerability,
- anticipate rebids,
- support constructive auctions.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Opening unsuitable balanced hands.
- Overvaluing unsupported honors.
- Ignoring suit quality.
- Preempting too aggressively when vulnerable.
- Opening light without partnership agreement.
- Confusing natural and artificial openings.
