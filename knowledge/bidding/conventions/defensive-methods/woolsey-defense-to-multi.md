# Woolsey Defense to Multi

## Overview

The **Woolsey Defense** is an expert defensive system designed to combat the
artificial **Multi 2♦** opening. Developed by **Kit Woolsey**, it is widely
played in high-level tournament bridge because it immediately distinguishes
between balanced hands, one-suited hands, and two-suited hands while allowing
the partnership to compete aggressively despite the uncertainty created by the
Multi opening.

Unlike purely natural defenses, Woolsey assigns conventional meanings to
several calls, maximizing descriptive accuracy and minimizing guesswork after
the opponents conceal their actual major suit.

Today, Woolsey is regarded as one of the standard expert defenses to Multi 2♦.

---

# Objectives

The Woolsey Defense aims to

- compete immediately against weak Multi openings,
- handle both possible major suits,
- distinguish balanced and distributional hands,
- identify two-suited hands efficiently,
- preserve penalty opportunities against strong Multi variants,
- maximize bidding accuracy.

---

# The Multi 2♦ Opening

The classical Multi 2♦ promises one of

- Weak Two Hearts
- Weak Two Spades

Many partnerships also include

- Strong balanced hand
- Strong one-suiter
- Strong two-suiter

Always verify the opponents' convention card before applying any defense.

---

# Basic Structure

The most common Woolsey structure is

| Call | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Double | 15–18 balanced or any very strong hand |
| 2♥ | Natural hearts |
| 2♠ | Natural spades |
| 2NT | Both minors (5-5 or better) |
| 3♣ | Clubs + a major |
| 3♦ | Diamonds + a major |
| 3♥ | Strong natural |
| 3♠ | Strong natural |
| 3NT | Natural |

This is the version most frequently encountered in expert partnerships.

# Double

## Meaning

Double shows

- 15–18 balanced,

or

- any hand too strong for a natural overcall.

Some partnerships widen the range to

```
14–18
```

depending upon vulnerability.

---

## Philosophy

Unlike a penalty double,

the Woolsey double initially shows

**values**.

The partnership determines later whether to defend or compete.

---

# Responses to Double

Responder normally bids naturally.

Typical continuations

```

Pass
```

Responder may

- bid a suit,
- bid 2NT,
- pass with defensive strength,
- cue bid after clarification.

# Why Woolsey Works

The defense separates

- balanced hands,
- natural overcalls,
- minor two-suiters,
- major/minor two-suiters.

This greatly simplifies competitive auctions after opener clarifies the major.

# Modern Expert Variations

There is **no single universal Woolsey Defense**. Three major approaches are
played internationally.

## Classical Woolsey

- Double = 15–18 balanced
- 2NT = Both minors
- 3♣ = Clubs + Major
- 3♦ = Diamonds + Major

---

## Strong-Hand Woolsey

Some partnerships broaden the double to include

- any 19+ hand,
- forcing hands unsuitable for natural overcalls.

---

## Transfer Woolsey

A few expert pairs replace the natural overcalls with transfer advances after
the double or adopt transfer responses to increase descriptive accuracy. These
methods are uncommon and require extensive partnership discussion.

---

# Expert Partnership Agreements

Before using Woolsey Defense, partners should explicitly agree on:

- Exact HCP range for the double
- Whether the double may contain any strong hand
- Minimum suit quality for natural overcalls
- Whether 2NT promises exactly 5-5 or may include 5-4
- Meaning of 3♣ and 3♦
- Continuations after opener identifies the major
- Actions against strong Multi variants
- Balancing and reopening style

---

# Historical Perspective

The Woolsey Defense was developed by **Kit Woolsey** as a practical response to
the growing popularity of the Multi 2♦ convention. Rather than relying solely
on natural overcalls, the defense uses a structured set of conventional calls
to classify balanced hands, natural suits, and two-suited distributions. Its
clarity and flexibility have made it one of the preferred expert methods for
countering the uncertainty created by the Multi opening.

---

# Summary

The **Woolsey Defense** is one of the leading expert defenses to the **Multi
2♦** convention. By assigning specific meanings to doubles, natural overcalls,
and two-suited bids, it enables the defenders to compete effectively despite
the hidden major suit.

Although several variations exist, the central philosophy remains the same:
describe the hand as accurately as possible while preserving flexibility until
the opponents reveal their actual suit. With clear partnership agreements,
Woolsey provides a powerful and reliable framework for defending against one of
bridge's most popular artificial openings.
