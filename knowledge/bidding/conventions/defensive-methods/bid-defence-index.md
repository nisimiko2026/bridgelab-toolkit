# Defensive Bidding

> *Competing effectively after the opponents open the bidding.*

Defensive bidding encompasses the methods used when the opponents open the
auction. Its objective is not merely to interfere, but to compete accurately,
identify the best contract, locate profitable sacrifices, and make constructive
use of limited bidding space despite opponent interference.

Modern defensive bidding combines natural overcalls, conventional defenses,
competitive doubles, partnership agreements, and sound judgment.

---

# Introduction

Unlike constructive bidding, defensive bidding begins with the opponents
holding the initiative.

The partnership must decide whether to

- compete,
- overcall,
- double,
- sacrifice,
- defend,
- or remain silent.

These decisions depend upon

- hand strength,
- distribution,
- vulnerability,
- partnership style,
- scoring method,
- the opponents' system.

---

# Objectives

Defensive bidding seeks to

- enter the auction constructively,
- obstruct the opponents,
- locate partnership fits,
- describe offensive hands,
- identify defensive opportunities,
- improve competitive decisions,
- maximize expected score.

# Vulnerability

Competitive style should adjust to vulnerability.

## Favorable

- Stretch more aggressively.
- Sacrifice more freely.
- Enter the auction more often.

---

## Equal

Apply normal partnership style.

---

## Unfavorable

- Require better suits.
- Avoid speculative actions.
- Emphasize defense.

---

# Matchpoints versus IMPs

## Matchpoints

- Fight for partscore.
- Compete aggressively.
- Thin sacrifices may succeed.

---

## IMPs

- Avoid expensive penalties.
- Emphasize vulnerable games.
- Exercise greater discipline.
