# Kokish Relay

## Overview

The **Kokish Relay** is an advanced relay treatment used after a **strong 2♣
opening**. Developed by **Eric Kokish**, it allows opener to distinguish
between **balanced hands** and **strong unbalanced hands** while consuming
minimal bidding space.

The key idea is that opener's **2♥ rebid is completely artificial**. Rather than
showing hearts, it instructs responder to bid **2♠**, after which opener
clarifies the true nature of the hand.

The convention has become one of the most popular expert treatments following a
strong 2♣ opening and is widely played in

- Standard American
- Two-over-One Game Force
- Precision (modified)
- Expert relay systems

---

# Objectives

The Kokish Relay enables the partnership to

- distinguish balanced and unbalanced hands,
- preserve bidding space,
- improve slam investigation,
- simplify later auctions,
- keep the auction low,
- provide a single relay structure.

# The Basic Auction

```
Opener      Responder

2♣
2♦          (waiting)
2♥*         (artificial)
2♠*         (forced relay)
2NT / 3♣ / 3♦ / 3♥ / 3♠
```

The bids

```
2♥

2♠
```

carry **no natural meaning**.

They are relay bids.

# Historical Perspective

Eric Kokish introduced this relay treatment to improve the efficiency of
auctions following a strong **2♣** opening. By making **2♥** an artificial relay
rather than a natural heart bid, the partnership gains an entire level of
bidding space before opener reveals whether the hand is balanced or
distributional.

The convention quickly became popular among expert partnerships because it
solves a long-standing problem: balancing the need to show a strong balanced
hand while preserving enough room to describe powerful one-suited and
two-suited hands accurately. Today, Kokish Relay is a standard feature of many
expert natural and strong-club systems.

---

# Expert Notes

The Kokish Relay is **not** a complete relay system. It is a **specific relay
treatment after a strong 2♣ opening**. It should not be confused with
**Symmetric Relay**, **MOSCITO Relay**, or other full relay bidding systems.

After opener's artificial **2♥** rebid, responder is expected to bid **2♠**
automatically, allowing opener to clarify the hand type while conserving
bidding space. This simple mechanism has become one of the most successful
expert enhancements to the standard strong **2♣** structure.

# Summary

The **Kokish Relay** is an expert relay treatment used after a **strong 2♣**
opening to distinguish **22–24 balanced hands** from powerful unbalanced hands
without sacrificing valuable bidding space. Its hallmark is the artificial
**2♥–2♠** relay sequence, after which opener accurately describes the hand.

Simple in concept yet highly effective, the Kokish Relay has become one of the
most widely adopted expert improvements to strong **2♣** bidding, providing
greater flexibility, improved slam investigation, and more descriptive
constructive auctions.
