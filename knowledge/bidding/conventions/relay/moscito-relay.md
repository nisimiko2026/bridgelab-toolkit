# MOSCITO Relay

## Purpose

**MOSCITO Relay** is the relay bidding method used in the **MOSCITO** bidding system.

Like other advanced relay methods, it assigns one player as the **Captain** and the other as the **Describer**. Through a sequence of artificial relay bids, the Captain learns responder's:

- Strength
- Distribution
- Suit lengths
- Controls
- Honor location
- Slam potential

The objective is to identify the best contract while preserving maximum bidding space.

---

# Background

**MOSCITO** stands for:

> **Major Oriented Strong Club – Intermediate Two Openings**

The system was developed primarily in Australia and New Zealand and is well known for combining:

- Strong Club methods
- Relay bidding
- Transfer responses
- Highly descriptive auctions

The relay structure is one of its defining features.

---

# Captain–Describer Principle

## Captain

The Captain:

- Directs the auction
- Asks questions through relay bids
- Chooses the final contract

## Describer

The Describer:

- Answers using predefined step responses
- Reveals information in a fixed order
- Does not make judgment bids

---

# Relay Structure

A typical relay sequence might begin:

```text
1♣
1♦   Relay
1♥   Step 1
1♠   Relay
2♣   Step 2
2♦   Relay
...
```

Each relay requests the next piece of information.

---

# Information Order

Although partnership styles vary, relay responses commonly describe:

1. Strength
2. Distribution
3. Exact suit lengths
4. Controls
5. Honor location
6. Slam features

Each step has a predetermined meaning.

---

# Shape Description

MOSCITO Relay aims to identify responder's exact pattern as efficiently as possible.

Typical classifications include:

- Balanced hands
- One-suited hands
- Two-suited hands
- Three-suited hands
- Exact 4-4-3-2, 5-3-3-2, etc.

Eventually the Captain often knows responder's complete distribution.

---

# Slam Investigation

Once shape has been established, relay bidding continues to investigate:

- Aces
- Kings
- Controls
- Key cards
- Queens
- Specific honor combinations

This allows extremely accurate slam bidding.

---

# Interference

Most MOSCITO partnerships adopt special agreements for relay interference, including:

- Pass as relay
- Double as relay
- Step substitutions
- Broken relay procedures

The exact methods depend on partnership agreement.

---

# Advantages

- Extremely precise hand description
- Outstanding slam bidding
- Efficient use of bidding space
- Standardized relay philosophy
- Excellent for strong club systems

---

# Disadvantages

- Very memory intensive
- Requires extensive partnership practice
- Vulnerable to competitive interference
- Unsuitable for casual partnerships

# Summary

**MOSCITO Relay** is the relay framework used within the **MOSCITO bidding system**. By combining Captain–Describer principles with standardized step responses, it enables the partnership to exchange exceptionally precise information about strength, distribution, and controls, making it one of the most sophisticated methods for game and slam bidding.
