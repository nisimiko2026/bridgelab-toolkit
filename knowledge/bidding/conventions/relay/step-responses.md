# Step Responses

## Overview

**Step Responses** are one of the fundamental building blocks of modern
artificial bidding systems. Instead of assigning a natural meaning to each bid,
the partnership uses **successive bidding steps** to convey structured
information.

Each step represents a predefined answer to a relay question. The meaning of a
step depends entirely upon the partnership agreement and the stage of the
auction.

Step Responses are used extensively in

- Relay systems
- Strong Club systems
- Symmetric Relay
- MOSCITO
- Kokish Relay continuations
- Control-asking sequences
- Shape relays
- Key-card relays

Although the exact meanings differ between systems, the underlying principle is
universal.

---

# Objectives

Step Responses allow the partnership to

- exchange information efficiently,
- preserve bidding space,
- eliminate ambiguity,
- describe hands systematically,
- support relay bidding,
- improve slam bidding.

---

# Fundamental Principle

Each available bid represents

a **step**.

Example

```
1st Step

2nd Step

3rd Step

4th Step
```

Each step corresponds to

a predetermined answer.

Unlike natural bidding,

the bid itself usually has no intrinsic meaning.

---

# What Is a Step?

A step is simply

the next available legal bid.

Example

```
2♦

2♥

2♠

2NT

3♣

3♦
```

Each bid represents

one additional step.

---

# First Step

Normally

the cheapest available bid.

Frequently used for

- minimum,
- weakest response,
- first shape,
- first control range.

---

# Second Step

Usually

the next available bid.

May represent

- stronger hand,
- second shape,
- additional controls.

---

# Higher Steps

Successive bids continue the agreed sequence.

The partnership may identify

- exact shape,
- controls,
- honor location,
- suit length,
- key cards.

---

# Why Use Steps?

Natural bidding quickly consumes space.

Step responses

compress information.

Instead of requiring

multiple natural bids,

one relay question

may identify

- strength,
- distribution,
- controls.

---

2. Strength
3. Controls

# Historical Perspective

Step Responses evolved alongside relay bidding during the development of strong
club systems in the latter half of the twentieth century. As partnerships sought
more accurate methods for describing shape, controls, and honor holdings, they
replaced natural meanings with structured response scales. These step-based
methods became the foundation of advanced relay systems and remain essential to
modern expert bidding.

---

# Expert Notes

Unlike conventions such as Stayman or Blackwood, **Step Responses are not a
convention**. They are a **communication technique** used within artificial
bidding systems. Every relay system defines its own step structure, making
partnership agreement essential. While the meanings differ from one system to
another, the underlying principle—using successive bids as ordered responses—is
common to virtually all advanced relay methods.

---

# Summary

**Step Responses** provide the structured language used by relay bidding
systems. By assigning ordered meanings to successive bidding steps, they allow
partnerships to exchange detailed information about shape, strength, controls,
and honor location while preserving valuable bidding space.

Although the exact step tables vary among systems, the concept is universal:
each bid represents the next answer in a predefined sequence. Together with the
Relay Principle, Step Responses form the foundation of modern expert relay
bidding.
