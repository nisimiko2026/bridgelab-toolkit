# Two-Way Checkback

## Overview

**Two-Way Checkback** is an advanced artificial checkback convention used after
opener rebids **1NT** following a natural one-level opening and responder's
one-level suit response.

Unlike **Checkback Stayman** or **New Minor Forcing**, which employ a single
artificial inquiry, Two-Way Checkback uses **two artificial relay bids** to
separate **invitational** hands from **game-forcing** hands before exchanging
further descriptive information.

The convention has become one of the preferred methods in modern
**Two-over-One Game Force**, Precision, and expert natural systems because it

- finds 5-3 and 4-4 major-suit fits,
- distinguishes invitational and forcing auctions immediately,
- allows precise description of shape,
- preserves maximum bidding space,
- integrates naturally with slam investigation.

---

# Objectives

Two-Way Checkback enables responder to

- distinguish invitational and game-forcing hands,
- locate undisclosed major-suit fits,
- determine opener's exact distribution,
- investigate notrump and minor-suit contracts,
- preserve bidding space,
- improve slam exploration.

# Basic Philosophy

Instead of using a single artificial inquiry,

Two-Way Checkback assigns different meanings to

```
2♣
```

and

```
2♦
```

One bid establishes

- invitational values.

The other establishes

- game-forcing values.

Responder immediately informs opener how far the auction is expected to continue.

---

# Typical Expert Structure

The most common treatment is

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **2♣** | Artificial checkback, invitational or one-round force |
| **2♦** | Artificial game-force checkback |

Many partnerships reverse these meanings.

The convention itself is the same.

# Requirements

## Invitational Sequence

Typically

- 10–11 HCP,
- game interest,
- possible major fit,
- balanced or semi-balanced hand.

---

## Game-Forcing Sequence

Typically

- 12+ HCP,
- forcing to game,
- possible slam interest,
- interest in further description.

# Why Play Two-Way Checkback?

Traditional Checkback Stayman cannot immediately distinguish

- invitational hands,
- game-forcing hands.

Two-Way Checkback solves this problem by separating them at the first decision.

# Opener's Priorities

Regardless of partnership style, opener normally answers in the following order

1. Three-card support for responder's major.
2. Four-card unbid major.
3. Five-card opening suit.
4. Minimum or maximum strength.
5. Balanced pattern.
6. Additional distribution.

Finding the correct major-suit fit always has priority.

---

Modern expert partnerships generally follow four priorities

1. Find the best major-suit fit.
2. Clarify strength immediately.
3. Describe shape economically.
4. Preserve bidding space.

# Modern Expert Treatment

Today, **Two-Way Checkback** is considered one of the most flexible methods
available after opener's **1NT rebid**. Many expert partnerships have replaced
traditional Checkback Stayman or New Minor Forcing entirely with Two-Way
Checkback because it combines

- immediate strength clarification,
- efficient fit discovery,
- economical bidding,
- and excellent slam potential.

Although exact relay meanings differ among partnerships, the underlying
principle is universal:

> **Separate invitational and game-forcing hands before exchanging descriptive
> information.**

---

# Expert Partnership Styles

There is **no universally accepted response table**.

The three most common expert approaches are

### Style 1 (Most Popular)

- **2♣** = Invitational Checkback
- **2♦** = Game-Forcing Checkback

### Style 2

- **2♣** = Universal relay
- **2♦** = Natural or system-dependent continuation

### Style 3

Two-Way Checkback is merged into a broader **relay structure**, often replacing
both New Minor Forcing and traditional Checkback Stayman.

Partnership agreement is essential.

---

# Summary

**Two-Way Checkback** is one of the premier modern conventions for auctions in
which opener rebids **1NT**. By using two artificial checkback bids to separate
**invitational** and **game-forcing** hands, it provides exceptional precision
in locating major-suit fits, describing hand strength, and preserving bidding
space.

Its flexibility and efficiency have made it a favorite among expert
partnerships, particularly in **Two-over-One Game Force** and relay-oriented
systems, where accurate hand evaluation and economical bidding are paramount.
