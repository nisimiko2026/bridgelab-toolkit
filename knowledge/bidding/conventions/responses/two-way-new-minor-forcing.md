# Two-Way New Minor Forcing

## Overview

**Two-Way New Minor Forcing (2WNMF)** is an advanced extension of the **New
Minor Forcing (NMF)** convention. It is used after opener rebids **1NT**
following a one-level suit opening and responder's one-level response.

Unlike standard New Minor Forcing, which uses a single artificial bid, Two-Way
New Minor Forcing employs **both the two-level and three-level bids of the
unbid minor** to distinguish **invitational** hands from **game-forcing**
hands.

The convention allows partnerships to investigate

- major-suit fits,
- notrump contracts,
- minor-suit contracts,
- game,
- and slam,

while preserving valuable bidding space.

---

# Objectives

The convention enables responder to

- distinguish invitational and game-forcing hands,
- locate undisclosed major-suit fits,
- determine opener's exact distribution,
- invite or force game efficiently,
- investigate slam,
- preserve bidding space.

---

# Basic Principle

After

```
1♣      Pass
1♥      Pass
1NT     Pass
?
```

Responder bids the **unbid minor** artificially.

The level of the bid determines responder's strength.

Typical expert treatment

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 2♦ | Invitational |
| 3♦ | Game Forcing |

The same principle applies whenever the auction qualifies.

```
1♦

1NT
```

```
1♦
1♠
1NT
```

The convention applies only when

- opener rebids **1NT**,
- responder has bid a suit,
- one minor remains unbid.

---

# Requirements

Responder typically has

### Invitational Sequence

- approximately 10–11 HCP
- game interest
- possible major fit
- or notrump interest

---

### Game-Forcing Sequence

- 12+ HCP
- game force
- possible slam interest
- interest in further exploration

---

# Why Play Two-Way NMF?

Standard New Minor Forcing cannot distinguish

- invitation
- game force

without consuming additional bidding space.

Two-Way NMF solves this problem elegantly.

---

# Comparison with Standard NMF

| Feature | Standard NMF | Two-Way NMF |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Artificial bids | One | Two |
| Invitational hands | Yes | Yes |
| Game forcing | Indirect | Direct |
| Slam investigation | Limited | Excellent |
| Space available | Less | More |

---

# Opener's Priorities

After the artificial inquiry opener should answer in priority order

1. Three-card support for responder's major
2. Four-card support
3. Four-card unbid major
4. Minimum or maximum strength
5. Natural notrump
6. Minor-suit preference

---

# Showing Three-Card Support

Example

```
1♦
1♥
1NT
2♣*
2♥
```

Shows

- three-card heart support.

Finding a 5-3 fit is opener's highest priority.

---

# Showing Four Cards

Example

```
1♣
1♠
1NT
2♦*
2♥
```

Shows

- four hearts.

Responder now evaluates

- 4-4 fit
- 5-3 fit
- notrump.

---

# Showing No Major Fit

Without support,

opener rebids naturally.

Example

```
1♦
1♠
1NT
2♣*
2NT
```

Denies

- three-card support,
- four-card unbid major.

---

# Invitational Continuations

After

```
1♦
1♠
1NT
2♣*
```

Responder may

- pass,
- invite,
- sign off,
- choose notrump,
- play a minor.

- strain,

- slam.

# Partnership Agreements

Partners should define

- which bid is invitational,
- which bid is forcing,
- opener's response priorities,
- strength ranges,
- continuations,

# Competition

If opponents interfere,

partnership agreement determines whether

- Two-Way NMF remains on,
- doubles replace artificial bids,
- cue bids substitute.

Many partnerships suspend the convention.

---

# Expert Style

Most expert partnerships use the following priorities

1. Show support.
2. Show an unbid major.
3. Clarify strength.
4. Clarify shape.

Information is given economically.

# Modern Expert Treatment

Modern tournament partnerships generally consider **Two-Way New Minor Forcing**
to be a natural evolution of standard NMF. By separating **invitational** and
**game-forcing** hands at the outset, the convention allows opener to exchange
descriptive information without wasting bidding space.

Expert priorities are

1. Find a major-suit fit.
2. Distinguish invitational from forcing auctions.
3. Describe shape economically.
4. Preserve room for slam investigation.

This philosophy integrates seamlessly with **Two-over-One Game Force**, **Two-Way
Checkback**, and modern relay-oriented constructive bidding systems.

---

# Summary

**Two-Way New Minor Forcing** is one of the most effective modern continuations
after opener rebids **1NT**. By using **both levels of the unbid minor** to
distinguish invitational and game-forcing hands, it greatly improves the
partnership's ability to locate major-suit fits, evaluate notrump contracts,
and conduct accurate game and slam investigations.

It is now a popular expert treatment among tournament partnerships seeking
greater precision than standard New Minor Forcing while remaining relatively
simple to learn and apply.
