# Rosenkranz Redouble

## Purpose

The **Rosenkranz Redouble** is the companion convention to the **Rosenkranz Double**.

It is used when:

- Partner makes a **natural suit overcall**.
- The next opponent **doubles** that overcall.
- You **redouble**.

The redouble is **artificial** and shows:

- Support for partner's suit.
- A top honor (usually the **Ace or King**) in that suit.

It helps partner evaluate whether to compete further and provides valuable lead information if the opponents become declarer.

---

# When It Applies

Typical auction:

```text
West     North     East     South

1♦        1♠        Double     Redouble
```

South's **Redouble** is **not** for business or penalties.

Instead, it shows:

- Support for spades.
- The ♠A or ♠K (partnership agreement).

---

# Basic Principle

The redouble tells partner:

> "I have support for your suit, and I hold one of the top honors."

This gives partner confidence that:

- The suit is likely to play well.
- Offensive values are concentrated in the agreed suit.
- A lead of the suit will often be attractive if defending.

---

# Requirements

Typical agreements:

- At least **three-card support**.
- The **Ace or King** of partner's suit.
- Competitive or constructive values.

Some partnerships require:

- Four-card support.
- Opening values.

These are partnership agreements.

---

# Example

South holds:

```
♠ K95
♥ Q84
♦ J73
♣ AQ72
```

Auction:

```text
West     North     East     South

1♦        1♠        Double     Redouble
```

South shows:

- Spade support.
- The ♠K.

North can compete more confidently.

---

# Continuations

After the Rosenkranz Redouble:

Partner may:

- Compete further.
- Invite game.
- Defend.
- Double later for penalties if appropriate.

Subsequent bidding is generally natural.

---

# Lead-Directing Value

If the opponents buy the contract:

```
4♥ by West
```

North knows South has:

- Support.
- A top spade honor.

A spade lead is therefore attractive.

---

# Advantages

- Shows constructive support.
- Improves competitive judgment.
- Provides useful lead information.
- Distinguishes supportive hands from other redoubles.

---

# Disadvantages

- Gives information to the opponents.
- Sacrifices the natural meaning of the redouble.
- Requires explicit partnership agreement.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Whether the honor promised is:
  - Ace only
  - Ace or King
  - Ace, King, or Queen
- Minimum support.
- Minimum strength.
- Whether the convention applies after all overcalls or only simple overcalls.
- Treatment after additional interference.

---

# Variations

## Standard Rosenkranz Redouble

Shows:

- Support.
- Ace or King.

---

## Modern Variants

Some partnerships:

- Include the Queen.
- Require four-card support.
- Use different strength requirements.

# Comparison

| Convention | Meaning |
|------------|---------|

|Rosenkranz Redouble|Support plus top honor after partner's overcall is doubled|
|Support Redouble|Exactly three-card support after opener is doubled|
|Lead-Directing Double|Requests a lead in a specified suit|

**Primary idea:** A **Rosenkranz Redouble** is an artificial **support redouble** used after partner's **suit overcall is doubled**, showing **support together with a top honor** in the overcalled suit. It assists both competitive bidding and defensive planning.
