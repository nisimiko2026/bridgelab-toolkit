# Jordan 2NT

## Purpose

**Jordan 2NT** (also known as **Truscott 2NT**) is an artificial response used **after partner makes a Takeout Double and right-hand opponent raises**.

It distinguishes:

- **Constructive raises** (limit raise or better)
- **Weak competitive raises**

Without Jordan 2NT, both hand types would often bid the same number of the suit.

---

# When It Applies

Typical auction:

```text
West     North     East     South

1♥        Double    2♥        ?
```

South:

```text
2NT
```

Artificial.

It says:

> "I have a limit raise or better in your implied suit."

---

# Basic Principle

After partner's takeout double:

- **2NT** = Limit raise or better
- **Simple raise** = Weak, competitive
- **Jump raise** = Preemptive (partnership agreement)

This mirrors the philosophy of **Good-Bad 2NT**, but in a different auction.

---

# What Does Jordan 2NT Show?

Typical requirements:

- Four-card support (occasionally three-card support by agreement)
- About **10+ points** or equivalent support values
- Invitational or game-going strength
- Interest in competing constructively

The exact strength depends on vulnerability and partnership style.

---

# Example 1

Auction:

```text
West     North     East     South

1♥        Double    2♥        2NT
```

South shows:

- Constructive heart support for partner's expected spade or minor-suit choice (depending on the double's context).
- Invitational or stronger values.

North now describes the hand naturally.

---

# Example 2

Auction:

```text
1♠
Double
2♠
2NT
```

Responder shows:

- Limit raise or better for partner's expected heart fit.

---

# Opener's (Doubler's) Continuations

After Jordan 2NT, the doubler may:

- Bid game with extras.
- Make a game try.
- Sign off with a minimum.
- Show a second suit.
- Explore slam with exceptional values.

---

# Why Use Jordan 2NT?

Without the convention:

```
1♥
Double
2♥
3♠
```

Could mean:

- Weak competition
- Invitational raise
- Game-going raise

Jordan 2NT removes this ambiguity.

---

# Advantages

- Clearly separates weak and constructive raises.
- Preserves simple raises for competitive hands.
- Helps judge games accurately.
- Widely played among tournament partnerships.

---

# Disadvantages

- Sacrifices the natural meaning of 2NT.
- Requires partnership agreement.
- Not useful if opponents do not raise.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Minimum support required.
- Strength range.
- Whether game-forcing hands are included.
- Treatment after redoubles or further interference.
- Whether jump raises remain preemptive.

---

# Variations

## Standard Jordan 2NT

Shows:

- Limit raise or better.

---

## Jordan with Three-Card Support

Some partnerships permit exactly three-card support when responder has sufficient values.

---

## Fit-Showing Extensions

Advanced partnerships combine Jordan 2NT with fit-showing jumps or cue bids.

# Summary

| Auction | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
|1♥ – Double – 2♥ – **2NT**|Limit raise or better for partner's takeout double|
|Simple raise|Weak competitive raise|
|Jump raise|Usually preemptive (by agreement)|

**Primary idea:** **Jordan 2NT** is an artificial **2NT response to partner's takeout double after the opponents have raised**, showing a **limit raise or better**. It allows direct raises to remain weak and competitive while accurately distinguishing constructive hands.
