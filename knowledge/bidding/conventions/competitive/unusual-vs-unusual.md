# Unusual vs. Unusual (UvU)

## Overview

**Unusual vs. Unusual (UvU)** is an advanced defensive convention used **after
the opponents make an Unusual Notrump overcall**.

The convention replaces natural competitive actions with artificial meanings,
allowing responder to distinguish

- constructive raises,
- limit raises,
- game-forcing raises,
- suit quality,
- and competitive strength,

while conserving valuable bidding space.

Modern expert partnerships frequently pair **Unusual vs. Unusual** with
**Michaels vs. Unusual**, **Lebensohl**, and **Rubensohl** as part of a complete
defense against artificial overcalls.

---

# Objectives

The convention enables the partnership to

- distinguish weak and strong raises,
- compete efficiently,
- identify game-going hands,
- preserve bidding space,
- improve slam investigation,
- reduce ambiguity after Unusual Notrump.

---

# Background

Suppose the auction begins

```
West     North     East     South

1♥        2NT*
```

The **2NT** overcall is **Unusual Notrump**, showing the two lowest unbid suits.

Without a special agreement,

responder has difficulty distinguishing

- weak raises,
- limit raises,
- cue raises,
- game-forcing hands.

UvU solves this problem.

---

# The Basic Principle

After an **Unusual Notrump overcall**, responder uses

- cue bids,
- suit bids,
- raises,

to distinguish

- strength,
- fit,
- suit quality.

The convention is entirely artificial.

---

# Typical Auction

```
1♥
(2NT*)
?
```

Assume

```
2NT = Clubs + Diamonds
```

Responder's available bids now receive conventional meanings.

---

# Most Common Expert Structure

The standard expert treatment is

| Bid Type | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Cue bid of the **lower** opponent suit | Limit raise or better |
| Cue bid of the **higher** opponent suit | Constructive raise |
| Jump raise | Preemptive |
| Simple raise | Weak competitive |
| New suit | Natural |

This is by far the most widely played version.

---

# Why Two Cue Bids?

The Unusual Notrump overcall shows **two suits**.

Responder therefore has **two cue bids** available.

Each cue bid receives a separate meaning.

This allows two different strength ranges to be shown immediately.

---

Auction

```

```
2NT = Clubs + Diamonds

```

```

```

Cue bid of the higher suit.

Shows

- constructive raise.

```

```

- weak competitive raise,

- approximately four-card support.

# Preemptive Raise

```

```

- weak,

- purely obstructive.

3♠
```

Natural.

Shows

- genuine spade suit,
- constructive values.

# Expert Style

Modern expert partnerships almost universally play

**Lower Cue**

=

Limit Raise or Better

**Higher Cue**

=

Constructive Raise.

This structure creates immediate separation between invitational and weaker
constructive hands.

# Modern Expert Treatments

There are **two principal expert styles**.

## Lower Cue = Limit+

This is the most common worldwide.

Example

```
1♠
(2NT = Clubs + Diamonds)

3♣*
```

Limit Raise or better.

```
3♦*
```

Constructive raise.

---

## Reverse Style

Some partnerships reverse the meanings.

Lower cue

Constructive.

Higher cue

Limit+.

Although less common, this style is still encountered in tournament play.

# Relationship to Michaels vs. Unusual

UvU and **Michaels vs. Unusual** are complementary conventions.

Together they provide structured defenses against the two most popular
two-suited overcalls.

| Opponent's Convention | Defensive Method |
|-----------------------|------------------|
| Unusual Notrump | Unusual vs. Unusual |
| Michaels Cue Bid | Michaels vs. Unusual |

Many expert partnerships adopt both conventions as a complete competitive
package.

---

# Historical Perspective

As **Unusual Notrump** became increasingly popular during the latter half of the
20th century, tournament partnerships recognized the need for a structured
response system. Unusual vs. Unusual emerged as the standard expert solution,
making efficient use of the two available cue bids to differentiate raise
strength while preserving natural suit bids.

Today it is considered one of the core defensive treatments against artificial
two-suited overcalls.

---

# Summary

**Unusual vs. Unusual (UvU)** is an advanced competitive convention used after
an opponent's **Unusual Notrump** overcall. By assigning different meanings to
the **lower and higher cue bids**, responder can distinguish **constructive
raises**, **limit raises**, and **stronger hands** while preserving natural
bidding space.

Together with **Michaels vs. Unusual**, UvU forms an essential part of the
modern expert partnership's defense against artificial two-suited overcalls,
providing accurate hand description and greatly improving competitive decision-
making.
