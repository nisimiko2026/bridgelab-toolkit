# Meckwell Convention

## Overview

**Meckwell** is an advanced defense to an opponent's **1NT opening**, developed by world champions **Jeff Meckstroth** and **Eric Rodwell**. It is designed to describe distribution efficiently while allowing the partnership to compete aggressively against both strong and weak notrump openings.

Unlike many traditional defenses, Meckwell combines:

- One-suited hands
- Major-minor two-suited hands
- Both-major hands
- Both-minor hands

into a compact and logical structure.

The convention is primarily played by experienced partnerships because it requires detailed partnership agreements and accurate follow-up bidding.

---

# Purpose

Meckwell is designed to:

- Compete effectively over 1NT.
- Show distribution precisely.
- Locate major-suit fits.
- Preempt the opponents.
- Maximize constructive bidding space.
- Minimize partnership ambiguity.

---

# When to Use

The convention applies after:

```
(1NT)
?
```

It is normally played against:

- Strong Notrump
- Weak Notrump

Many partnerships vary their style according to vulnerability.

---

# Standard Meckwell Structure

The most common modern version is:

| Bid | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Double | One minor or both majors |
| 2♣ | Clubs and Hearts OR Clubs only (by agreement) |
| 2♦ | Diamonds and Spades OR Diamonds only (by agreement) |
| 2♥ | Hearts and Spades |
| 2♠ | Spades and a Minor |
| 2NT | Both Minors |

**Important:** Several published versions exist. The meanings above represent one common expert treatment.

---

# Double

```
(1NT)
Double*
```

Shows one of:

- Both major suits
- A long minor suit

Advancer normally bids:

```
2♣
```

unless holding a strong preference.

Overcaller clarifies.

---

## Example

```
West   North

1NT    Double*
Pass   2♣
Pass   2♦
```

Shows:

- Long diamond suit

or another agreed continuation.

---

# 2♣

Usually shows:

- Clubs and Hearts

Some partnerships instead play:

- Natural clubs.

Agreement is essential.

---

# 2♦

Usually shows:

- Diamonds and Spades

Again, some partnerships play alternative structures.

---

# 2♥

```
(1NT)
2♥*
```

Shows:

- Both major suits.

Usually:

- 5-4
- 5-5

---

# 2♠

```
(1NT)
2♠*
```

Shows:

- Spades
- An unspecified minor.

Usually:

- 5-5

---

# 2NT

Shows:

- Clubs
- Diamonds

Normally:

- 5-5

---

# Strength Requirements

Typical values:

Against Strong NT:

- 8–14 HCP

Against Weak NT:

- 10–16 HCP

Distribution is considerably more important than raw HCP.

---

# Suit Requirements

Normally:

- 5-5

Some partnerships allow:

- Excellent 5-4

Natural one-suited hands generally promise:

- Six-card suit.

# Advantages

Meckwell:

- Extremely descriptive.
- Covers almost every offensive hand type.
- Excellent competitive tool.
- Strong preemptive effect.
- Preferred by many expert partnerships.

---

# Limitations

The convention:

- Requires extensive partnership agreements.
- Has multiple published versions.
- Artificial bids reveal distribution.
- Unsuitable for casual partnerships.

---

# Variations

Published versions include:

- Standard Meckwell
- Modified Meckwell
- Transfer Meckwell
- Precision-compatible Meckwell

Every partnership should define:

- Double.
- 2♣.
- 2♦.
- Relay sequences.
- Continuations.

---

---

## Using Weak Suits

Distribution is important, but suit quality remains essential.

---

## Forgetting Vulnerability

Aggressive actions should be adjusted according to vulnerability.

---

## Overcompeting

Not every distribution justifies entering the auction.

# Tips

- Master Landy and DONT before learning Meckwell.
- Agree on one published version.
- Practice relay continuations.
- Memorize the complete structure.
- Review agreements regularly.

---

# Modern Usage

Meckwell is primarily played by expert partnerships and advanced tournament players. It is admired for its descriptive accuracy and flexibility but is less common than DONT, Cappelletti, or Landy because of its complexity.

The convention is especially popular among partnerships that enjoy highly artificial competitive methods and are willing to invest in detailed agreements.

---

# Summary

The Meckwell Convention is an advanced defense to a 1NT opening that combines artificial overcalls and relays to describe one-suited and two-suited hands with exceptional precision. Developed by Jeff Meckstroth and Eric Rodwell, it provides expert partnerships with a highly flexible method for competing over notrump while locating major-suit fits and disrupting the opponents' auction. Although several versions exist, Meckwell remains one of the most sophisticated and powerful defenses available to experienced players.
