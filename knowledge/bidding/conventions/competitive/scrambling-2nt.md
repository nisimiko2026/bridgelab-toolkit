# Scrambling 2NT

## Purpose

**Scrambling 2NT** is an artificial competitive convention used when the partnership believes that **defending is undesirable**, but neither partner knows the best trump suit.

The bid asks partner:

> **"Please choose our best fit among the remaining suits."**

Unlike **Good-Bad 2NT** or **Jordan 2NT**, Scrambling 2NT is **not** about showing strength—it is about finding the safest contract.

---

# Basic Principle

Scrambling 2NT says:

- I have tolerance for several suits.
- I am unsure which suit is best.
- Please bid your cheapest acceptable suit.
- We are trying to survive the auction, not invite game.

The convention is almost always used in **competitive or rescue auctions**.

---

# When It Applies

Typical auction:

```text
West     North     East     South

1♦        Double    2♦        Pass
Pass      2NT
```

The **2NT** is artificial.

North is asked to bid the lowest suit in which the partnership is likely to have a fit.

---

# Typical Situations

Scrambling 2NT is commonly used when:

- Both partners have bid several suits.
- No fit has been established.
- The opponents have competed aggressively.
- Defending would likely be worse than declaring.

---

# Responses

Partner normally:

- Bids the cheapest four-card suit.
- Prefers a known fit.
- Avoids notrump unless specifically agreed.

Example:

```text
2NT
3♣
```

If clubs are unsuitable:

```text
3♦
```

or

```text
3♥
```

according to partnership agreement.

---

# Example 1

Auction

```text
West     North     East     South

1♥        Double    2♥        Pass
Pass      2NT
```

North:

```text
3♣
```

Shows clubs as the preferred escape.

---

# Example 2

Auction

```text
1♠
Double
2♠
Pass
Pass
2NT
```

The partnership searches for the safest minor-suit contract.

---

# Hand Requirements

Typical hand:

- Defensive prospects are poor.
- No clear fit has been identified.
- Tolerance for several suits.
- Distributional values.

High-card strength is relatively unimportant.

---

# Advantages

- Finds hidden fits.
- Avoids defending unfavorable contracts.
- Improves part-score results.
- Particularly effective in competitive auctions.

---

# Disadvantages

- Requires detailed partnership agreements.
- May consume bidding space.
- Not all partnerships play the same continuations.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Which auctions use Scrambling 2NT.
- Whether partner always bids the cheapest suit.
- Priority among equal-length suits.
- Whether responder may pass 2NT (usually not).
- Treatment after further interference.

---

# Variations

## Standard Scrambling 2NT

Partner bids the cheapest playable suit.

---

## General Scrambling

Some partnerships extend the concept to:

- Scrambling cue bids.
- Scrambling doubles.
- Other rescue auctions.

# Summary

**Primary idea:** **Scrambling 2NT** is an artificial **rescue bid** used in competitive auctions to locate the partnership's safest trump fit. It asks partner to bid the most suitable suit and is based on **distribution and fit**, not on strength.
