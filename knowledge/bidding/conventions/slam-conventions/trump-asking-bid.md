# Trump-Asking Bid (TAB)

## Purpose

A **Trump-Asking Bid (TAB)** is an artificial bid used during **slam investigation** to obtain information about the agreed trump suit.

Unlike **Ace-Asking Bids** or **Control-Asking Bids**, a Trump-Asking Bid focuses specifically on the partnership's trump holding, helping determine whether the agreed trump suit is sufficiently strong for a small or grand slam.

Trump-Asking methods are primarily found in advanced relay and strong club systems.

---

# When It Is Used

A Trump-Asking Bid is made only after:

- A trump suit has been agreed.
- The partnership is committed to game.
- Basic key-card or control information is already known.
- Further information about the trump suit will influence the final contract.

Typical auction:

```text
1♠
3♠
4NT
5♦
5NT
6♣   ← Trump Ask (partnership agreement)
```

The exact asking bid depends on the partnership's methods.

---

# Information Requested

A Trump-Asking Bid may request one or more of the following:

- Possession of the trump queen
- Number of trumps
- Specific trump honors
- Trump jack
- Trump intermediates (10, 9)
- Trump quality
- Exact trump holding

The precise meaning depends on the agreed system.

---

# Common Uses

## Trump Queen Ask

The most common application.

Responder shows whether the agreed trump queen is held.

Often incorporated into:

- Roman Key Card Blackwood
- Kickback
- Redwood
- Minorwood

---

## Trump Length Ask

Some relay systems ask responder to show:

- Four-card support
- Five-card support
- Longer support

This helps evaluate whether the partnership has a nine-card or ten-card fit.

---

## Trump Honor Ask

Advanced systems may identify:

- AQ
- KQ
- QJ
- AQJ
- Other honor combinations

This is particularly useful for grand slam investigation.

---

# Response Methods

Several response structures are used.

## Step Responses

| Step | Meaning |
|------|---------|
|1st Step|Weakest agreed trump holding|
|2nd Step|Trump queen only|
|3rd Step|Queen plus jack|
|4th Step|Queen plus additional honors|

Exact meanings vary by partnership.

---

## Natural Responses

Some systems simply show:

- Trump queen
- Extra trump length
- Top honors

through natural bids.

---

# Advantages

- Evaluates the quality of the agreed trump suit.
- Improves grand slam decisions.
- Complements key-card and control asks.
- Particularly effective in relay systems.

---

# Disadvantages

- Not standardized.
- Requires detailed partnership agreements.
- Usually unnecessary for casual partnerships.
- Rarely played outside advanced systems.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Discuss:

- Which bid initiates the Trump Ask.
- What information is requested.
- Response structure.
- Whether trump length is shown.
- Whether intermediate honors are shown.
- Relationship with key-card and control asks.

---

# Variations

Different systems use Trump-Asking Bids in different ways.

Examples include:

- Relay-based trump asks
- Precision trump asks
- Blue Team methods
- Strong Club relay methods

Some partnerships limit the ask to the trump queen, while others identify the complete trump holding.

# Summary

A **Trump-Asking Bid** is an artificial slam-investigation method that requests information about the **agreed trump suit**, such as the trump queen, trump length, or trump honors. It is generally used **after key-card or control information has already been obtained** and is most commonly found in advanced relay and strong club systems.
