# Responder Evaluation

## Purpose

After partner makes a Game Try, responder must decide whether the partnership
has sufficient playing strength for game.

This decision should never be based solely on High Card Points.

Instead, responder re-evaluates the hand in light of the information revealed
by opener's Game Try.

---

# General Principle

The first evaluation occurred when responder raised opener's major.

The Game Try provides new information.

Responder must therefore perform a **second evaluation** using that new
information.

---

# The Evaluation Process

1. Understand the Game Try.
2. Identify what opener is asking.
3. Re-evaluate your hand.
4. Upgrade useful features.
5. Downgrade wasted values.
6. Decide whether game is justified.

---

# Step 1 – Understand the Message

Different Game Tries ask different questions.

| Game Try | What opener wants to know |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Help Suit | Can you help this suit? |
| Long Suit | Will this suit produce tricks? |
| Short Suit | Do your values fit my shortage? |
| Maximal | Do you have maximum support? |
| Two-Way | Which feature fits best? |
| Artificial | Re-evaluate using our agreements |

---

# Step 2 – Trump Quality

Excellent trump support often outweighs one or two HCP.

Upgrade

- five-card support,
- excellent intermediates,
- KJ10x,
- QJ10x,
- AKxx.

Downgrade

- weak trumps,
- poor intermediates,
- four small trumps.

---

# Step 3 – Controls

Upgrade

- Aces,
- Kings,
- singleton,
- void.

Controls frequently win games that point count alone would reject.

---

# Step 4 – Distribution

Upgrade

- fifth trump,
- sixth trump,
- singleton,
- void,
- double fit.

Distribution is one of the strongest indicators of offensive potential.

---

# Step 5 – Working Values

A King supporting opener's long suit is valuable.

A Queen opposite opener's singleton is often wasted.

Evaluate honors according to how well they cooperate with opener's hand.

---

# Step 6 – Wasted Values

Downgrade

- unsupported Queens,
- duplicated honors,
- honors opposite known shortage,
- defensive values with little offensive use.

---

# Step 7 – Fit

A nine-card fit is good.

A ten-card fit is excellent.

An eight-card fit with outstanding controls may still justify game.

---

# Step 8 – Source of Tricks

Ask

Can opener's long suit become established?

Can shortness create ruffs?

Can my honors contribute?

---

# Step 9 – Losing Trick Count

When your partnership uses Losing Trick Count,

combine it with

- controls,
- trump quality,
- distribution,

rather than relying on LTC alone.

---

# Typical Upgrade Factors

- Extra trump length
- Trump honors
- Aces
- Kings
- Singleton
- Void
- Fit with opener's long suit
- Fit opposite opener's shortness
- Useful intermediates

---

# Typical Downgrade Factors

- Flat shape
- Weak trumps
- Unsupported Queens
- Honors opposite shortage
- Defensive values
- Poor suit texture

---

# Evaluation by Game Try Type

## Help Suit

Upgrade honors in the requested suit.

---

## Long Suit

Upgrade support for the long suit.

---

## Short Suit

Downgrade honors opposite opener's shortage.

---

## Maximal

Evaluate overall strength.

---

## Two-Way

Determine whether the long suit or shortness is more valuable.

---

## Artificial

Follow partnership agreements before evaluating.

---

# Common Errors

- Counting HCP only.
- Ignoring trump quality.
- Ignoring distribution.
- Forgetting working values.
- Accepting automatically with maximum HCP.
- Rejecting automatically with minimum HCP.

---

# Practical Checklist

Before accepting a Game Try ask yourself:

✓ Do my honors fit partner's hand?

✓ Are my trumps strong?

✓ Do I have controls?

✓ Is my distribution useful?

✓ Are any honors wasted?

✓ Will our combined hands produce ten tricks?

---

# Golden Rule

> Re-evaluate your hand after every Game Try.

Never rely on your original hand evaluation alone.

---

# Summary

Responder's role is not simply to count points.

Responder must determine whether the partnership's combined assets—

- controls,
- trump quality,
- distribution,
- working honors,
- offensive potential,

are sufficient to justify bidding game.

This re-evaluation process is the key to successful use of every Game Try convention.
