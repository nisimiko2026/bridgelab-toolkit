# Super Precision

## Overview

**Super Precision** is an advanced member of the **Precision family of strong club systems**, designed to provide even more accurate hand description than Standard Precision through the extensive use of **relay bidding**, **limited openings**, and **scientific continuations**.

Rather than being a single standardized system, **Super Precision** refers to a family of enhanced Precision methods developed by expert partnerships. Most versions retain the core Precision philosophy—a **strong artificial 1♣ opening** with **limited natural one-level openings**—while incorporating relay sequences, control-showing methods, and highly structured slam investigations.

Super Precision is intended for experienced partnerships and has been used successfully in high-level national and international competition.

---

# System Characteristics

- Strong artificial **1♣**
- Five-card major openings
- Limited natural openings
- Strong notrump
- Extensive relay methods
- Scientific hand description
- Excellent slam bidding
- Partnership-specific continuations

# Basic Opening Structure

| Opening | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **1♣** | Artificial, strong (16+ HCP or equivalent) |
| **1♦** | Natural, limited |
| **1♥** | Natural, 5+ hearts, limited |
| **1♠** | Natural, 5+ spades, limited |
| **1NT** | Balanced, 15–17 HCP |
| **2♣** | Natural or strong, depending on partnership style |
| **2♦** | Weak Two (or partnership agreement) |
| **2♥** | Weak Two |
| **2♠** | Weak Two |
| **2NT** | Balanced, 20–21 HCP |
| **3-level** | Preemptive |

> **Note:** Opening structures vary among Super Precision partnerships. The defining features are the **strong club** and the **enhanced relay structure**, not the exact meanings of every opening bid.

# The Strong Club

## 1♣

Artificial.

Shows:

- Approximately **16+ HCP**
- Any distribution
- Hand too strong for the limited natural openings

The bid does **not** promise clubs.

Example:

♠ AKQ8  
♥ AQ5  
♦ AK7  
♣ K94

Open:

**1♣**

---

# Responses to 1♣

Most Super Precision versions use an advanced response structure.

A typical framework is:

| Response | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **1♦** | Negative or relay |
| **1♥** | Positive, hearts |
| **1♠** | Positive, spades |
| **1NT** | Positive balanced |
| **2♣** | Positive, clubs |
| **2♦** | Positive, diamonds |

Subsequent bidding often employs relay sequences to describe:

- Exact distribution
- High-card strength
- Controls
- Singletons
- Voids
- Suit quality
- Slam suitability

---

# Limited Natural Openings

One-level natural openings generally show:

- Approximately **11–15 HCP**
- Defined suit
- Limited strength

Because stronger hands open **1♣**, responder can evaluate game prospects immediately.

## 1♠

# Notrump Opening

## 1NT

Shows:

- Balanced hand
- **15–17 HCP**

Typical balanced shapes:

- 4-3-3-3
- 4-4-3-2
- 5-3-3-2

# Relay Philosophy

Relay bidding is the principal enhancement over Standard Precision.

Relay bids allow opener and responder to exchange highly specific information while conserving bidding space.

Typical relay sequences identify:

- Exact shape
- Strength
- Controls
- Singleton or void location
- Suit quality
- Slam potential

Most relay structures use step responses, with each bid conveying predefined information.

---

# Weak Two Openings

Many Super Precision versions use:

- **2♦** = Weak Two
- **2♥** = Weak Two
- **2♠** = Weak Two

Some partnerships adopt alternative multi-purpose openings instead.

- Rubensohl

# Slam Methods

Super Precision excels in slam bidding.

Typical agreements include:

- Roman Key Card Blackwood
- Cue Bidding
- Control Bids
- Serious 3NT
- Last Train
- Extensive relay continuations

The accurate exchange of shape and control information enables highly efficient slam exploration.

- Extremely accurate hand description
- Outstanding slam bidding

# Legacy

Super Precision represents the evolution of the original Precision philosophy toward greater descriptive accuracy through relay bidding and scientific continuations. While never standardized into a single official system, its methods have influenced many modern expert partnerships and advanced strong-club approaches.

# Summary

**Super Precision** is an advanced **strong club** system that extends the original Precision methods with **extensive relay bidding**, **limited natural openings**, and highly scientific slam investigation. Intended for expert partnerships, it offers exceptionally accurate hand description at the cost of increased complexity and partnership preparation.

---

# Notes

- **Super Precision is not a single standardized system.** The name describes a family of enhanced Precision methods rather than one official convention card.
- Individual partnerships may differ substantially in their relay structures, opening-bid meanings, and continuations while remaining within the Super Precision framework.
