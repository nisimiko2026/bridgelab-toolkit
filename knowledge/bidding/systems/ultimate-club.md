# Ultimate Club

## Overview

**Ultimate Club** is a modern **strong club bidding system** designed to combine the descriptive power of artificial bidding with the practicality of natural methods. It belongs to the family of **Precision-inspired systems**, using a **strong artificial 1♣ opening**, **limited natural one-level openings**, and **five-card major openings**.

Unlike classic Precision, Ultimate Club typically incorporates more modern relay methods, optimized responses, and competitive agreements while remaining less complex than highly sophisticated relay systems such as **MOSCITO**. Because there is **no single official version**, the name refers to a family of closely related partnership methods rather than a universally standardized convention card.

Ultimate Club is primarily played by experienced tournament partnerships seeking greater bidding precision without adopting a full relay system.

---

# System Characteristics

- Strong artificial **1♣**
- Five-card major openings
- Limited natural openings
- Strong notrump
- Precision-based philosophy
- Modern relay and asking bids
- Scientific slam bidding
- Partnership-oriented agreements

# Basic Opening Structure

| Opening | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **1♣** | Artificial, strong (16+ HCP or equivalent) |
| **1♦** | Natural, limited |
| **1♥** | Natural, 5+ hearts, limited |
| **1♠** | Natural, 5+ spades, limited |
| **1NT** | Balanced, 15–17 HCP |
| **2♣** | Natural clubs or partnership-defined |
| **2♦** | Weak Two (or partnership agreement) |
| **2♥** | Weak Two |
| **2♠** | Weak Two |
| **2NT** | Balanced, 20–21 HCP |
| **3-level** | Preemptive |

> **Note:** Exact opening meanings vary among Ultimate Club partnerships. The strong 1♣ opening and the limited nature of the remaining one-level openings are the defining features.

# The Strong Club

## 1♣

Artificial.

Shows:

- Approximately **16+ HCP**
- Any distribution
- Hand too strong for a limited natural opening

The bid makes **no promise of club length**.

Example:

♠ AKQ8  
♥ AQ7  
♦ AK6  
♣ K95

Open:

**1♣**

---

# Responses to 1♣

Most Ultimate Club partnerships employ structured responses designed to distinguish:

- Negative hands
- Positive hands
- Balanced hands
- Suit-oriented positives

A typical framework is:

| Response | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **1♦** | Negative or waiting |
| **1♥** | Positive with hearts |
| **1♠** | Positive with spades |
| **1NT** | Positive balanced |
| **2♣** | Positive with clubs |
| **2♦** | Positive with diamonds |

Many partnerships continue with relay or asking bids to clarify:

- Shape
- Strength
- Controls
- Shortness
- Slam potential

---

# Limited Natural Openings

Natural one-level openings generally promise:

- Approximately **11–15 HCP**
- Defined suit length
- Limited strength

Because all stronger hands begin with **1♣**, responder can judge game prospects early.

# Legacy

Ultimate Club represents the continuing evolution of Precision-based systems toward greater bidding accuracy while avoiding the full complexity of advanced relay systems. Although not standardized, it demonstrates how modern expert partnerships can combine natural bidding with carefully designed artificial structures to improve competitive and slam bidding.

# Summary

**Ultimate Club** is a modern **Precision-inspired strong club system** built around a **strong artificial 1♣ opening**, **limited natural one-level bids**, and **five-card majors**. It emphasizes structured continuations, accurate hand description, and efficient slam bidding while remaining more flexible and generally less relay-intensive than systems such as **MOSCITO**.

---

# Notes

- **Ultimate Club is not a single officially standardized system.** Different expert partnerships use different versions under the same general name.
- The system should be viewed as a **family of modern Precision-derived methods**, sharing a common philosophy rather than a fixed convention card.
