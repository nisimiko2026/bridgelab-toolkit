# Carrot Club System

## Overview

**Carrot Club** is a natural bidding system developed in the United Kingdom as an alternative to traditional Acol and Standard American systems. It combines many modern bidding ideas while maintaining natural one-level openings. The system is named after the ♦ (diamond) suit, often called the "carrot" in British slang.

Carrot Club emphasizes:

- Five-card major openings
- Strong notrump
- Natural minor openings
- Modern competitive methods
- Light opening bids
- Aggressive style

It is less common than Acol or Standard American but has influenced several modern natural systems.

# Opening Bids

## 1♣

Shows:

- 3+ clubs
- 11–21 HCP
- May conceal longer diamonds when balanced

Examples:

♠ KQ8 ♥ AJ7 ♦ K42 ♣ QJ743

♠ AQJ ♥ K65 ♦ A84 ♣ K1083

---

## 1♦

Shows:

- 3+ diamonds
- 11–21 HCP
- Usually longer diamonds than clubs if both minors are available

---

## Major Openings

### 1♥

Shows:

- Five or more hearts
- 11–21 HCP

### 1♠

Shows:

- Five or more spades
- 11–21 HCP

---

# Notrump Opening

## 1NT

Shows:

- 15–17 HCP
- Balanced hand

Typical shapes:

- 4-3-3-3
- 4-4-3-2
- 5-3-3-2

---

# Strong Opening

## 2♣

Artificial.

Shows:

- Very strong hand
- Game forcing or nearly game forcing
- Usually 22+ HCP or equivalent playing strength

---

# Weak Twos

## 2♦

Weak Two.

Shows:

- Six-card diamond suit
- Approximately 6–10 HCP

## 2♥ / 2♠

Standard Weak Two openings.

---

# Responses to 1NT

Most partnerships employ:

- Stayman
- Jacoby Transfers
- Texas Transfers
- Minor Suit Stayman (optional)
- Smolen (optional)

---

# Responses to Major Openings

Modern agreements frequently include:

- Jacoby 2NT
- Bergen Raises
- Splinter bids
- Limit raises
- Drury (passed hand)
- Fourth Suit Forcing

---

# Responses to Minor Openings

Typical methods include:

- Inverted Minor Raises
- New Minor Forcing
- Walsh style responses (optional)
- Weak jump shifts (partnership agreement)

---

# Competitive Methods

Most Carrot Club partnerships adopt modern treatments such as:

- Negative Doubles
- Responsive Doubles
- Support Doubles
- Michaels Cue Bid
- Unusual 2NT
- Takeout Doubles
- Lebensohl
- Rubensohl
- Weak Jump Overcalls

---

# Slam Conventions

Common agreements include:

- Roman Key Card Blackwood (1430 or 3014)
- Cue Bidding
- Control Bids
- Exclusion Key Card Blackwood (optional)
- Last Train
- Serious 3NT (optional)

---

# Opening Style

Carrot Club generally favors:

- Light opening bids
- Active competitive auctions
- Frequent preempting
- Accurate notrump ranges
- Early identification of major-suit fits

# Advantages

- Simple natural structure
- Five-card majors
- Strong notrump
- Modern competitive methods
- Easy transition from Standard American
- Accurate major-suit auctions

---

# Disadvantages

- Fewer distinctive features than highly artificial systems
- Requires partnership agreements for many modern conventions
- Minor-suit auctions may be less descriptive than specialized club systems

# Summary

Carrot Club is a natural five-card-major system with a strong notrump and modern competitive agreements. It retains the simplicity of Standard American while incorporating contemporary bidding treatments, making it suitable for partnerships seeking a straightforward yet effective natural system.
