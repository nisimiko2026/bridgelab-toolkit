# Roman Precision

## Overview

**Roman Precision** is a **strong club bidding system** that combines the **Precision Club** philosophy with concepts from the **Roman Club** and **Roman System** developed by the Italian Blue Team. It retains the **strong artificial 1♣ opening** of Precision while incorporating several Roman bidding treatments, particularly in hand evaluation, relay methods, and slam investigation.

Roman Precision was developed during the evolution of strong club systems in the 1960s and 1970s. Although never as widely played as Standard Precision, it influenced many later expert partnerships and helped bridge the gap between the Italian strong-club tradition and North American Precision methods.

---

# System Characteristics

- Strong artificial **1♣**
- Five-card major openings
- Limited natural one-level openings
- Strong notrump
- Precision-based structure
- Roman relay and asking methods
- Scientific slam bidding
- Control-oriented hand evaluation

---

# Basic Opening Structure

| Opening | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **1♣** | Artificial, strong (16+ HCP or equivalent) |
| **1♦** | Natural, limited |
| **1♥** | Natural, 5+ hearts, limited |
| **1♠** | Natural, 5+ spades, limited |
| **1NT** | Balanced, 15–17 HCP |
| **2♣** | Natural clubs or partnership-defined |
| **2♦** | Weak Two (or partnership agreement) |
| **2♥** | Weak Two |
| **2♠** | Weak Two |
| **2NT** | Balanced, 20–21 HCP |
| **3-level** | Preemptive |

> **Note:** Roman Precision is not a single standardized system. Opening structures and continuations vary among partnerships while preserving the strong-club philosophy.

# The Strong Club

## 1♣

Artificial.

Shows:

- Approximately **16+ HCP**
- Any distribution
- Hand too strong for a limited natural opening

The opening promises strength, **not clubs**.

Example:

♠ AKQ8  
♥ AQ5  
♦ AK7  
♣ K94

Open:

**1♣**

---

# Responses to 1♣

A common structure is:

| Response | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **1♦** | Negative or waiting |
| **1♥** | Positive with hearts |
| **1♠** | Positive with spades |
| **1NT** | Positive balanced |
| **2♣** | Positive with clubs |
| **2♦** | Positive with diamonds |

Subsequent bidding often employs relay or asking sequences to identify:

- Exact distribution
- Controls
- Strength
- Singletons and voids
- Slam potential

---

# Limited Natural Openings

Natural one-level openings generally show:

- Approximately **11–15 HCP**
- Defined suit
- Limited strength

Since stronger hands begin with **1♣**, responder can evaluate game prospects quickly.

## 1♥

- Five or more hearts
- Approximately 11–15 HCP

## 1♠

- Five or more spades
- Approximately 11–15 HCP

Treatment varies by partnership.

# Notrump Opening

## 1NT

Shows:

- Balanced hand
- **15–17 HCP**

Typical balanced shapes:

- 4-3-3-3
- 4-4-3-2
- 5-3-3-2

---

# Relay Philosophy

Roman Precision frequently uses relay or asking bids to exchange detailed information below game level.

Relay sequences help identify:

- Exact shape
- Controls
- High-card strength
- Shortness
- Suit quality
- Slam suitability

Compared with MOSCITO, relay methods are generally less extensive but more structured than in standard Precision.

---

# Weak Two Openings

Many versions use:

- **2♦** = Weak Two
- **2♥** = Weak Two
- **2♠** = Weak Two

Alternative partnership treatments are possible.

---

# Responses to 1NT

Typical agreements include:

- Stayman
- Jacoby Transfers
- Texas Transfers
- Smolen (optional)
- Minor Suit Stayman (optional)

# Competitive Methods

Modern Roman Precision partnerships commonly adopt:

- Negative Doubles
- Support Doubles
- Responsive Doubles
- Michaels Cue Bid
- Unusual 2NT
- Lebensohl
- Rubensohl
- Weak Jump Overcalls

---

# Slam Methods

Roman Precision places particular emphasis on slam bidding through:

- Roman Key Card Blackwood
- Cue Bidding
- Control Bids
- Serious 3NT
- Last Train
- Relay continuations

Accurate control-showing sequences are one of the system's principal strengths.

# Legacy

Roman Precision represents one of the important evolutionary branches of the Precision family. By combining the strong-club philosophy with Roman bidding concepts, it contributed to the development of more descriptive relay methods and more efficient slam investigation in expert bridge.

# Summary

**Roman Precision** is a **Precision-derived strong club system** that combines a **strong artificial 1♣ opening**, **limited natural one-level bids**, and **Roman-style relay and control-showing methods**. Designed for experienced partnerships, it emphasizes accurate hand description and scientific slam bidding while retaining the core structure of the Precision family.

---

# Notes

- **Roman Precision is not a single officially standardized system.** Several partnership versions exist, differing in relay structures and opening-bid treatments.
- The system should be viewed as a **member of the Precision family**, influenced by the Italian Roman bidding tradition rather than as a completely distinct bidding philosophy.
