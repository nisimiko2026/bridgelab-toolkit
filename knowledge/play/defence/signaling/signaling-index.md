# Defensive Signalling

## Overview

Defensive signalling is the primary method by which defenders legally
communicate during the play of the hand.

Because defenders cannot speak or exchange unauthorized information, every card
played may convey valuable information about

- attitude,
- count,
- suit preference,
- distribution,
- entries,
- defensive plans.

Successful signalling enables defenders to function as a single coordinated
partnership while revealing as little useful information as possible to
declarer.

This section covers the world's most widely used signalling methods together
with the principles behind effective defensive communication.

# Design Principles

## One Message Per Card

Each signal should communicate one clear message.

Avoid ambiguous signalling whenever possible.

---

## Partnership Consistency

A signalling system is valuable only if both partners apply it consistently.

---

## Information Management

Communicate what partner needs to know while revealing as little useful
information as possible to declarer.

---

## Priority Matters

When several messages are possible, partnership agreements should determine
which information takes precedence.
