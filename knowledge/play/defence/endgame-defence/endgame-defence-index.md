# Endgame Defence

## Overview

The endgame is often the most technically demanding phase of defensive play.

As the number of remaining cards decreases, every card becomes increasingly
valuable. Accurate counting, precise timing, communication, and visualization
allow defenders to prevent declarer's advanced techniques and preserve vital
tricks.

Unlike the opening and middle game, defensive mistakes in the endgame are
rarely recoverable.

This section covers the techniques defenders use to

- prevent squeezes,
- avoid endplays,
- preserve communication,
- maintain trump control,
- recognize critical positions,
- force declarer into losing lines.

# Design Principles

## Every Card Matters

In the endgame, a single discard or exit card may determine the entire result.

---

## Count to the End

Accurate counting becomes even more valuable as the number of unknown cards
decreases.

---

## Preserve Communication

Many defensive endings are won or lost because defenders maintain—or lose—their
ability to communicate.

---

## Think Several Tricks Ahead

Expert defenders anticipate declarer's plan before the critical position is
reached.
