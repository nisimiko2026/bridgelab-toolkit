# Opening Leads

## Overview

The opening lead is the only card played before declarer reveals dummy and is
often the single most important defensive decision of the entire hand.

A successful opening lead can

- establish immediate tricks,
- defeat communication,
- promote partner's honors,
- develop long suits,
- prevent declarer's plan,
- determine the outcome before trick one.

A poor opening lead may immediately give declarer an extra trick or reveal
critical information.

BridgeLab organizes opening leads according to the type of contract,
partnership agreements, and strategic objectives.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- choose effective opening leads,
- distinguish active and passive leads,
- lead correctly against suit and notrump contracts,
- understand standard lead agreements,
- recognize dangerous lead positions,
- use auction information effectively,
- cooperate with partner through the opening lead.

# Design Principles

## The Auction Comes First

The bidding provides the most important clues for selecting the correct opening
lead.

---

## Lead With a Purpose

Every opening lead should have a clear objective.

Examples include

- establishing tricks,
- attacking communication,
- obtaining a ruff,
- making a passive lead.

---

## Partnership Agreements Matter

Opening lead systems are effective only when both partners understand and apply
the same agreements consistently.

---

## Risk Versus Reward

The best opening lead balances the possibility of establishing tricks against
the danger of giving declarer an unnecessary advantage.
