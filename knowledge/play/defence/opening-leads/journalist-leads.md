# Journalist Leads

## Overview

**Journalist Leads** are an opening lead agreement in which the lead of an **honor card** conveys specific information about the holding. Developed by Ely Culbertson and popularized by bridge journalist and author **Victor Mollo**, the method seeks to make opening leads more descriptive than standard honor leads.

Although less common today than **Standard Leads**, **Rusinow Leads**, or **Third/Fifth Leads**, Journalist Leads are still played by some expert partnerships.

The guiding principle is:

> **The honor led describes the remaining honor structure.**

---

# Purpose

Journalist Leads allow defenders to:

- Convey precise information about honor holdings.
- Help partner place missing honors.
- Improve continuation decisions.
- Reduce ambiguity in honor leads.
- Coordinate the defense more accurately.

---

# Basic Agreements

The exact agreements vary by partnership. A common version is:

| Lead | Typical Holding |
|------|-----------------|
| **Ace** | AK or AKx; asks partner for attitude or count, depending on partnership agreement |
| **King** | KQ, KQJ, or strong sequence without the Ace |
| **Queen** | QJ, QJ10, or equivalent sequence |
| **Jack** | JT, J109, or interior sequence |
| **Ten** | Interior sequence such as KJ10x, AQ10x, or 109x |

Because there are several published versions of Journalist Leads, **partnership discussion is essential**.

---

# General Principles

- Honor leads promise touching or near-touching honors.
- Interior sequences are frequently led.
- The lead attempts to identify the exact honor combination.
- Partner should interpret the lead according to the agreed style.

---

# Example 1 – King Lead

Holding

```
♠ K Q 8 4
```

Opening lead:

```
♠ K
```

Partner infers that the King is supported by the Queen and normally does **not** expect the Ace.

---

# Example 2 – Queen Lead

Holding

```
♥ Q J 10 5
```

Opening lead:

```
♥ Q
```

Partner knows the Queen is from a strong sequence and can usually unblock an honor if appropriate.

---

# Example 3 – Ten Lead

Holding

```
♦ A Q 10 7
```

Opening lead:

```
♦ 10
```

The Ten indicates an **interior sequence**, helping partner place the missing honors.

---

# Against Notrump

Journalist Leads are most effective against **Notrump contracts**, where:

- Establishing long suits is important.
- Honor location matters.
- Accurate continuation decisions are critical.

Typical priorities:

- Lead from strong sequences.
- Show interior sequences clearly.
- Preserve communication with partner.

---

# Against Suit Contracts

Some partnerships simplify Journalist Leads against suit contracts by using:

- Standard honor leads.
- Journalist leads only from clear sequences.
- Ace for attitude or count.
- King from unsupported sequences.

Discuss these treatments before play.

---

# Partner's Responsibilities

After an honor lead, partner should consider:

- Which honor combination has been shown?
- Is an unblock necessary?
- Should the suit be continued?
- What does dummy suggest?
- Does declarer have the missing honor?

---

# Advantages

- More descriptive than standard honor leads.
- Helps partner locate missing honors.
- Clarifies interior sequences.
- Improves defensive accuracy.
- Particularly useful against Notrump.

---

# Disadvantages

- Several competing versions exist.
- Requires detailed partnership agreement.
- Easy to misinterpret without discussion.
- Less widely played than Standard or Rusinow Leads.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Assuming all partnerships play the same version.
- Forgetting the agreed meanings.
- Leading an honor outside the agreed structure.
- Misreading partner's honor lead.
- Mixing Journalist and Standard Leads.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Before using Journalist Leads, agree on:

- Which honor combinations each lead promises.
- Ace lead meaning (AK, AKx, attitude, or count).
- King lead meaning.
- Interior sequence leads.
- Differences between Notrump and suit contracts.
- Follow-up signaling after the opening lead.

# Defender's Checklist

Before making a Journalist Lead:

- Am I leading from an agreed honor combination?
- Does this lead accurately describe my holding?
- Have my partner and I agreed on this treatment?
- Is this the best suit to attack?
- Will partner understand the message?

---

# Memory Guide

## Five Steps

1. Select the correct suit.
2. Identify your honor combination.
3. Lead the agreed honor.
4. Watch partner's signal.
5. Continue according to the combined information.

---

# Comparison

| Method | Main Idea |
|---------|-----------|
| Standard Leads | Traditional honor and fourth-best leads |
| Journalist Leads | Honor lead describes the honor structure |
| Rusinow Leads | Second-highest honor from a sequence |
| Third/Fifth Leads | Spot-card leads show length |

# Typical Defensive Process

```
Choose the Opening Suit
↓

Identify the Honor Holding

↓

Lead the Agreed Honor

↓

Partner Interprets the Holding

↓

Coordinate the Defense
```

---

# Memory Aid

**Journalist Leads = "The Honor You Lead Describes the Honors You Hold."**

Remember:

- **Journalist Leads are not universal—agree on the exact version with partner.**
- **Honor leads are designed to describe the underlying honor combination.**
- **They are most effective against Notrump contracts.**
- **Interior sequences are a key feature of the system.**
- **Clear partnership agreements are essential for success.**

Journalist Leads are a sophisticated opening lead system that provides more information than standard honor leads. By accurately describing honor combinations and interior sequences, they enable defenders to coordinate more effectively and make better continuation decisions. Because multiple variants exist, thorough partnership discussion is essential before adopting the method.
