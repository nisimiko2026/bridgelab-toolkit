# Honor Leads

## Overview

Honor leads are among the most important opening lead agreements in contract
bridge.

Partnerships use honor leads to communicate information about

- suit length,
- touching honors,
- interior sequences,
- supported honors,
- requests for attitude,
- requests for count,
- unblock requests.

Because different partnerships employ different lead systems, understanding the
meaning of an honor lead requires knowing the partnership's agreements.

This section covers the most common honor lead methods used throughout the
bridge world.

---

# Objectives

After studying this section, the reader should be able to

- choose the correct honor lead,
- recognize standard honor sequences,
- understand partnership agreements,
- interpret partner's responses,
- avoid common honor-lead mistakes,
- compare different honor-lead systems.

# Design Principles

## Partnership Agreements Come First

The meaning of an honor lead depends entirely on the agreed lead system.

---

## Lead the Correct Honor

Different honor combinations require different opening leads.

Consistency is essential.

---

## Communication

Honor leads should provide useful information without unnecessarily helping
declarer.

---

## Match the Contract

The best honor lead may differ significantly between suit and notrump
contracts.
