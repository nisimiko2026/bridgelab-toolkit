# Finesses

## Overview

The **Finesses** section covers one of the most important and frequently used
techniques in contract bridge.

A finesse attempts to win a trick with a card that is not the highest remaining
card in the suit by taking advantage of the presumed location of missing honors.

Although often viewed as a single technique, bridge contains numerous forms of
finesses, each suited to different card combinations and tactical situations.

Expert declarers combine finesse techniques with

- counting,
- inference,
- timing,
- communication,
- safety play,
- elimination,
- squeeze play.

# Classification

| Technique | Primary Purpose |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Standard Finesse | Win against one missing honor |
| Two-Way Finesse | Choose the correct opponent |
| Double Finesse | Capture two honors |
| Repeat Finesse | Reuse the same finesse position |
| Delayed Finesse | Gain information before finessing |
| Ruffing Finesse | Combine finesse and trump play |
| Marked Finesse | Use known honor location |
| Backward Finesse | Reverse normal finesse direction |
| Intra-Finesse | Advanced combination play |
| Deep Finesse | Capture an intermediate honor |
