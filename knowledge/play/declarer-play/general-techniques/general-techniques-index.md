# General Techniques

General techniques are the fundamental card-play skills used by declarer in
both **suit contracts** and **notrump contracts**. Unlike specialized methods
such as squeezes, endplays, or trump coups, these techniques appear
continuously during ordinary play and form the foundation of expert declarer
play.

They focus on

- communication,
- establishing tricks,
- preserving entries,
- deception,
- information gathering,
- efficient card management.

Mastery of these techniques significantly improves declarer's ability to
execute successful contracts.

# Summary

The **General Techniques** section forms the foundation of expert declarer play.
These techniques are applicable in virtually every contract and provide the
essential skills required for planning, communication, trick development,
counting, visualization, and deception. Mastering these core concepts enables
declarers to execute more advanced techniques such as finesses, squeezes,
endplays, and coups with confidence and precision.
