# Unblocking

## Overview

**Unblocking** is the technique of playing a high card **earlier than would normally be necessary** to prevent a suit from becoming blocked. The objective is to maintain communication between declarer and dummy so that all established winners can be enjoyed.

Unblocking is a fundamental element of expert card play. It occurs in both **notrump** and **suit contracts**, although it is particularly important in notrump play, where communication cannot be restored by ruffing.

---

# Objectives

Unblocking allows declarer to:

- Preserve communication between the hands.
- Prevent long suits from becoming blocked.
- Reach established winners.
- Maintain the planned order of play.
- Avoid stranding high cards.

---

# What Is a Blocked Suit?

A suit is **blocked** when high cards in one hand prevent access to smaller winners in the opposite hand.

Example:

```
Dummy
♦ A J 10 9

Declarer
♦ K Q
```

If declarer cashes the ♦A first and later the ♦K and ♦Q, the ♦J and ♦10 may become stranded because communication has been lost.

Proper unblocking avoids this problem.

---

# The Basic Principle

When a high card serves **no future purpose**, it may be correct to play it early.

Doing so clears the suit and preserves communication.

The play may appear unnecessary at the time but is essential for enjoying all the winners later.

---

# Why Unblocking Matters

Many contracts fail because declarer:

- Establishes a long suit.
- Preserves entries.
- But forgets to unblock an honor.

The result is that one or more winners cannot be reached.

Good declarers plan the order of honors before playing the first trick.

---

# Planning the Suit

Before cashing honors, ask:

- Which hand contains the long suit?
- Which hand contains the entries?
- Could this suit become blocked?
- Should one honor be played earlier than normal?

Planning avoids later communication problems.

---

# Example 1 – Simple Unblock

Dummy:

```
♣ A J 10 9
```

Declarer:

```
♣ K Q
```

Correct play:

- Cash the ♣K.
- Cash the ♣Q.

Dummy's clubs are now clear.

If the honors are played in the wrong order, communication may be lost.

---

# Example 2 – Establishing a Long Suit

Contract:

```
3NT
```

Dummy:

```
♥ A Q 10 9 8
```

Declarer:

```
♥ K J
```

Declarer first drives out the defenders' remaining honor.

Later, one of the touching honors is unblocked so the established heart suit can be run smoothly.

---

# Example 3 – Unblocking an Entry

Dummy:

```
♠ A 5
```

Declarer:

```
♠ K Q
```

If declarer unnecessarily preserves both honors, the suit may become blocked.

Playing one honor at the correct time keeps communication open.

---

# Unblocking in Notrump

Unblocking is especially important in notrump because:

- Long suits provide extra tricks.
- Entries are limited.
- Ruffing cannot restore communication.
- A blocked suit often means a failed contract.

Many classic notrump endings depend on timely unblocking.

---

# Unblocking in Suit Contracts

Although less frequent, unblocking is also important in suit contracts.

Examples include:

- Preserving a trump entry.
- Preventing blockage in a side suit.
- Preparing for a squeeze.
- Maintaining communication before drawing trumps.

---

# Unblocking and Entries

Unblocking and entry management are closely related.

Sometimes declarer must:

- Preserve an entry.
- Unblock an honor.
- Delay cashing another winner.

The correct sequence ensures that every established trick can be reached.

---

# Unblocking and Timing

Good timing is essential.

Unblock:

- Too early, and an important honor may be wasted.
- Too late, and the suit may become permanently blocked.

The correct moment depends on the overall declarer plan.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Cashing honors in the wrong order.
- Ignoring communication between the hands.
- Preserving a high card that serves no useful purpose.
- Establishing a suit without planning how to enjoy it.
- Forgetting to identify potential blocked suits before trick one.

# Key Principles

- Unblocking prevents a suit from becoming blocked.
- Plan the order of honors before playing the suit.
- Communication is often more important than preserving an unnecessary honor.
- Unblocking is particularly important in notrump contracts.
- A well-timed unblock allows declarer to enjoy every established winner.
