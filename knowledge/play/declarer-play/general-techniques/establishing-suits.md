# Establishing Suits

## Overview

One of the most important techniques in declarer play is **establishing a long
suit**. Declarer deliberately loses one or more tricks to drive out the
defenders' higher cards, allowing the remaining small cards to become winners.

Establishing long suits is especially important in **notrump contracts**, where
ruffing is unavailable, but it is also valuable in suit contracts whenever
additional tricks are required.

Expert declarers recognize that losing a trick early is often the correct price
for creating several winners later.

---

# Objectives

Establishing suits allows declarer to

- create additional winners,
- promote small cards,
- develop long suits,
- force out opponents' honors,
- maximize trick-taking potential.

---

# General Principles

Before attempting to establish a suit, declarer should determine

- how many losers must be conceded,
- whether sufficient entries exist,
- whether defenders can cash their winners,
- whether another suit offers a better source of tricks.

Planning is essential.

---

# Long Suit Establishment

The most common method.

Example

```
Dummy
♣ AQ765

Declarer
♣ K43
```

Opponents hold

```
♣ J10982
```

Declarer loses one club trick.

The remaining clubs become winners.

---

# Force-Out Technique

Declarer deliberately loses to a defender's honor.

Example

```
Dummy
♦ AQ876

Declarer
♦ K54
```

Losing once to the defenders' remaining honor establishes the rest of the suit.

This is called **forcing out an honor**.

---

# Promoting Small Cards

Small cards become winners after the defenders' higher cards disappear.

Example

```
Dummy
♥ 8765

Declarer
♥ AKQ
```

Once the opponents' remaining honors are exhausted, the small hearts become
valuable tricks.

---

# Establishing Suits in Notrump

Suit establishment is one of the primary sources of tricks in notrump.

Typical sources include

- long minors,
- long majors,
- combined suit length.

Declarer frequently sacrifices an early trick to establish multiple winners.

---

# Establishing Suits in Trump Contracts

Suit contracts provide additional flexibility.

Declarer may

- ruff losers,
- establish side suits,
- preserve trump control,
- delay drawing trumps until the suit is established.

Timing is often critical.

---

# Entries

Entries are essential.

Example

```
Dummy
♣ AQ987

Declarer
♣ K2
```

After establishing the clubs,

declarer must still be able to reach dummy to cash them.

Without entries,

established winners may remain inaccessible.

---

# Timing

Declarer should establish long suits before defenders gain control of the hand.

Sometimes

- winners should be cashed first,
- trumps should be drawn first,
- or communication should be preserved.

Correct timing determines success.

---

# Choosing the Right Suit

When multiple suits can be developed,

consider

- number of potential winners,
- number of losers required,
- available entries,
- likelihood of bad breaks,
- defensive threats.

Often only one suit should be developed.

---

# Combining Techniques

Suit establishment frequently works together with

- finesses,
- hold-up play,
- entries,
- elimination,
- squeezes,
- endplays.

Declarer should integrate all available techniques.

---

# Communication

Before establishing a suit, ask

- Can I reach the winners?
- Are enough entries available?
- Will communication survive?

Many contracts fail because the winners cannot be reached.

---

# Counting the Suit

Count

- combined length,
- missing cards,
- expected distribution,
- likely number of losers.

Example

Holding eight cards,

expect a **3–2** break most of the time.

Unexpected breaks may require a different plan.

---

# Typical Suit Breaks

| Missing Cards | Most Likely Break |
|--------------:|------------------|
| 4 | 2–2 |
| 5 | 3–2 |
| 6 | 4–2 |
| 7 | 4–3 |

Probability should influence declarer's plan.

---

# Partnership Agreements

Although establishing suits is primarily a declarer-play technique,

bidding often provides valuable information about

- suit lengths,
- honor locations,
- likely distributions.

Use auction information whenever possible.

---

# Modern Expert Philosophy

Expert declarers

- identify their source of tricks immediately,
- determine how many losers must be conceded,
- preserve entries,
- establish the most profitable suit first.

Every trick should contribute toward the overall plan.

---

# Common Mistakes

## Choosing the Wrong Suit

Not every long suit should be developed.

---

## Forgetting Entries

Established winners are useless if they cannot be reached.

---

## Waiting Too Long

Delaying establishment may allow defenders to cash their winners first.

---

## Ignoring Timing

Sometimes trumps should be drawn first.

Sometimes they should not.

---

## Miscounting the Suit

Always count

- combined length,
- missing cards,
- expected breaks.

---

# Expert Evaluation

Experts evaluate

- potential winners,
- required losers,
- entries,
- communication,
- timing,
- probabilities,

before attempting to establish a suit.

---

# Universal Principles

Successful suit establishment requires declarer to

- identify the best source of tricks,
- preserve communication,
- force out honors efficiently,
- integrate the technique into the overall plan.

# Typical Examples

## Force Out an Honor

```
Dummy
♦ AQ765

Declarer
♦ K43
```

Lose one diamond trick.

The remaining diamonds become winners.

---

## Long Minor in Notrump

```
Dummy
♣ AQ8765

Declarer
♣ K4
```

Drive out the remaining honor.

Cash the established clubs.

---

## Preserve Entries

```
Establish the suit first.

Keep an entry to cash it later.
```

---

# Summary

Establishing suits is one of the principal methods of creating additional tricks
in declarer play. By **forcing out defenders' honors**, declarer promotes small
cards into winners and transforms long suits into powerful sources of tricks.

Successful suit establishment depends on careful **planning**, **timing**,
**entries**, **communication**, and **probability assessment**. Expert
declarers identify their best source of extra tricks before the opening lead and
integrate suit establishment into the overall strategy for the hand, maximizing
their chances of bringing the contract home.
