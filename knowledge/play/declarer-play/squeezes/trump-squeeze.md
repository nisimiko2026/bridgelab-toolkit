# Trump Squeeze

## Overview

A **Trump Squeeze** is a squeeze in a suit contract where the **trump suit plays an essential role** in creating or enjoying the squeeze. Unlike a simple squeeze in notrump, declarer may use a trump as a threat, an entry, or the squeeze card itself.

Trump squeezes are among the most elegant and difficult declarer techniques. They combine the principles of squeeze play with careful trump management, timing, and entry preservation.

---

# Objectives

A trump squeeze aims to:

- Establish an additional winner.
- Force a defender to abandon a critical guard.
- Exploit the flexibility provided by the trump suit.
- Create an extra trick without relying on a finesse.

---

# Requirements

Most trump squeezes require:

- A squeeze card.
- One or more threat cards.
- A remaining trump in at least one hand.
- Proper communication.
- Accurate counting.
- Rectification of the count.

Because trumps provide additional flexibility, some trump squeezes are possible even when an equivalent notrump squeeze is not.

---

# The Basic Idea

In a suit contract:

- Declarer retains one or more trumps.
- A defender must guard multiple suits.
- When declarer plays the squeeze card, the defender is forced to discard.
- Declarer uses the remaining trump(s) and established threats to score the remaining tricks.

The trump suit often acts as an additional entry or threat.

---

# Why Trumps Matter

The trump suit may serve several purposes:

- Preserve communication between declarer and dummy.
- Create an additional threat.
- Provide an extra entry.
- Allow declarer to ruff after the squeeze.
- Increase the number of possible squeeze positions.

This flexibility distinguishes trump squeezes from ordinary notrump squeezes.

---

# Planning the Trump Squeeze

Declarer should identify:

- The squeeze card.
- The threat suits.
- Remaining trumps.
- Critical entries.
- The defender under pressure.
- Whether the count has been rectified.

The squeeze should be planned well before the ending.

---

# Rectifying the Count

As with most squeezes:

Declarer must first lose every trick that can safely be lost.

Only then can the squeeze force the defender to make a meaningful discard.

---

# Preserving Trumps

One of the distinctive features of a trump squeeze is that declarer often **deliberately preserves a trump** until the ending.

Drawing all the trumps too early may destroy:

- An essential entry.
- A threat card.
- The squeeze itself.

Trump management is therefore an integral part of the technique.

---

# Communication

Entries are especially important.

Declarer must be able to:

- Reach the squeeze card.
- Reach the established winner after the discard.
- Use the remaining trump if necessary.

Poor communication frequently causes the squeeze to fail.

---

# Conceptual Example

Near the end of the hand:

```
Dummy
♥ A
♣ K
♠ 5

Declarer
♠ A
♦ A
♣ A
```

West must protect both:

- Hearts
- Clubs

Declarer cashes the ♦A.

West must discard.

If West releases:

- A heart, dummy's ♥A becomes the extra winner.
- A club, dummy's ♣K is established.

The remaining trump provides the necessary communication to enjoy the winner.

---

# Trump Squeeze vs. Simple Squeeze

| Simple Squeeze | Trump Squeeze |
|----------------|---------------|
| Usually played in notrump | Occurs in a suit contract |
| No trumps remain | One or more trumps remain |
| Communication through high cards | Communication may use trumps |
| Threats in side suits | Threats may include the trump suit |

---

# Common Situations

Trump squeezes commonly arise when:

- Declarer preserves one small trump.
- Dummy contains a long threat suit.
- One defender guards two suits.
- Entries are limited.
- A simple squeeze is impossible without the remaining trump.

---

# Common Mistakes

- Drawing all the trumps too early.
- Failing to rectify the count.
- Destroying a vital entry.
- Cashing winners in the wrong order.
- Misidentifying the defender under pressure.
- Overlooking the role of the remaining trump.

# Key Principles

- A trump squeeze is a squeeze in which the trump suit plays an essential role.
- Preserve the necessary trump until the squeeze has been executed.
- Accurate timing and entry management are critical.
- Rectify the count before applying the squeeze.
- Trump squeezes illustrate the close relationship between trump management and advanced squeeze play.
