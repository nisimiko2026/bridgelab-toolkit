# Double Progressive Squeeze

## Overview

A **Double Progressive Squeeze** is one of the most complex forms of squeeze play. It consists of **two successive progressive squeezes**, in which the first squeeze creates a second squeeze position, and that second squeeze in turn creates yet another squeeze, eventually forcing the defenders to surrender an extra trick.

Unlike a **Double Squeeze**, where two defenders are placed under simultaneous pressure, a Double Progressive Squeeze develops **step by step**, with each squeeze preparing the next.

This is a highly specialized ending that rarely occurs in practice but illustrates the power of sustained pressure and precise planning.

---

# Objectives

A double progressive squeeze aims to:

- Apply successive squeeze pressure.
- Force multiple defensive concessions.
- Establish an additional winner through a chain of squeezes.
- Exploit communication and timing to their fullest.

---

# Basic Principle

The sequence is:

1. The **first squeeze** does not immediately establish a winner.
2. It creates a **second squeeze position**.
3. The **second squeeze** again changes the position.
4. A **third squeeze effect** finally establishes the winning trick.

Each squeeze advances the position toward the next.

---

# Comparison with Other Squeezes

| Technique | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Simple Squeeze | One squeeze produces one winner. |
| Progressive Squeeze | One squeeze creates a second squeeze. |
| Double Progressive Squeeze | Two progressive squeezes occur in succession before the final winner is established. |

---

# Requirements

A successful double progressive squeeze normally requires:

- Multiple squeeze cards.
- Several threat cards.
- Accurate communication.
- Careful entry management.
- Precise timing.
- Rectification of the count.
- Accurate counting of the defenders' distributions.

Because the ending develops in several stages, every entry must be preserved.

---

# How It Develops

The play typically follows this pattern:

### Stage 1

Declarer cashes the first squeeze card.

A defender is forced to weaken a guard.

No immediate winner is established.

---

### Stage 2

The weakened position creates a new squeeze.

Declarer reaches the second squeeze card.

Another defender must discard.

Again, the position changes.

---

### Stage 3

The cumulative effect of both squeezes finally establishes the extra trick.

Only after both progressive squeezes have occurred does declarer enjoy the winning card.

---

# Planning the Play

Before attempting a double progressive squeeze, declarer should identify:

- Every squeeze card.
- Every threat card.
- The order of the squeezes.
- The defender(s) under pressure at each stage.
- Every remaining entry.

The entire ending must be visualized before the first squeeze card is played.

---

# Communication

Communication is often the deciding factor.

Declarer must preserve enough entries to:

- Reach the first squeeze card.
- Reach the second squeeze card.
- Cash the established winner.

A single wasted entry usually destroys the ending.

---

# Rectifying the Count

As with nearly all squeeze endings:

Declarer must first lose every unavoidable loser.

If the count has not been rectified, the defenders may simply win a trick before the squeeze sequence is complete.

---

# Conceptual Example

Near the end of the hand:

- West guards hearts and clubs.
- East guards clubs and diamonds.

Declarer cashes the first squeeze card.

West weakens a guard.

The new position allows declarer to reach another winner, which becomes the second squeeze card.

East is now squeezed.

The cumulative effect of both squeezes finally establishes declarer's remaining threat.

---

# Recognizing the Position

A double progressive squeeze may exist when:

- One squeeze does not immediately produce a winner.
- A second squeeze becomes available after the first.
- Multiple entries remain.
- Both defenders gradually lose control of their guards.
- Declarer can maintain communication throughout the ending.

These positions are extremely difficult to recognize at the table.

---

# Double Progressive Squeeze vs. Double Squeeze

| Double Progressive Squeeze | Double Squeeze |
|----------------------------|----------------|
| Squeezes occur sequentially | Two defenders are squeezed in the same ending |
| Pressure develops gradually | Pressure exists simultaneously |
| One squeeze creates another | Both squeeze positions already exist |

---

# Double Progressive Squeeze vs. Progressive Squeeze

| Progressive Squeeze | Double Progressive Squeeze |
|---------------------|----------------------------|
| One progressive sequence | Two successive progressive sequences |
| Two squeeze stages | Three or more squeeze stages |
| Rare | Extremely rare |

---

# Common Mistakes

- Expecting the first squeeze to establish a winner.
- Destroying an entry needed for a later squeeze.
- Failing to rectify the count.
- Cashing winners in the wrong order.
- Losing track of the evolving defensive guards.

# Key Principles

- A double progressive squeeze is a sequence of progressive squeezes rather than a single squeeze position.
- Each squeeze prepares the next until the final winner is established.
- Accurate visualization, timing, and communication are essential.
- Every entry must be preserved throughout the sequence.
- Although extremely rare, the double progressive squeeze represents one of the highest levels of declarer technique and squeeze theory.
