# Squeezes

## Overview

A **squeeze** is one of the most elegant and powerful techniques in contract
bridge.

A squeeze occurs when a defender is forced to discard a card that protects one
threat while simultaneously guarding another, allowing declarer to gain an
additional trick.

Successful squeeze play depends upon

- planning,
- counting,
- communication,
- timing,
- rectifying the count,
- preserving entries,
- recognizing threat cards.

Although squeezes are often viewed as advanced techniques, many occur naturally
once declarer recognizes the required position.
