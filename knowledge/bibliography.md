# Bibliography

## Overview

BridgeLab is based upon the accumulated knowledge of the international bridge
community, including official laws, world championship literature, classic
texts, expert publications, and modern instructional works.

The bibliography includes foundational references for bidding systems,
conventions, declarer play, defensive technique, duplicate bridge, partnership
methods, and tournament strategy.

The inclusion of a work does not necessarily imply that every treatment or
partnership agreement described in BridgeLab follows that source exactly.
Where multiple expert methods exist, BridgeLab identifies the most widely
accepted approach and discusses important variations.

# Editorial Principles

BridgeLab follows several principles when presenting technical material.

- Preference is given to official governing bodies for laws and regulations.
- Widely accepted expert methods are presented before partnership-specific
  variations.
- Where no universal standard exists, multiple treatments are identified and
  distinguished.
- Historical conventions are clearly separated from current tournament practice.
- Partnership agreements are explicitly identified where required.

---

# Citation Policy

BridgeLab is an educational encyclopedia rather than a research journal.
Consequently, individual articles generally do not include formal academic
citations. Instead, this bibliography documents the principal references that
have influenced the encyclopedia as a whole.

Readers seeking detailed historical development or alternative partnership
methods are encouraged to consult the original publications listed above.
